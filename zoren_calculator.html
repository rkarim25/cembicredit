1. Creditor-on-Creditor Violence vs Bank Syndicate Cooperation: The Game Theory

While Zorlu's fundamental asset coverage is 171.8% ($2,050M operational EV + $240M cash vs $380M OpCo loans and $1,100M Eurobond claims), offshore Eurobond holders ($1.1B ZOREN 11.00% 2030) are structurally subordinated to operating company bank facilities (ADB and Turkish commercial banks). Rather than abstract percentage haircuts, the desk quantifies the real conflict between banks and bondholders in cents per dollar of claim (+upside or -discount):

  • Bank Syndicate Cooperation vs DSRA Cash Trap (-4.5c to +2.0c): Senior secured project finance loans have 6-month Debt Service Reserve Accounts (DSRA) and minimum DSCR covenants (1.15x–1.20x). If banks turn hostile, they can trap all plant cash flows inside OpCo, starving HoldCo of coupon/redemption liquidity (-4.5c discount). If banks cooperate on a consensual A&E, cash flows upstream freely (+2.0c upside). Base Case reflects -1.5c friction.
  • Structural Priming & Creditor-on-Creditor Uptier Risk (-3.5c to +1.0c): Domestic Turkish banks holding bilateral working capital loans could demand first-lien pledges over unencumbered Zorlu Yenilenebilir (renewable subsidiary) or ZES (EV charging) shares as a condition of rolling bank lines, structurally priming HoldCo Eurobonds (-3.5c discount). Supranational DFI oversight (EBRD/IFC) prevents aggressive priming (+1.0c). Base Case reflects -0.5c.
  • Turkish Sovereign Macro & Lira FX Transmission (-4.0c to +2.0c): OEDAŞ grid tariffs and retail revenues are TRY-denominated and EMRA-regulated. Rapid Lira depreciation creates cash conversion lags and high CBRT interest rates compress HoldCo cash (-4.0c discount). Macro stabilization and disinflation yield +2.0c upside. Base Case reflects -1.0c.
  • Zorlu Yenilenebilir IPO & Strategic Monetization (-2.5c to +3.5c): Successful IPO of the renewable arm on Borsa Istanbul or a minority stake sale to a Gulf sovereign wealth fund (e.g., TAQA, Masdar) would inject $250M+ cash directly to retire 2026 Eurobonds (+3.5c upside). An aborted deal forces high-cost bank refinancing (-2.5c discount). Base Case reflects -0.5c.
2. Why Banks Cooperate: Four Structural Pillars Preventing Aggressive Foreclosure
1. Supranational DFI Umbrella (EBRD & IFC): EBRD and IFC anchor Kızıldere geothermal loans. As multilateral development institutions, their strict charters prevent predatory creditor violence, enforcing consensual, value-maximizing workout standards.
2. Regulatory & Grid Mandate (EMRA): OEDAŞ supplies 1.95 million customers across 5 Turkish provinces. Turkish energy authorities will not tolerate an aggressive bank enforcement or asset freeze that imperils national grid operations.
3. Zorlu Yenilenebilir IPO Equity Cushion: Banks recognize that their ultimate recovery cushion is the equity value of the renewable arm ($1.2B+). Creditor-on-creditor warfare would destroy the upcoming IPO, destroying bank collateral value.
4. Turkish Bank Balance Sheet Shield: Domestic banks (Garanti, Akbank, İşbank) hold billions in Zorlu conglomerate loans. Forcing a default on ZOREN 2026 Eurobonds triggers Stage 2/3 IFRS 9 NPL provisioning, wiping out bank capital.
