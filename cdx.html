How the levels are used

  • Regime read: CDX HY and Crossover against their 52-week percentiles say whether risk appetite is rich or cheap; CDX EM against EMBI GD says whether the derivative is leading or lagging cash.
  • Hedging: CDX EM is the liquid hedge for EMBI beta; single-name sovereign CDS (Turkey, South Africa, Egypt, Saudi Arabia) for CEEMEA concentration. Basis to cash bonds is recorded on the sovereign page when captured.
  • Method notes: relative value, macro regimes; official macro data on the sovereign dashboard and the macro monitor; the UST desk carries the Treasury curve from Yahoo Finance, which is real data.
