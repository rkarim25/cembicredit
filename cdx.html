Institutional Credit Stance

Neutral / Selective Carry with Asymmetric Out-of-the-Money Protection

Across global credit derivatives, spreads trade in the tight 20th–35th percentiles of historical 1-year ranges: US HY CDX at ~322 bps, iTraxx Europe Crossover at ~296 bps, and CDX EM at ~174 bps. While corporate fundamentals (interest coverage >4x, low default rates ~2.5%) support clipping index carry, compressed risk premiums leave little cushion against a macro growth shock or 10-Year Treasury breakout above 5.05%. The optimal trade expression is Overweight CDX.EM carry (backed by sovereign reserve buffers) while maintaining an asymmetric hedge on US HY via out-of-the-money 375 bps payer swaptions to protect against refinancing wall indigestion.
