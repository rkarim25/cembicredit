## View
Neutral on the benchmark AKBNK 6.45% 2029; the rating cannot be higher than the Turkish sovereign's. Franchise, profitability and capital are adequate for BB-/Ba3. H1-26 net income was TL 34.3bn and CAR was 16.4%.
The stored issuer record is unreliable for a bank: NPL is flat at 1.5% and CAR flat at 19.4% for 2021–24, and the FCF bridge is copied from a corporate template. Use the 2026 figures below instead.
Asset quality has deteriorated from the stored figure. NPL is about 3.8% against 3.7% at end-2025, Stage 2+3 is about 11.5%, and management guides to cost of risk of 200bp.
NIM including swap costs fell to 3.0% in Q2 from 3.3% in Q1. Easing (policy rate 37%, about 100bp of cuts expected per meeting) should relieve funding cost but also cuts asset yields.
The main risk is unsecured retail and SME credit drift combined with an FX or political shock that inflates RWA on a CET1 of 11.3%.
Confidence: Medium. Ratings and results rest on tier 2 releases relayed by tier 3 press, and the bond level is stale.

## Analysis
### Ratings and sovereign link
- Fitch: long-term IDR BB- with Stable outlook, reaffirmed 11-Jun-26. The outlook was moved from Positive to Stable in April 2026 after the sovereign outlook change (CNBC-e, 11-Jun-26, https://www.cnbce.com/borsa/fitch-ratingsden-akbank-aciklamasi-h31446; Paratic, https://paratic.com/fitch-akbank-icin-gorunumu-pozitife-cevirdi/) (Medium).
- Moody's: Ba3/Stable on long-term FX and local-currency deposits and on unsecured debt, affirmed mid-July 2026 (Forbes TR reports 16-Jul-26 and a search summary gives 17-Jul-26; https://www.forbes.com.tr/ekonomi/akbank-in-moody-s-notlarinda-degisiklik-olmadi) (Medium).
- S&P: not verified. The repo's "B1" is not supported by the Moody's reports found (open items 3 and 4).
- All ratings track the sovereign (Fitch BB-/Stable, Moody's Ba3/Stable). A sovereign action would move the bank's rating.

### H1-26 results
- Net income TL 34.333bn, loans TL 2.913tn, deposits TL 2.511tn, assets TL 4.012tn, CAR 16.4% (Akbank release, 28-Jul-26, https://www.akbank.com/en/news/akbank-supports-the-turkish-economy-through-loans-of-tl-2-trillion-913-billion-in-the-first-half-of-2026) (High). Loans to deposits is about 116% in TL terms from these figures; the ratio mixes TL and FX and is not comparable with the stored 86%.
- Q2 net income was TL 15.2bn (BloombergHT, 29-Jul-26, https://www.bloomberght.com/akbank-tan-ikinci-ceyrekte-15-2-milyar-tl-net-kr-3784014) (Medium). Net income growth of about 38% year on year is from secondary summaries (Medium).
- NIM including swap costs was 3.0% in Q2, down 30bp from 3.3% in Q1 (Quartr summary, https://quartr.com/events/akbank-t-a-s-akbnk-q2-2026_F9FloJgJ) (Medium).

### Asset quality
- NPL ratio is about 3.8%, up from 3.7% at end-2025, and Stage 2+3 is about 11.5% of loans. Guidance is NPL at or below 3.5% and cost of risk of about 200bp (Quartr; İş Yatırım, 29-Jul-26, https://arastirma.isyatirim.com.tr/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/AKBNK_2C26_Kar-Analizi_290726.pdf) (Medium). The figures came from search summaries, not the filing.
- NPL guidance of 3.5% or lower sits below the reported 3.8%, so the guidance implies improvement that has not yet appeared. Coverage was not found.

### Capital
- CET1 is 11.3% (Medium) and CAR is 16.4% (High). The gap implies a large share of Tier 1 and Tier 2 instruments, so the AT1 and Tier 2 call decisions matter for the ratio. Tier 1 of about 13.3% is quoted by one secondary source and not confirmed.
- A lira move inflates RWA on FX assets. No FX-shock sensitivity was found (open item 8).

### Macro backdrop
- The central bank held its policy rate at 37% on 10-Sep-26, the fifth consecutive hold. August CPI was 31.51%. Cuts of about 100bp are expected at each of the 22-Oct and 10-Dec meetings (Intellinews, https://new.intellinews.com/articles/turkish-central-bank-sticks-to-37-policy-rate-for-fifth-straight-time-467058; Hürriyet Daily News, https://www.hurriyetdailynews.com/eyes-on-rate-decision-following-august-inflation-data-226436) (Medium). The CBRT and TurkStat releases were not checked.

### Funding and market
- A subordinated-debt programme of $4bn is noted in a KAP filing (https://kap.org.tr/en/api/BildirimPdf/1556536) (Low; date not confirmed). No 2026 Eurobond or syndication was confirmed.
- Stored bond level: AKBNK 6.45% 2029 at price 100.2, yield 6.45% and spread 185bp as of 19-Sep-26 (repo record, Medium, stale).
- Searches that returned nothing relevant: S&P action on Akbank, 2026 syndication roll rate, Bloomberg and FT articles, and the IMF Article IV.
