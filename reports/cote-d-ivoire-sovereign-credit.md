## View
- Stable to modestly positive credit. Growth of 6.0% in 2026E, debt of 55.1% of GDP, a current account deficit of 1.1% of GDP and the euro peg support hard-currency debt service (IMF, Jun-26; IMF WEO; Medium).
- The IMF completed the final EFF/ECF and RSF reviews in Jun-26 and rated debt distress risk 'low' (IMF, Jun-26, Medium).
- Ratings are BB/BB/Ba2, all Stable (S&P 14-Nov-25, Fitch 12-Jun-26, Moody's Mar-26; Medium).
- The biggest risk is the loss of the programme anchor. The 2026 deficit of 3.8% sits above the 3.0% WAEMU ceiling, and consolidation to 2028 now rests on the 2027 Finance Law (15-Dec-26) without Fund conditionality.
- Secondary risks: the 7.3% fall in commodity terms of trade in the 12 months to May-26 (cocoa is the swing factor), tight WAEMU bank liquidity, and Sahel spillover.
- Monetary conditions are benign: policy rate 3.0%, CPI 1.2% y/y in Aug-26, ex-ante real rate 1.8% on trailing CPI.
- No market levels were supplied and no independent 90-day news search was completed this run. Relative value cannot be assessed.
- Confidence: Medium on fundamentals, because the figures are tier 2 and four months old, and Low on valuation.

## Analysis

### Ratings and market access
- S&P BB/Stable (14-Nov-25), Fitch BB/Stable (affirmed 12-Jun-26, upgraded from BB- on 12-Dec-25), Moody's Ba2/Stable (Mar-26) (knowledge base citing Rating Agencies, Jun-26, Medium).
- Eurobonds: €1.1B 4.30% 2040, 15-year amortising, issued Feb-26, with a 5.39% hedged yield per the previous version; US$1.75B issued Mar-25; US$2.6B in two tranches (2033/2037) issued Jan-24 (Ministry of Finance, Feb-26, Medium).
- Amortising structures reduce refinancing concentration. The schedule, ISINs, outstanding amounts and CAC type are not in the brief (open item 5).
- No spread, yield or index-weight data was supplied, so relative value versus BB Africa and EMBI GD is not assessed.

### Programme status and debt sustainability
- In Jun-26 the IMF Board completed the sixth and final reviews of the US$3.5B EFF/ECF and the fifth review of the US$1.3B RSF. It reclassified debt distress risk to 'low' (IMF, Jun-26, Medium).
- The previous version states this is the only SSA sovereign with a 'low' rating and that about US$832.8M was disbursed. Neither is re-verified this run, so treat them as Low until checked.
- Government debt peaked at 56.3% of GDP in 2025 and is projected at 55.1% in 2026, below the WAEMU 70% limit (IMF / Ministry of Finance, Jun-26, Medium). These are projections.

### Fiscal
- The 2026 deficit is budgeted at 3.8% of GDP, with convergence to 3.0% by 2028. The previous version states 2025 was at 3.0% (IMF / Ministry of Finance, Jun-26, Medium).
- Without a programme, the 2027 Finance Law due 15-Dec-26 is the first test of the path. A draft deficit at or below 3.5% would be positive; above 4% would be negative (analyst judgement).
- The previous version cites tax-to-GDP of about 13.5% (IMF, Jun-26, Low, not re-verified). Interest-to-revenue and gross financing need are not available (open item 10).

### External and monetary
- The current account is -1.1% of GDP in 2026E (IMF WEO, Medium). Net FDI was 2.03% of GDP in 2025 (World Bank WDI, Medium). Nominal GDP is US$112.1B in 2026E (IMF WEO, Medium).
- The REER z-score is 0.6 (Aug-26, IMF CPI-based, 10-year basis): no material misalignment. Commodity terms of trade fell 7.3% from May-25 to May-26 (IMF CTOT, Medium).
- Reserves are pooled at the BCEAO. No reserve or net foreign asset data is in the brief, so reserve adequacy is unassessed (open item 6).
- The BCEAO held its policy rate at 3.0% and its marginal lending rate at 5.0% on 09-Sep-26 (BCEAO, High). CPI was 1.2% y/y in Aug-26, down from 1.9% in Jul-26 (INS, High). The peg is 655.957 XOF per EUR.
- The ex-ante real rate of 1.8% is computed on trailing CPI (data record, Medium).

### Growth and structure
- Real GDP growth is projected at 6.0% in 2026E after 6.5% in 2025 (IMF, Jun-26, Medium).
- Eni took a US$4B FID on Baleine Phase 3 in May 2026, targeting 150k bpd of oil and 200 mmcf/d of gas for the domestic grid (Eni, May-26, Medium). The fiscal take from the field is not quantified.

### Politics and security
- Ouattara was re-elected on 25-Oct-25 with 89.8% of the vote (CEI, Oct-25, Medium). The previous version cites a 50.1% turnout, not re-verified.
- Policy continuity is high near term. Sahel spillover in the north and long-run succession remain risks (Fitch, Jun-26, via previous version, Low).
- The previous version also cites tightening WAEMU bank liquidity (UMOA-Titres, Jun-26, Low, not re-verified).
