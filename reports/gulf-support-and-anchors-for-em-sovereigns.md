## View
- Gulf support is a conditional anchor. It has moved from open-ended deposits to longer rollovers, conversion into investments and IMF-gated commitments (Medium).
- Pakistan is the strongest current anchor. Saudi Arabia rolled USD 5bn to Dec-28 and added USD 3bn of fresh deposits in Apr-26 (SBP via Tribune, 29-Jul-26, Medium–High). S&P upgraded Pakistan to B (22-Jul-26, Medium).
- Egypt is the live test. A Saudi medium-term deposit of about USD 5.3–5.4bn matures in Oct-26, and conversion into investments is under negotiation (Medium). The EFF ends in Dec-26.
- The anchor changes the recovery distribution as well as default probability. Gulf creditors prefer equity and asset stakes, so bondholders cannot assume pari passu treatment (framework High; inference Low).
- Donor quality now varies. Saudi and UAE interests diverge in 2026, and the war has cut Gulf growth, but sovereign balance sheets remain intact (Medium).
- Biggest risk: the Egypt deposit is not renewed or converted on acceptable terms while the IMF programme is ending.
- Confidence: Medium. Deposit figures rest on press reports of official statements, and no primary central bank or IMF page was read in full.

## Analysis
### Pakistan
- SBP Governor Jameel Ahmad said Saudi Arabia rolled USD 5bn "till December 2028". Total Saudi deposits are USD 8bn, including USD 3bn received in Apr-26. Gross external financing requirement is USD 21.5bn, and reserves were USD 17.3bn on 17-Jul-26 (Tribune, 29-Jul-26, https://tribune.com.pk/story/2620947/ksa-rolled-over-5-billion-for-three-years-sbp-governor-says; ProPakistani, 30-Jul-26, https://propakistani.pk/2026/07/30/saudis-roll-over-5-billion-to-pakistan-for-3-years/) (Medium–High).
- S&P upgraded Pakistan to B, stable, citing IMF-backed reforms (Profit/Pakistan Today, 22-Jul-26, https://profit.pakistantoday.com.pk/2026/07/22/sandp-upgrades-pakistans-sovereign-credit-rating-to-b-on-imf-backed-reforms) (Medium).
- The three-year tenor lifts the Gulf cliff beyond the typical IMF programme horizon. Reserves of USD 17.3bn include these deposits, so usable reserves are smaller (inference, Medium).

### Egypt
- CBE holds about USD 10.3bn of Saudi deposits: USD 5bn short-term, renewed annually, and USD 5.3bn medium-term maturing Oct-26. The CBE is working with the finance and investment ministries on a swap converting part of the deposits into investments in real estate and other sectors (Enterprise, 25-May-25, https://enterpriseam.com/egypt/2025/05/25/saudi-deposits-could-be-converted-into-new-investments-in-real-estate-among-other-sectors/; search summary, 09-Oct-26) (Medium).
- The New Arab reports USD 5.434bn due in Oct-26 under negotiation for renewal or conversion. It also says USD 18.3bn of GCC deposits are barred from withdrawal until the EFF ends in Dec-26 (New Arab, 22-Sep-26, https://www.newarab.com/news/talks-over-saudi-deposit-egypts-bank-amid-iran-yemen-wars) (Medium).
- Kuwait's USD 2bn deposit was renewed at end-2024 and a further renewal was being sought in Apr-26 as regional conflict stalled FDI conversions (AGBI, Dec-24, https://agbi.com/economy/2024/12/kuwait-renews-2bn-deposit-with-central-bank-of-egypt; Enterprise, 09-Apr-26, https://enterpriseam.com/egypt/2026/04/09/central-bank-of-egypt-looks-to-renew-usd-2-bn-kuwaiti-deposit-as-regional-conflict-stalls-fdi-conversions/) (Medium).
- IMF Country Report 26/69 covers the fifth and sixth reviews and the first RSA review (https://www.imf.org/-/media/files/publications/cr/2026/english/1egyea2026001.pdf). It was not read, so the DSA treatment of Gulf deposits is unverified (Medium).
- Ratings: S&P B/B stable (Enterprise, 14-Apr-26, https://enterpriseam.com/egypt/2026/04/14/sp-global-affirms-egypts-rating-at-b-b-stable-outlook/) (Medium). Moody's Caa1, positive, per Business Monthly, date not confirmed (Low–Medium).
- Conversion into equity or real estate reduces official liabilities but gives the Gulf creditor an asset claim outside the bond waterfall (inference, Medium). The Ras El-Hekma ADQ deal (Feb-24, about USD 35bn) is the template (Medium).

### Bahrain
- A search result summary on 09-Oct-26 gives Fitch B+ negative (affirmed Feb-26, outlook cut from stable), S&P B stable, Moody's B2 negative. Fitch says the rating includes "exceptionally strong support" from GCC partners, mainly Saudi Arabia and the UAE (arab.news, Feb-26, https://arab.news/4zuc4) (Medium).
- Fitch projects debt at about 136% of GDP and a deficit near 9% of GDP in 2026 (same source) (Medium).
- A separate press item says S&P holds B stable (The National, 23-May-26). Fitch's Paul Gamble attributes resilience to "prospects for additional support" (Hellenic Shipping News, undated). The Moody's rating is not verified from a primary source (Low). Ratings are recorded as reported and are unresolved (Open item 2).

### Gulf capacity and politics
- Fitch affirmed UAE at AA- and projects 2026 real GDP of -4.8% for the UAE and -1.7% for Saudi Arabia. Moody's holds Saudi Aa3, Qatar Aa2, Kuwait A1 (The National, 23-May-26, https://www.thenationalnews.com/business/economy/2026/05/23/gulf-countries-maintain-credit-ratings-and-stable-outlooks-amid-iran-war-uncertainties/) (Medium).
- Fitch put Qatar and Ras Al Khaimah on Rating Watch Negative (Gulf Daily News, date unconfirmed, https://www.gdnonline.com/Details/1397146/GCC-sovereigns-remain-resilient-as-Fitch-cuts-global-outlook) (Low–Medium).
- Gulf SWFs committed USD 53.9bn across 108 deals in H1-26 (Khaleej Times, https://www.khaleejtimes.com/business/gulf-sovereign-funds-defy-iran-war-jitters-commit-record-dh198-billion-in-h1-2026) (Medium).
- Foreign Policy describes a Saudi-led grouping with Qatar, Turkey, Pakistan and Egypt, with the UAE outside it (Foreign Policy, 01-Jul-26, https://foreignpolicy.com/2026/07/01/saudi-qatar-pakistan-turkey-egypt-uae-axis-alliance-iran-middle-east/) (Medium). A Commons Library briefing is titled "The UAE exits OPEC and Saudi-UAE tensions in 2026" (https://commonslibrary.parliament.uk/research-briefings/cbp-10833/). Only the title was read (Low–Medium).
- Implication: Saudi-linked recipients (Pakistan, Egypt) and UAE-linked recipients carry different risks. This is inference (Low).

### Reserves treatment
- The framework asks for gross reserves, reserves net of one-year drains, and the central bank's own net definition (knowledge/frameworks/sovereign-credit.md, section 10a, High as method).
- Gulf deposits are rolled, so they behave as quasi-reserves. They are not senior debt (Medium).
