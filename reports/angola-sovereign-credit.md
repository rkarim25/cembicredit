## View
- Fundamentals support a stabilising B-category credit: Moody's B3/Positive (25-Sep-26), S&P B-/Stable (14-Aug-26), Fitch B-/Stable (08-May-26) (Medium).
- Drivers: CPI fell from 12.42% (Mar-26) to 8.78% (Aug-26) with the kwanza flat at about 912–913/USD. The current account was +$2.8B over the last four quarters. Commodity terms of trade are +17.5% over 12 months. Debt was about 49% of GDP at end-2025. Chinese oil-backed debt is cited at $6.83B (mid-2026) against $16.3B in 2020.
- The 2026 external surplus is cyclical. The goods surplus is oil-price driven, while services (about -$2.0B to -$2.5B a quarter) and primary income (about -$1.1B to -$1.9B a quarter) are structurally negative.
- Liquidity buffer: gross reserves including gold were $15.8B (Aug-26). Reserves net of one-year predetermined and contingent drains were only $0.618B (Jul-26). The drains include non-sovereign and BNA items, so this overstates sovereign stress, but it is thin.
- Biggest risk: oil dependence (1.00–1.05M bpd) with a thin net buffer, ahead of the Aug-27 election and the 2028 Eurobond maturity.
- Three inputs conflict or are missing: the policy rate level, the Eurobond stock reconciliation, and fiscal debt-service metrics. No view on valuation is possible without Bloomberg levels.
- Confidence: Medium on the fundamental direction. Low on the policy rate level, the liability management details and fiscal strength. No relative-value call.

## Analysis

### Ratings and momentum
- Moody's affirmed B3 and moved the outlook to Positive from Stable on 25-Sep-26 (Moody's, 25-Sep-26, Medium; reasons as summarised in the knowledge base, not read in primary).
- S&P is B-/Stable (14-Aug-26) and Fitch is B-/Stable (08-May-26) (Medium). Only Moody's has a positive bias, so the rating drift is one-agency.

### External position
- Current account (IMF BOP, USD bn): Q3-25 +0.31, Q4-25 -1.07, Q1-26 +1.48, Q2-26 +2.08. The four-quarter sum is +2.8 (IMF, High). The 2026E surplus is 2.2% of GDP (IMF/BNA, Medium).
- Goods balance rose from +2.97 (Q4-25) to +6.45 (Q2-26). Terms-of-trade index jumped from 82.2 (Feb-26) to 96.4 (Mar-26) (IMF CTOT, Medium). The surplus is a price effect until proven otherwise.
- Net FDI is small: 1.8% of GDP in 2025 (World Bank WDI, High), and quarterly FDI liabilities were $0.04B–$0.18B.
- Gross reserves including gold: $15.8B in Aug-26, made up of $13.19B ex gold and $2.63B gold (IMF IL, High). BNA reports $14.9B and 6.8 months of import cover for Q2-26 (BNA, Medium). Definitions differ and are not reconciled.
- Reserves net of one-year drains: $0.618B (Jul-26), from predetermined drains of $14.457B and contingent drains of $1.567B (IMF reserve template, Medium). The May-26 value of -$208.8B is a data error and is ignored. Reserves momentum is +4.2% over 12 months.
- Chinese oil-collateralised debt: $16.3B (2020) to $6.83B (mid-2026), targeted to zero by 2028 (MINFIN/IMF, May-26, Medium). The DSRA release amount is unknown (open item 13).

### Fiscal and debt
- Public debt was about 49.2% of GDP at end-2025, under the 60% ceiling in the Fiscal Sustainability Law (MINFIN/IMF, Medium). Fiscal balance 2026E is -2.4% of GDP (IMF WEO, Medium). Nominal GDP is $152.4B (2026E, IMF WEO).
- No interest-to-revenue, primary balance, financing need, or domestic and FX-linked debt split is available. That blocks any view on whether B-/B3 is cheap or rich on fiscal grounds.
- Eurobond listing: $1.75B 8.250% 2028, $1.50B 8.000% 2029, $1.75B 8.750% 2032, $1.25B 9.375% 2048 and $1.75B 9.125% 2049, totalling $8.0B. The stated stock is $13.06B (MINFIN/UGD, Sep-26, Medium). The $5.06B difference is unexplained.
- The prior version cited a ~$750M tender of the 2028/2029s funded by 2031 and 2037 notes. These notes are not in the bond list, so the claim is Low until verified.

### Monetary
- CPI y/y: 12.42% (Mar-26), 11.58% (Apr-26), 10.88% (May-26), 10.11% (Jun-26), 9.33% (Jul-26), 8.78% (Aug-26) (INE, High). The AOA/USD was 912.1–913.1 over Mar–Aug-26 (BIS, High).
- Policy rate: the data record lists 14.75% (BNA, Sep-26) while the IMF series shows 17.5% (Mar–Apr-26) and 17.0% (May–Aug-26). The level and the size of the September move are unresolved (open item 1). Easing is directionally supported by disinflation, but the real-rate magnitude is not established.
- Banking (IMF FSI, Q3-25, High): CAR 24.14%, NPL 12.54%, provisions to NPL 132%, ROE 22.05%. The data is a year old.

### Growth and politics

- The next general election is in Aug-27 (Government of Angola, Medium). OGE 2027 approval is due 31-Dec-26, so the budget is pre-election and the oil price assumption matters.
