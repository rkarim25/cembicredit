## View
- Credit trajectory is positive: Moody's B3/Stable (09-Oct-26, press), Fitch B/Positive, S&P B-/Stable. Moody's raised the foreign-currency ceiling to B1 (Medium).
- The IMF ECF closed on 27-Jul-26. A 36-month non-financing PCI follows, and debt distress risk was cut to moderate (IMF, GNA, High). The policy anchor stays and the financing backstop goes.
- Fiscal consolidation is ahead of plan. The mid-year review shows a commitment deficit of 0.4% of GDP against a 2.0% target and debt of 45.0% of GDP at Jun-26 against 61.6% at end-2024 (GNA, Jul-26, Medium-High). Interest is 17.2% of revenue (Moody's via press, Medium).
- The policy rate is 14.0% against 5.2% CPI, a trailing real rate of about +880bp (BoG, GSS, High). A Fitch Solutions forecast of CPI near 9% by end-2026 (press, undated, Low) would erode that cushion.
- Reserves are the weak point. IMF gross reserves fell from US$12.41bn (Mar-26) to US$9.72bn (Jul-26), and ex-gold from US$9.36bn to US$6.53bn. BoG reports US$12.0bn for Sep-26. The series are unreconciled (Medium-Low).
- Biggest risk: a gold or terms-of-trade reversal or reserve drain with no IMF money behind it, as Eurobond service steps up (about US$6.4bn in 2027–30, World Bank, Medium).
- The 2027 budget, due in early Nov-26, is the next test of fiscal discipline.
- Stance: constructive, not adding on rating momentum alone. No Bloomberg levels were supplied, so valuation is unassessed.
- Confidence: Medium on the stabilisation story, Low on reserve liquidity and bond valuation.

## Analysis

### Ratings and programme status
- Moody's upgraded to B3 from Caa1 on 09-Oct-26, outlook stable, ceiling raised to B1 (MyJoyOnline 09-Oct-26 https://www.myjoyonline.com/moodys-upgrades-ghanas-credit-ratings-to-b3-but-changes-outlook-to-stable/; Zawya https://www.zawya.com/en/economy/moodys-lifts-ghana-rating-to-b3-as-debt-burden-eases-1587364; Medium, tier 3 reporting a tier 2 action). The prior positive outlook is dated 14-Apr-26 (Financial Afrik, Medium).
- Fitch is B/Positive after an upgrade from B-. Several outlets date it 08-May-26 (Bloomberg headline 08-May-26; Medium-High). The knowledge base says 22-May-26, which is unresolved.
- S&P: affirmed B-/stable on 27-Mar-26 after the Nov-25 upgrade from CCC+ (Financial Afrik, 29-Mar-26, Medium). Press says S&P "maintained B-/B, stable" and also affirmed the B- transfer and convertibility assessment (Graphic, ModernGhana, Capmad). It gives no day, so a September action is Low-Medium.
- IMF Board completed the sixth and final ECF review on 27-Jul-26 (US$371m, ECF total US$3bn), concluded the 2026 Article IV and reviewed a 36-month non-financing PCI. Risk of debt distress was raised from high to moderate (GNA https://gna.org.gh/2026/07/ghana-completes-imf-ecf-programme-secures-final-us371m/; MyJoyOnline; High on completion, Medium on PCI detail). The PCI documents and DSA were not read.

### External position
- IMF International Liquidity gross incl. gold: US$12.41bn (Mar-26), US$12.25bn (May-26), US$10.77bn (Jun-26), US$9.72bn (Jul-26). Ex-gold: US$9.36bn (Mar-26), US$6.53bn (Jul-26) (IMF, High on data).
- BoG: US$12.9bn in Jun-26 (5.0 months of cover) and US$12.0bn at end-Sep-26 (press, knowledge base, Medium). Press cites a record US$14.5bn in Feb-26 (Medium-Low). The BoG figure is about US$2.1bn above the IMF figure in Jun-26, so definitions differ. Net usable reserves are unknown (Low).
- Current account, US$bn: 1.715 (Q2-25), 0.579 (Q3-25), 4.667 (Q4-25), 3.097 (Q1-26), US$10.06bn over four quarters. The goods surplus drives it. FDI liabilities are about US$0.46–0.54bn a quarter (IMF BOP, High).
- Gold was 67% of goods exports in 2025 and the trade surplus was US$8.85bn by Aug-26 (press, Medium). The cedi fell nearly 10% Mar–Jun-26 (World Bank Africa Economic Update, Oct-26, via press, Medium).
- REER z-score +1.86 against the trailing 10-year mean (IMF CPI-based, Aug-26). It is a mean-reversion flag only (Medium-Low).

### Fiscal and debt
- Mid-year review: commitment deficit 0.4% of GDP against a 2.0% target, cash deficit 0.8% against 2.9%. Debt was 45.0% of GDP at Jun-26 against 44.7% at end-2025 and 61.6% at end-2024. No supplementary budget was sought (GNA https://gna.org.gh/2026/07/highlights-2026-mid-year-budget-review/, Medium-High).
- Moody's, via press, puts interest at 17.2% of H1-26 revenue (Medium).
- WEO 2026E shows debt of 53% of GDP and a fiscal balance of -2.3% of GDP. The gap to official figures is unreconciled, probably definition or vintage (Medium-Low).
- Eurobond principal and coupon of about US$6.4bn is due in 2027–30 (World Bank, Oct-26, via ModernGhana https://www.modernghana.com/news/1534409/ghana-faces-us64bn-eurobond-maturities-between.amp, Medium). Press cites about US$2bn in 2027 and about US$570m in Jan-27 (Low-Medium). Neither was rebuilt from the prospectus.
- A US$700m Eurobond obligation was settled early on 02-Jul-26 without, per the authorities, undue pressure on reserves (Ecofin https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-finances/0607-57109-ghana-makes-early-700-million-eurobond-repayment, Medium). Together with the Jul-26 reserve fall, it suggests debt service contributed to the drop. This is an inference (Low).
- Jul-26: further 2035 Step-Up and 1.5% 2037 amortising notes were issued against US$253.2m of 12.5% 2026 secured bonds (RNS, 10-Jul-26, Medium-High).
- The FY2026 Eurobond pause is not independently verified (Low-Medium). The 2027 budget is due in the first week of Nov-26 (MyJoyOnline, Medium).
- Arrears, energy-sector and COCOBOD contingent liabilities and the primary balance were not found, so the fiscal assessment is incomplete.

### Monetary and financial
- MPC held the policy rate at 14.0% for the third time in September (GNA, High on the rate, Medium on the date).
- CPI y/y was 3.4% (Apr-26), 5.3% (Jun-26), 5.0% (Aug-26) and 5.2% (Sep-26) (GSS, IMF, High). Non-food inflation was 6.8% in Aug-26 (press, Medium). The 880bp real rate uses trailing CPI. Fitch Solutions projects CPI near 9% by end-2026 (MyJoyOnline, undated, Low).
- Real GDP growth was 6.4% in Q1-26 and 6.0% in Q2-26 (press, Medium). WEO 2026E is 4.8%.
- Banks (IMF FSI, Q2-26): NPL 16.06% (18.11% in Q1), CAR 20.45%, provisions 77.64% of NPLs, ROE 23.15% (High). Asset quality is improving but weak, and ROE reflects high yields on government paper.

### Valuation
- No Bloomberg levels were supplied. Press cites a spread of about 239bp in Aug-26 (unverified, Low). Relative value cannot be assessed without open items 1, 2, 5 and 6.

## Where the models disagreed

Fitch's date has multi-outlet support and is kept as Medium-High. S&P's September action stays open under the equal tier 3 rule, because no source gives a day.
