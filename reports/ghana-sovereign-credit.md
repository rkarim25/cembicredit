## View
- Credit trajectory is positive. Moody's reportedly upgraded Ghana to B3 from Caa1 on 09-Oct-26 with a stable outlook (MyJoyOnline, Zawya, Medium). Fitch is B/Positive. S&P is B-/Stable.
- The IMF ECF closed on 27-Jul-26 with the final US$371m disbursement. A 36-month non-financing PCI follows, and risk of debt distress is back to moderate (IMF, GNA, High). Policy anchor stays; the financing backstop goes.
- Drivers are gold (67% of goods exports in 2025, press, Medium), a current account surplus (US$10.06bn over the last four quarters, IMF BOP, High), inflation of 5.2% (Sep-26) and a small overall deficit (press, Medium).
- Policy rate is 14.0% against 5.2% CPI. Trailing real rate is about +880bp, which leaves room to cut (BoG, GSS, High on data).
- Reserves are the weak point. IMF gross reserves incl. gold fell from US$12.41bn (Mar-26) to US$9.72bn (Jul-26), and ex-gold from US$9.36bn to US$6.53bn. BoG reports US$12.0bn for September. The two series are unreconciled (Medium-Low).
- Biggest risk: a gold price or terms-of-trade reversal, or a reserve drain, with no IMF money behind it and about US$6.4bn of Eurobond service in 2027–30 (World Bank, Oct-26, Medium).
- Other flags: REER z-score +1.86, bank NPLs 16.1%, and the rating-date conflicts in open item 7.
- Stance: constructive but not adding on rating momentum alone. The upgrade is probably largely priced, but no Bloomberg levels were supplied.
- Confidence: Medium on the stabilisation story. Low on reserve liquidity and bond valuation.

## Analysis

### Ratings and programme status
- Moody's upgraded to B3 from Caa1 on 09-Oct-26, outlook stable, and raised the foreign-currency ceiling to B1. It cited lower financing costs, fiscal discipline and gold exports (MyJoyOnline 09-Oct-26 https://www.myjoyonline.com/moodys-upgrades-ghanas-credit-ratings-to-b3-but-changes-outlook-to-stable/; Zawya, undated, https://www.zawya.com/en/economy/moodys-lifts-ghana-rating-to-b3-as-debt-burden-eases-1587364; Medium, tier 3 reporting a tier 2 action). The prior positive outlook is dated 14-Apr-26 (Financial Afrik, https://www.financialafrik.com/en/2026/04/14/moodys-revises-ghanas-outlook-to-positive/, Medium).
- Fitch is B/Positive after an upgrade from B- (Bloomberg headline 08-May-26, https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-08/ghana-s-credit-rating-upgraded-by-fitch-on-improved-finances, Medium). The date conflicts with the knowledge base.
- S&P affirmed B-/stable on 27-Mar-26 after the Nov-25 upgrade from CCC+ (Financial Afrik, 29-Mar-26, https://www.financialafrik.com/en/2026/03/29/ghana-makes-a-comeback-sp-confirms-b-b-rating-stable-outlook, Medium). No 18-Sep-26 action was found.
- IMF Board completed the sixth and final ECF review on 27-Jul-26 with a US$371m disbursement, which took the 39-month ECF to US$3bn. It approved a 36-month PCI and put the risk of debt distress at moderate (GNA Jul-26, https://gna.org.gh/2026/07/ghana-completes-imf-ecf-programme-secures-final-us371m/; IMF Article IV, https://www.imf.org/-/media/files/publications/cr/2026/english/1ghaea2026001.pdf; High). Article IV and PCI documents (DSA, financing assurances, targets) were not read in full.
- First PCI monitoring mission is 15-Dec-26 (IMF calendar via knowledge base, Medium).

### External position
- BoG reports gross reserves of US$12.0bn. Import cover is 4.5 months at 22-Sep-26 in press and 5.2 months at end-Sep-26 in the knowledge base (BoG, Medium). The conflict is unresolved.
- IMF International Liquidity: gross incl. gold US$12.41bn (Mar-26), US$12.25bn (May-26), US$10.77bn (Jun-26), US$9.72bn (Jul-26). Ex-gold US$9.36bn (Mar-26), US$6.53bn (Jul-26) (IMF, High on data; definitions differ from BoG's). A BoG figure of US$12.0bn would need a rise of about US$2.3bn in Aug–Sep. GoldBod reported US$1.871bn of FX in Sep-26 (knowledge base, unverified, Medium-Low).
- Current account, US$bn: 1.715 (Q2-25), 0.579 (Q3-25), 4.667 (Q4-25), 3.097 (Q1-26). The goods surplus drives it; services and primary income are negative. FDI liabilities run at about US$0.46–0.54bn a quarter (IMF BOP, High). The surplus is volatile and tied to gold.
- Trade surplus US$8.85bn by Aug-26 (press, Medium). Gold was 67% of goods exports in 2025 (press, Medium), so terms of trade are the dominant external driver.
- REER z-score +1.86 vs trailing 10-year mean (IMF CPI-based, Aug-26). It is a mean-reversion flag, not an equilibrium estimate (Medium-Low).

### Fiscal and debt
- Overall deficit was 0.2% of GDP against a 1.9% target, and public debt 45.9% of GDP against 44.7% in Dec-25 (press quoting official data, Medium). WEO 2026E shows debt of 53% of GDP and a fiscal balance of -2.3% of GDP. The two sets differ in definition and period and are not reconciled (Medium-Low).
- Eurobond maturities and service are about US$6.4bn in 2027–30 (World Bank Africa Economic Update, Oct-26, via ModernGhana https://www.modernghana.com/news/1534409/ghana-faces-us64bn-eurobond-maturities-between.amp, Medium). Sources differ on whether this is principal only or principal plus coupons.
- In Jul-26 Ghana issued further 2035 Step-Up and 1.5% 2037 amortising notes in exchange for US$253.2m of 12.5% 2026 secured bonds (RNS 10-Jul-26, https://www.ticker.app/lse/PT83/rns/2026-07-10/Issue-of-Debt/9695L, Medium-High).
- The Ministry of Finance's FY2026 Eurobond pause is not independently verified (Low-Medium). A US$1.5bn Eurobond plan story exists but is undated and may be old.
- No primary balance, interest-to-revenue, arrears or contingent-liability data (energy sector, COCOBOD) were found. The fiscal assessment is incomplete.

### Monetary and financial
- MPC held the policy rate at 14.0% for the third time (GNA, https://gna.org.gh/2026/09/bog-maintains-policy-rate-at-14-per-cent-for-third-time/; High on the rate, Medium on the date).
- CPI y/y: 3.2% (Mar-26), 5.3% (Jun-26), 5.0% (Aug-26), 5.2% (Sep-26) (GSS, IMF, High). Non-food inflation was 6.8% in Aug-26 on utility tariffs, with oil above US$100/bbl (press, Medium). The 880bp gap uses trailing CPI.
- Q2-26 real GDP growth 6.0% (press, Medium); WEO 2026E 4.8%.
- Banks (IMF FSI, Q2-26): NPLs 16.06% (18.11% in Q1), CAR 20.45%, provisions 77.64% of NPLs, ROE 23.15% (High). Asset quality is improving but remains weak, and sovereign exposure is a contingent liability.

### Valuation
- No Bloomberg levels were supplied. Press cites a sovereign spread of about 239bp in Aug-26 (unverified, Low). On that figure the credit trades tight for a B-/B3 name, and relative value cannot be assessed without the index data requested in open items 1–2.
