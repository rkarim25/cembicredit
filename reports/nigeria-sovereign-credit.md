## View
- Constructive, upgraded from neutral to modestly constructive. All three agencies now have a positive action in 2026: S&P to B (15-May-26), Moody's B3/Positive (29-Aug-26), Fitch B/Positive (09-Oct-26). The repo page still shows Fitch at Stable and is stale (Medium-High).
- Dollar ability to pay is strong. Gross reserves are $55.07B (09-Oct-26, after a CBN downward revision from about $58B), and the current account surplus was $15.61B over the last four quarters.
- Disinflation continues: CPI was 14.63% in Sep-26 (15.39% in Aug-26), the lowest since Oct-20. The MPR of 23% leaves a strongly positive real rate.
- Fiscal is the binding constraint. Debt is about 32% of GDP, but interest takes more than half of revenue (53.7% projected for 2026 per press on the IMF Article IV; the framework's danger zone starts at 30%).
- Biggest risk: an oil shock that hits FX inflows and revenue together. S&P assumed Brent at USD 100; the IMF Oct-26 WEO assumes an average of USD 95.74 for 2026. The election cycle is the second risk.
- Not covered: market levels, net reserves, IMF status, 2027 financing plan. Confidence: Medium on fundamentals; Low on value.

## Analysis

### Ratings and reform trajectory
- Fitch affirmed the Long-Term IDR at B and revised the outlook to Positive on 09-Oct-26. It cited stronger reserves ($54.9B at 25-Sep-26, against $32B in mid-April 2024), reforms and moderating inflation, a 2026 current account surplus of 6.4% of GDP and reserve cover of 6.3 months of current external payments by year-end. Fiscal pressure and debt service costs remain concerns (Nairametrics, 10-Oct-26, https://nairametrics.com/2026/10/10/fitch-revises-nigerias-credit-outlook-to-positive-affirms-b-rating/; ThisDay, 11-Oct-26, Medium-High; agency release not read).
- Fitch moved outlooks on 11 Nigerian banks to Positive on the lower sovereign risk (ThisDay, 16-Oct-26; Nairametrics, 15-Oct-26, Medium).
- Moody's affirmed B3 and moved the outlook to positive on 28/29-Aug-26, citing reserves, growth and the current account, and flagging weak revenue capacity and debt affordability (Nairametrics, 29-Aug-26, Medium).
- S&P upgraded to B from B- (stable) on 15-May-26, assuming Brent at USD 100 for the rest of 2026 (AllAfrica, 19-May-26, Medium). The IMF April reference scenario was USD 82 (IMF WEO, Apr-26, High), so the upgrade is oil-sensitive.

### External position
- Gross reserves were $55.07B at 09-Oct-26 (CBN via Nairametrics corrigendum, 14-Oct-26, Medium-High). The IMF series including gold was $54.85B in Aug-26, up 30.8% in 12 months; ex gold $51.78B (IMF International Liquidity, High).
- Fitch's 2026 current account projection (6.4% of GDP) compares with 5.8% in the IMF WEO (Medium). Current account, IMF BOP (High), USD bn: 2025-Q2 5.17, Q3 4.06, Q4 1.40, 2026-Q1 4.98; last four quarters $15.61B. 2025-Q4 shows the sensitivity.
- Import cover is 9.2 months in the repo (Medium), against Fitch's 6.3 months of current external payments. The definitions differ and are not reconciled.
- REER (IMF, CPI-based) is 93.29 (Aug-26), 10-year z-score -0.51: no overvaluation signal (High). ₦ per USD was 1,352.97 in Aug-26 (BIS, High). Fitch cites about 7% naira appreciation in the first nine months of 2026 (ThisDay, 16-Oct-26, Medium).
- Upstream output was 1.68M bpd in Aug-26 and Dangote throughput 736,470 bpd (NUPRC, Aug-26, Medium).
- Net usable reserves are unknown (open item 5).

### Monetary policy and real rates
- MPR is 23.00% from the 307th MPC on 22-Sep-26 (CBN, High). The path from 27.0% (IMF series to Apr-26) is unreconciled (open item 12).
- CPI was 14.63% y/y in Sep-26, 0.06% m/m; food 19.59% y/y, core 12.95% (NBS release 15-Oct-26, via Legit.ng, Tekedia, VON, Medium). I did not read the NBS report. MPR minus CPI is about +837 bps ex-post.
- Next MPC: 24-Nov-26 (CBN calendar, Medium).

### Fiscal and debt
- Total public debt was ₦166.79T ($120.93B) at 30-Jun-26: domestic ₦91.59T, external $54.52B (DMO, 25-Sep-26, High). Debt/GDP is 32.3% (DMO, Jun-26, Medium; label unclear).
- Article IV interest/revenue is 53.7% for 2026 and 52.4% for 2027; public external debt is projected to rise from $51.9B in 2025 to $72.6B in 2027 (IMF Article IV, 01-Jun-26, via BusinessDay, Medium). The repo's ">55%" basis is unresolved.
- IMF WEO 2026E: fiscal balance -4.4% of GDP (3.5% in 2025), real growth 4.1%, nominal GDP $377.4B (Medium). The 2026 budget was signed in mid-April (Medium).
- No primary balance or gross financing need is available, so the pb* gap cannot be computed.

### Eurobonds and market access
- Eurobonds outstanding are $15.12B, 27.7% of external debt (DMO, 30-Jun-26, High).
- Nov-25 dual tranche raised $2.35B: 2036 at 8.63% and 2046 at 9.13%. The split is disputed between repo and press (open item 11).
- Next maturity: $1.50B 6.500% bullet, 28-Nov-27 (DMO, Sep-26, High on the repo record).
- No levels are available, so relative value cannot be assessed.

### Politics
- The presidential election is reported for 16-Jan-27 and governorship elections for 06-Feb-27 (AllAfrica, 27-Feb-26; Semafor, 16-Feb-26, Medium). APC zones backed Tinubu as sole candidate (AllAfrica, 26-Mar-26, Medium). Fitch cited reform continuity ahead of the election.

### Banking system
- Q2-26 FSIs: CAR 13.15%, Tier 1 11.43%, NPL 10.82% (10.59% in Q1), provisions/NPL 64.11% (51.45%), ROE 28.11%, FX open position -12.67% of capital (IMF FSI, High). NPLs rise slowly while provisioning improves.
