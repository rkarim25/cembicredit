## View
- Stance: Refinancing Watch. The repo's "Tactical Overweight" (23-Sep-26) predates two negative rating actions and should be treated as stale until prices are refreshed.
- Moody's cut the CFR to Caa1 from B3, outlook Negative (24-Sep-26). Fitch cut the IDR and senior secured rating to B- and placed them on Rating Watch Negative (29-Sep-26).
- Houlihan Lokey and Servo Capital were appointed as advisers on capital structure on 14-Sep-26. Zorlu Holding has filed for financial restructuring (FYY).
- Core issue: quarterly interest (~US$64m) exceeds quarterly cash EBITDA (~US$56m in 2Q26). 1H26 cash EBITDA was US$102m against US$260-270m full-year guidance.
- Liquidity to the Oct-27 amortisation depends on the ADB loan. Closing is unconfirmed.
- Biggest risk: Holding-level restructuring spilling into the Zorlu Enerji perimeter through related-party receivables, share pledge and cross-default, with the 23-Oct-26 coupon as the first test.
- Mitigant: YEKDEM-linked generation is insulated from the collapse in spot power prices; the asset base is large relative to the 63.5 price.
- Confidence: Medium on direction (negative); Low on relative value, because no post-downgrade pricing and no agency rationale were available.

## Analysis
### Ratings and corporate actions
- Moody's: CFR B3 to Caa1, outlook Negative (Foreks, 24-Sep-26, Medium; wording taken from press summaries, not the agency page). CNBC-e headlined the Fitch action as "first Moody's, now Fitch" (CNBC-e, ~29-Sep-26, Medium).
- Fitch: IDR and senior secured rating revised to B-, Rating Watch Negative, recovery rating RR4 (KAP disclosure via Foreks, 29-Sep-26, High on the action). The prior Fitch rating is not established. Intellinews gave B+/Negative from late 2025 (Medium, dated).
- The repo's "B- / CCC+" does not correspond to either agency's current rating on the evidence found. Open item 2.
- Houlihan Lokey and Servo Capital appointed on 14-Sep-26 to evaluate financing options and strategic alternatives for the capital structure (CNBC-e, Sep-26, Medium).
- Zorlu Holding submitted a Financial Restructuring (FYY) application covering its Vestel and Textile groups (DHA, 2026, date not confirmed, Medium). The Holding link is the main contagion channel. The repo records 28.8bn TRY of related-party advances to the Holding (repo, 23-Sep-26, Medium).
- Causation of the rating actions is inferred, not sourced (Low).

### Financials and liquidity
- Repo 2025A (USD m basis as recorded): EBITDA 13,094, FCF -8,915, net leverage 4.65x, interest cover 1.6x (issuer record, 23-Sep-26, Medium; unit labelling should be checked, as the Cognitive Credit extract is in TRY).
- 1H26 (TRY): revenue 13.0bn (1H25 17.3bn), EBITDA 6.3bn, net loss 6.03bn, net debt/EBITDA 4.11x (Midas summary of KAP release, 14-Aug-26, Medium).
- 2Q26: cash EBITDA US$56m (generation US$24m, distribution US$32m), cash ~US$51m, FCF burn ~US$15m, escrow release ~US$29m (Jefferies, 19-Aug-26; JPMorgan, 14-Aug-26; Medium).
- Distribution regulatory underinvestment penalties ~US$10m in 1H26, ~US$10m in 2H26 and ~US$20m a year to 2030 (JPMorgan, 09-Aug-26, Medium).
- Local TRY debt fell from US$83m (1Q26) to US$70m (2Q26) and ~US$55m currently (Jefferies, 19-Aug-26, Medium).
- Spot power averaged US$20/MWh in 2Q26 against US$61/MWh in 2Q25 (JPMorgan, 01-Jul-26, Medium).

### Capital structure and asset sales
- Benchmark: ZOREN 11.00% 2030 HoldCo bond, issued Oct-24 at US$800m per repo. Secondary press cites upsizing to US$1.1bn and a further US$100m in 2025 (Low-Medium). Unreconciled; open item 8.
- Fitch expected interest above 63% of EBITDA in 2025 and 50% in 2026, with total debt ~US$1.3bn at end-2026 (Intellinews, undated, Low-Medium).
- Dorad sale closed at ~US$210m gross, of which only 21-40% went to debt reduction against the 60% initially guided (Jefferies, 18-Aug-25, Medium).
- Gökçedağ wind sold on 29-Dec-25 for US$117.0m after liabilities (KAP summary, Medium). Use of proceeds unknown.
- Wren House Infrastructure bought 12.34% for US$38m, with proceeds applied to debt, and a US$150m loan repayment extended to 2028 (Ekonomist, date not confirmed, Low-Medium).
- The 375MW wind/storage portfolio (monetisable after licence conversion, 30-Jun-27) and the Pakistan assets are the remaining disposal pipeline (repo, Medium).

### Liquidity bridge
- The US$95m ADB loan has a 6-year tenor, 2-year grace period, is pari passu and has no guarantors (JPMorgan, 09-Aug-26, Medium). No closing confirmation was found in searches of 09-Oct-26 (Low on status).
- Without it, 2Q26 cash of ~US$51m is under one quarter of interest cost, so the ADB loan is central to the Oct-27 bridge.
