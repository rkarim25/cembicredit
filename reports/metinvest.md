## View
Metinvest is a stressed credit that has kept paying bonds through the war, with a moderate end-June balance sheet and impaired H2 26 earnings. Net debt at end-June 26 was $866mn (1.2x LTM EBITDA ex-JVs). The $428mn April-26 notes were repaid from internal cash, and the 2027 (Mar-26) and 2029 (May-26) coupons were paid on schedule.

Zaporizhstal has been idle since 11-Aug-26 after four strikes, the last on 17-Sep-26. No restart is public. Barclays has H2 26E EBITDA ex-JVs of $98mn and net debt/EBITDA of 2.4x for 2026E and 2.5x for 2027E.

Cash of $191mn at end-June sits against $332mn due on 01-Oct-27, and H1 interest paid was $67mn. The non-Ukrainian re-rolling assets (c.$150mn EBITDA) and the payment record are the offsets.

The biggest risk is further strikes that turn a temporary outage into lasting capacity loss before the 2027 maturity, with no funding route for those notes yet visible. Ratings, liquidity, covenants and market levels remain unverified, so no directional bond call is made. Confidence: Medium on events, Low on the earnings and liquidity outlook.

## Analysis
### Operations and the strikes
- Zaporizhstal stopped after the ballistic strike of 11-Aug-26. It was hit again on 27-Aug (five missiles), 12-Sep (four) and 17-Sep-26, 17 missiles in total, with 8 killed and 28 injured. It has "remained completely idled since an attack on August 11" (SteelOrbis, 17-Sep-26, Medium).
- Metinvest will declare force majeure on part of its contracts after the Zaporizhstal and Kamet stoppages (Forbes.ua, 15-Sep-26; Kurs.com.ua, Sep-26, Medium-High). The scope and volume are not disclosed.
- Russian strikes in Aug–Sep 26 shifted from the grid to steelmaking utilities: gas mains, oxygen plants and water pipelines (J.P. Morgan EM Credit Conference note, 15-Sep-26, via repo, Medium).
- No source reports a restart. Search results for October returned only Aug–Sep items and undated older partial-resumption stories that cannot be tied to this episode (Low).
- Barclays is the only quantification of the earnings impact: H2 26E EBITDA ex-JVs of $98mn against H1 of $300mn (Barclays, Oct-26, Medium).

### Financials and liquidity
- H1 26: revenue $3,657mn, EBITDA ex-JVs $300mn, net loss $202mn, capex $89mn, interest paid $67mn (Metinvest H1 26, 14-Sep-26, via repo, Medium-High). I did not re-read the primary report.
- End-June 26: gross debt $1,057mn, cash $191mn, net debt $866mn, 1.2x LTM EBITDA ex-JVs (same source, Medium-High).
- FY25 EBITDA was $765mn (down 24.2%) and the net loss $191mn. Total debt fell 15% in 2025, from $1,705mn to $1,441mn (Interfax-Ukraine, 23-Apr-26, Medium).
- Barclays has net debt/EBITDA at 2.4x for 2026E and 2.5x for 2027E (Barclays, Oct-26, Medium). H1 interest paid of $67mn is about $134mn annualised, which is above Barclays' FY26E EBITDA run-rate in H2 ($98mn for the half), so H2 cover is thin.
- Liquidity data are missing: RCF, covenants, offshore cash and trade finance.

### Capital structure and market
- 2027 notes: $332.354mn, 7.650%, due 01-Oct-27, coupons 1-Mar and 1-Sep (Metinvest IR, 09-Oct-26, High). Total 2027 debt service is $351mn (Interfax-Ukraine, 23-Apr-26, Medium).
- 2029 notes: $500mn, 7.750%, due 17-Oct-29, coupons 17-May and 17-Nov (metinvestholding.com, 16-Oct-26, High). Total 2029 debt service is $550mn (Interfax-Ukraine, 23-Apr-26, Medium).
- Coupons paid on time: 2027s on 1-Mar-26 (Forbes.ua, 02-Mar-26, Medium-High), 2029s in May-26 (Forbes.ua, 18-May-26, Medium-High). The 1-Sep-26 coupon is unconfirmed.
- The 8.5% notes due 23-Apr-26 ($428mn) were repaid in full from internal cash (Interfax-Ukraine, 23-Apr-26, Medium-High).
- Barclays levels: 2027s c.87 cash / 23% yield; 2029s c.77 cash / 18% yield (Barclays, Oct-26, Medium). The inverted yield curve is consistent with the 2027s being the near-term restructuring candidate. This rests on one dealer.

### Ratings
- The repo records Moody's Caa3 Stable (Aug-26), S&P CCC+ Negative and Fitch CCC- (Barclays, Oct-26, Low-Medium).
- An undated snippet shows CCC- from S&P as well (Low). Older items show S&P and Fitch at B/B+ or BB- after earlier upgrades (GMK Center, undated, stale, Low).
- No agency release was found.

### Diversification
- European re-rolling assets (Italy, Bulgaria, UK, Romania) produced 1.5mt in 2025 and c.$150mn EBITDA, about 23% of group EBITDA ex-JVs (Barclays, Oct-26, Medium). Cash upstreaming is unverified.
- Zaporizhstal had shipped over 22,000t of hot-rolled coil to the Iași plant in 2026, with up to 180,000t planned (Romania-Insider and SteelOrbis, Feb-26, Medium). The idling therefore also hits feedstock for the European assets.
- JORC iron ore reserves are 2.1bn tonnes (July-21 audit) (Barclays, Oct-26, Medium).

### Data quality of the repo record
- The "2024A" row repeats the end-June 26 balance sheet (gross debt 1,057, cash 191, net debt 866). Its FCF is -193 in the table and -550 in the metadata.
- The 2021A row shows FCF of 4,013 against CFO of 484.8, which is implausible.
- The stored benchmark "METINV 15.65% 2029" at 93.27 / 14.98% / 1022bp (27-Sep-26) matches no bond.
- No Cognitive Credit model exists. A model must be built from Metinvest's FY25 and H1 26 IFRS reports at metinvestholding.com.
