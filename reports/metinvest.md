## View
Metinvest is a stressed credit with a moderate pre-strike balance sheet and impaired H2 26 earnings. End-June 26 net debt was $866mn (1.2x LTM EBITDA ex-JVs), and the $428mn April-26 bond was repaid from internal cash. Zaporizhstal has been idle since 11-Aug-26 and was struck four times, most recently on 17-Sep-26. No restart date is public.

Barclays forecasts H2 26E EBITDA of $98mn and net debt/EBITDA of 2.4x in 2026E and 2.5x in 2027E. Against that, H1 interest paid was $67mn, and cash was $191mn against $332mn due on 01-Oct-27. The non-Ukrainian re-rolling assets (c.$150mn EBITDA) and the company's record of paying bonds through the war are the offsets.

The biggest risk is further strikes that turn a temporary outage into permanent capacity loss ahead of the 2027 maturity. Current ratings, liquidity, covenants and market levels are unverified, so no directional bond call is made. Confidence: Medium on events, Low on the earnings and liquidity outlook.

## Analysis
### Operations and the strikes
- Zaporizhstal stopped after the ballistic strike of 11-Aug-26, with damage to energy, infrastructure and blast-furnace facilities. It was hit again on 27-Aug (five missiles) and 12-Sep (four missiles) (Kurs.com.ua, Sep-26, Medium).
- A fourth attack on 17-Sep-26 hit engineering, railway infrastructure and main technological equipment. That makes 17 missiles in total, with 8 killed and 28 injured. The plant has "remained completely idled since an attack on August 11" (SteelOrbis, 17-Sep-26, Medium).
- Metinvest will declare force majeure on part of its contracts after the Zaporizhstal and Kamet stoppages (Forbes.ua and Kurs.com.ua, 15-Sep-26, Medium-High). The scope and volume affected are not disclosed.
- As of late Sep-26, no source reports a full restart; the plants were clearing rubble and recovering (Kurs.com.ua, Sep-26, Medium). I found nothing for October, and an undated Zaporizhstal item on partial resumption cannot be tied to this episode.
- Earnings impact: no source quantifies it beyond Barclays' H2 26E EBITDA of $98mn against H1 of $300mn (Barclays, Oct-26, Medium).

### Financials and liquidity
- H1 26: revenue $3,657mn, EBITDA ex-JVs $300mn, net loss $202mn, capex $89mn, interest paid $67mn (Metinvest H1 26, 14-Sep-26, via repo, Medium-High).
- End-June 26: gross debt $1,057mn, cash $191mn, net debt $866mn, 1.2x LTM EBITDA ex-JVs (same source, Medium-High). The primary report was not re-read in this run.
- End-25 gross debt was $1.441bn, down from $1.705bn (Interfax-Ukraine, 2026, Medium).
- The $428mn 8.5% notes due 23-Apr-26 were repaid in full from internal cash (Interfax-Ukraine and GMK Center, Apr-26, Medium-High).
- Barclays projects net debt/EBITDA of 2.4x in 2026E and 2.5x in 2027E (Barclays, Oct-26, Medium). H2 26E EBITDA of $98mn is below the $67mn of H1 interest paid on an annualised basis, so cover is thin.
- RCF, covenant and offshore cash data are missing.

### Capital structure and market
- 2027 notes: $332.354mn, 7.650%, due 01-Oct-27, coupons 1-Mar and 1-Sep (Metinvest IR, 09-Oct-26, High). The coupon due 1-Mar-26 was paid on time (Forbes.ua, 02-Mar-26, Medium-High).
- 2029 notes: $500mn, 7.750%, due 17-Oct-29, coupons 17-May and 17-Nov (metinvestholding.com, 16-Oct-26, High).
- Barclays levels: 2027s c.87 cash / 23% yield; 2029s c.77 cash / 18% yield (Barclays, Oct-26, Medium). The curve is inverted in yield, consistent with the 2027s being the near-term restructuring candidate. This rests on one dealer and needs a Bloomberg check.

### Ratings
- The repo records Moody's Caa3 Stable (Aug-26), S&P CCC+ Negative and Fitch CCC- (Barclays, Oct-26, Low-Medium). No agency release was found, and the mix of CCC+ and CCC- is unusual.
- Older sources show S&P at B and Fitch at BB- after the earlier upgrades. These are stale (GMK Center, undated, Low).

### Diversification
- European re-rolling assets (Italy, Bulgaria, UK, Romania) produced 1.5mt in 2025 and c.$150mn EBITDA, about 23% of group EBITDA ex-JVs (Barclays, Oct-26, Medium). Cash upstreaming is unverified.
- Zaporizhstal had shipped over 22,000t of hot-rolled coil to the Iași plant in 2026, with up to 180,000t planned (Romania-Insider and SteelOrbis, Feb-26, Medium). The idling therefore hits feedstock for the European assets too.

### Data quality of the repo record
- The "2024A" row repeats the end-June 26 balance sheet. Its FCF is -193 in the table but -550 in the metadata.
- The stored benchmark, price, yield and spread are unverified.
- The record shows "CCC+" only.
- No Cognitive Credit model exists. A model must be built from Metinvest's FY25 and H1 26 IFRS reports at metinvestholding.com.
