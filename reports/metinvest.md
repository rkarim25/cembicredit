## View
Metinvest is a stressed CCC-area credit. The H1 26 balance sheet is moderate: net debt $866mn, 1.2x, and a $428mn bond repaid in April from internal cash. It pre-dates the strikes.

Zaporizhstal has been idle since 11-Aug-26 after repeated ballistic strikes, and force majeure is being declared on part of its contracts. The central risk is a prolonged loss of Ukrainian steel and ore output ahead of the $332mn Oct-27 maturity. The offset is the non-Ukrainian re-rolling assets (c.$150mn EBITDA) and a record of paying bonds through the war.

The H2 EBITDA hit, RCF headroom and current ratings are unverified. No directional call on the bonds is made without Bloomberg levels. Confidence: Medium on events, Low on the earnings and liquidity outlook.

## Analysis
### Operations and the strikes
- Zaporizhstal stopped after the ballistic strike of 11-Aug-26, with damage to energy, infrastructure and blast-furnace facilities. It was struck again on 27-Aug (five missiles) and 12-Sep (four missiles) (Kurs.com.ua, Sep-26, Medium). The repo log counts four barrages and 17 missiles by 17-Sep, with 8 killed and 28 injured (J.P. Morgan EM Credit Conference note, 15-Sep-26, Medium).
- Zaporizhstal and Kamet Steel have halted, and force majeure will be declared on part of the contracts (Interfax-Ukraine and Kurs.com.ua, c.15-Sep-26, Medium-High). The company cites c.30% higher production costs and restoration costs of "tens of millions" (Interfax-Ukraine, c.15-Sep-26, Medium). These figures come from the first model's research; I did not re-read the article.
- The company names 2026 as one of its hardest years of the war, citing port export limits, EU quotas and CBAM (Kurs.com.ua, Sep-26, Medium).
- The earnings impact is not quantified by any source.

### Financials and liquidity
- H1 26: revenue $3,657mn, EBITDA ex-JVs $300mn, net loss $202mn. End-June gross debt was $1,057mn, cash $191mn, net debt $866mn, and net leverage 1.2x (Metinvest H1 26, 14-Sep-26, via repo log, Medium-High).
- Press quotes adjusted EBITDA of $311mn and net leverage of 1.4x at end-25 (SteelOrbis and GMK Center, c.14-Sep-26, Medium). The $300mn and $311mn figures likely differ by basis; the primary report has not been read.
- The $428mn bond was repaid in April 26, taking wartime bond debt service above $1bn (Metinvest H1 26, 14-Sep-26, High-Medium).
- Cash of $191mn against $332mn due in Oct-27, with an H1 net loss of $202mn and the plants offline, makes the maturity the refinancing test. RCF, covenant and offshore cash data are missing.

### Capital structure
- The 2027 notes are $332.354mn outstanding, due 01-Oct-27. The issuer page shows a 7.650% coupon, paid 1-Mar and 1-Sep (Metinvest IR, accessed 09-Oct-26, High). The repo log shows 7.75% (Open item 8).
- The 2029 notes are $500mn (repo log, Medium).
- The September 2027-note coupon was paid on schedule; the issuer says "all payments are going according to plan" (Interfax-Ukraine/GMK Center, Medium on date).

### Ratings
- Moody's Caa3 Stable (Aug-26), S&P CCC+ Negative and Fitch CCC- (Barclays, Oct-26, via repo log, Medium). A search result summary gave the same set (09-Oct-26, Low-Medium). Agency pages were not accessed.
- The repo record shows only "CCC+", which is stale or partial.

### Diversification
- Standalone re-rolling assets in Italy, Bulgaria, the UK and Romania produced 1.5mt in 2025 and c.$150mn EBITDA, c.23% of group ex-JV (Barclays, Oct-26, Medium). Cash upstreaming is unverified.

### Data quality of the repo record
- The "2024A" row duplicates the end-June 26 balance sheet.
- `fcf_2024a` of -550 conflicts with the row's -193, and capex is negative.
- The benchmark "15.65% 2029" at 93.27 does not match any coupon found. Treat the stored price, yield and spread as unverified.
- No Cognitive Credit model exists. A model must be built from the Metinvest H1 26 and FY25 IFRS reports at metinvestholding.com.

### Searched, not found
- No agency release after the strikes. No primary source on RCF or covenants. No Oct-27 liability-management announcement. No official Ukraine sovereign data retrieved in this run.
