### Working hypotheses, unverified
- Public debt: believed low and mostly external and concessional. No ratio is cited and none can be confirmed this run. (Prior knowledge, Low)
- External: believed to have a services surplus (tourism, ICT, transit) plus remittances, with some dependence on Russian-linked flows. Unquantified. (Prior knowledge, Low)
- Monetary: believed to be inflation targeting with a floating lari and NBG FX purchases. No policy rate, CPI or reserves level can be cited. (Prior knowledge, Low)
- Politics: direction believed negative since the government paused EU accession talks in Nov-24, followed by the foreign-agents law and Western measures against officials. Current status is not verified. (Press recollection, Low–Medium on direction, Low on current status)
- Market: a US$500m 2.75% bond due 22-Apr-26 is recalled. Its redemption and any replacement are unconfirmed. (Prior knowledge, Low)

### What would change the view
- Negative: sanctions or funding cuts that touch banks or public finances, a widening current account deficit, falling net FDI, or erosion of NBG independence.
- Positive: restored EU accession progress, or lifting of US and EU measures.
