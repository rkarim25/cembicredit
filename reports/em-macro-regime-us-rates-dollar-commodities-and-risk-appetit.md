## View
- Regime: an oil-supply shock (Hormuz) feeding US inflation, with the Fed in a hiking cycle. It hiked to 3.75–4.00% on 16-Sep-26, then held on 28-Oct-26 by 10–2, both dissents (Cook, Hammack) for a hike. A December hike is the market base case, reported at 70–85% (Medium).
- The 10y UST is about 4.9–5.0% (4.95% on 03-Nov-26), off the early-October peak of about 5.30%. A hike is largely priced (Medium).
- Brent is high but off the 16-Oct-26 settle of USD 109.86; the current level (USD 96–104) is unreconciled (Medium on direction, Low on level).
- The dollar is near 102 and has not been the marginal driver. Real rates and oil are (Medium-Low).
- Brazil is the main idiosyncratic shock. Lula won the 25-Oct-26 runoff against market expectations; the real fell more than 8% and the Ibovespa about 14% at the close, with the exact size unverified (Medium on outcome, Low on size).
- Dispersion is the implication: importers with thin buffers or negative real rates against exporters with terms-of-trade gains and high real carry (High on the cross-section, Medium on persistence).
- Biggest risk: a December hike with Brent sustained above USD 100, pushing the 10y back through 5% and widening spreads.
- Confidence: Medium on the regime, High on the cross-section, Low on current levels and EM spreads and flows.

## Analysis
### US rates and the Fed
- The FOMC held at 3.75–4.00% on 28-Oct-26 by 10–2. Cook and Hammack dissented in favour of a 25bp hike. The statement retained language that inflation remains elevated (investinglive.com, 28-Oct-26; Wolf Street, 28-Oct-26, Medium-High; Fed statement not read directly).
- Market pricing of a December hike was reported at 70% (up from 50%) and about 85% (Admiral Markets; babypips.com, 29-Oct-26, Medium, tier 3 sources that differ).
- The first hike was on 16-Sep-26, 12–0 (Federal Reserve release via Schwab, 16-Sep-26, Medium-High). September CPI was 3.7% y/y, core 2.6% (investinglive.com, 14-Oct-26, Medium). October CPI is due about 10-Nov-26 (date unconfirmed).
- Long end: the 10y was 4.951% on 03-Nov-26 and 4.92% on 04-Nov-26, with the 2y at 4.608% (CNBC, 03-Nov-26; Closing Look, 03-Nov-26, Medium), implying a 2s10s slope of about 33bp. It was 5.07% on 19-Oct-26 and 4.96% on 22-Oct-26, after about 5.30% in early October (FinanceFeeds, Oct-26; Gramercy, 03-Oct-26, Medium). A search snippet at 4.09% and others at 4.22–4.24% conflict with dated sources and are disregarded.
- Early-October data: September payrolls +29k against about 90k expected, unemployment 4.2%, August core PCE 3.0% y/y (Gramercy, 03-Oct-26, Medium).

### Dollar and EM FX
- DXY broke just below 102.00 on 01-Oct-26 from a double bottom near 98.50 (FXStreet, 07-Oct-26, Medium) and was about 102.15 after the 28-Oct-26 hold (babypips.com, 29-Oct-26, Medium-Low). A forex.com piece (Oct-26) says the index is near 2026 highs with December hike bets above 70%. The two differ by a point or two, so the level is imprecise (Low-Medium).
- A snippet quoting a broad dollar index of 121 is a different index and is not used.

### Commodities
- Brent settled at USD 109.86 on 16-Oct-26, the highest since May, and closed at USD 109.55 on 19-Oct-26. It was USD 90.1 at end-Aug-26 (Mitrade, 19-Oct-26; Aberdeen, Aug-26, Medium).
- Brent settled at USD 103.60 and WTI at USD 96.09 on 03-Nov-26 (babypips.com recap, Medium). Tradingeconomics showed USD 96.30 on 05-Nov-26, up 1.77% on the day; that implies a USD 9 one-day fall that cannot be reconciled (Low). A USD 123 snippet is disregarded.
- Hormuz flows averaged 11.62m b/d in the week to 13-Oct-26 against a 17.15m b/d baseline (FinanceFeeds, Oct-26, Medium). Tanker transits were reported at a two-month low on renewed US–Iran tension (devdiscourse, undated, Low).
- Exporters gain and importers lose per the structural map (knowledge/themes/commodities-and-em.md, Medium). Terms of trade, 12m to May-26 (macro monitor, 02-Nov-26, High on data, pre-dating the latest oil move): Kuwait +28, Angola +18, Iraq +17, Saudi Arabia +16, Qatar +15, Azerbaijan +14, Kazakhstan +12, Nigeria +5. Côte d'Ivoire −7, Jordan −6, Thailand −4, Kenya −3.
- Gold, copper and agricultural prices were not sourced.

### Brazil
- Lula won the 25-Oct-26 runoff 51.8–48.2, against prediction-market pricing for Flávio Bolsonaro (CNBC, 26-Oct-26, Medium).
- On 26-Oct-26 the real lost more than 8% and Ibovespa futures fell about 15%, touching nearly 20% at the worst point; interest rate futures rose 3.1% (MercoPress, 26-Oct-26, Medium). The Ibovespa closed down 13.9% (Rio Times, Oct-26, Low-Medium). Other tertiary outlets report the real down 11–12% to about 5.58 and the index down 17% (tftc.io, hngn.com, 26-Oct-26, Low). A B3 circuit breaker was triggered.
- Bolsonaro has said he will ask the courts to annul the result; his party recognised the win (newscord.org, 26-Oct-26, Low).
- Background: Selic 13.75% after five cuts; macro monitor 2026E fiscal balance −7.7% of GDP, gross debt 96%, real rate 9.2 (macro monitor, 02-Nov-26, High on data). Current agency ratings and outlooks were not retrieved.

### Cross-section (macro monitor, 02-Nov-26, tier 2)
- High real policy rates: Turkey 12.1, Brazil 9.2, Ghana 8.8, Nigeria 8.4, Uzbekistan 7.8, Kazakhstan 7.5, Angola 6.0, Colombia 5.7, Hungary 4.0, Serbia 3.5, Mexico 3.0.
- Negative real rates: Rwanda −7.0, Philippines −2.2 (CPI 7.2%, policy 5.00%), Thailand −1.5. CPI dates lag policy dates, so signs may change.
- Romania: CPI 6.2% (Aug-26) against policy 6.50%, current account −USD 32.2bn over four quarters, fiscal −6.2% of GDP, growth 0.7% in 2026; S&P BBB-/negative outlook (Agerpres, 03-Oct-26).
- Thin buffers:
  - Egypt: net-of-drains reserves USD 17.2bn against USD 53.6bn gross (Jul-26), fiscal −12.1%.
  - Pakistan: reserves USD 31.6bn, CPI 10.3%.
  - Kenya: reserves USD 14.7bn, fiscal −6.4%.
  - Turkey: net USD 137.1bn against USD 171.6bn gross, CA 4Q −USD 38.9bn.
  - Indonesia: net USD 74.5bn against USD 146.5bn gross.
- Deep buffers: India net USD 715.7bn, Saudi Arabia USD 480.8bn, Poland USD 267.4bn, Czech Republic USD 172.0bn.
- Central banks are diverging. The CBRT cut 100bp to 36% on 22-Oct-26, in line with consensus (Hürriyet Daily News, 22-Oct-26, Medium-High). SARB hiked 25bp to 7.25% on 24-Sep-26, and Banxico held 6.50% (knowledge base, Sep-26, Medium-High).

### Risk appetite and EM credit
- US equities are near highs: the S&P 500 closed at 7,568 on 03-Nov-26 (babypips.com; cappnotes, 03-Nov-26, Medium).
- EM debt: in August HY spreads tightened 13bp and IG 6bp, and EMBI GD returned +0.89% (Aberdeen, Aug-26, Medium). The week to 03-Oct-26 had hard-currency sovereigns −1.59%, local debt −1.11% and corporates −0.83% (Gramercy, Medium).
- Spread levels and flows after August are unsourced. Search results on a USD 5bn outflow and a 337bp EMBI spread look like 2024 material and were excluded.
- IMF WEO Oct-26: emerging and developing economies 3.7% in 2026 against 4.5% in 2025, China 4.5%, with higher oil named as a headwind (The Week, 13-Oct-26, Medium; secondary report of a tier 2 source).
