## View
- Egypt is an improving single-B credit with a heavy interest burden. Hold as a carry credit and do not add duration before the Eighth Review (about mid-Dec-26). Medium confidence.
- Moody's upgraded Egypt to B3 from Caa1 on 16-Oct-26 and kept the outlook Positive. This replaces the "Caa1/Positive" line in the previous version. S&P is B/Stable (11-Oct-26) and Fitch is B/Stable. All three are known through press only (Medium).
- Drivers: a primary surplus of 4.9% of GDP in FY25/26 (Moody's and S&P), public debt of 81.8% of GDP at Jun-26 (95.2% in 2023), a flexible FX rate since Mar-24 and higher reserves (Moody's via press, Medium).
- Disinflation is slow. Sep-26 headline CPI was 12.8% y/y and the CBE deposit rate is 19.00%, so the ex-post real rate is about 6.2pp. The next MPC is on 12-Nov-26.
- The IMF anchor holds through the Eighth and final EFF review. No successor arrangement is documented, which makes it the key event risk.
- Biggest risk: hot money and the interest burden. Interest takes 59% (Moody's) to 67% (S&P) of revenue. Foreign holdings of local securities are $34.5B, after $9.5B left in the recent shock. Reserves net of one-year drains were $17.2B at May-26.
- No market levels were supplied, so there is no relative-value call.

## Analysis

### Ratings
- Moody's: B3/Positive, upgraded from Caa1 on 16-Oct-26 (Daba Finance, 16-Oct-26; The National, 17-Oct-26; Medium). The stated drivers are fiscal consolidation, lower debt and higher reserves, and the policy shift since Mar-24. The release was not read.
- S&P: B/Stable, affirmed 11-Oct-26 per press. Upgrade trigger: faster debt reduction via FDI and asset sales. Downgrade triggers: less FX flexibility, renewed FX shortages or rising interest costs (Arab News, 11-Oct-26, Medium).
- Fitch: B/Stable. Press reports it affirmed after Aug-26 and cites FY26/27 growth of 4.7% and a current account deficit below 3.5% of GDP by FY27/28 (Egypt Today, Oct-26, Medium). The date is unverified (Low).
- Eurobonds listed: 7.600% 01-Mar-29 ($2.0B), 9.450% 04-Feb-33 ($1.5B), 6.875% 30-Apr-40 ($1.25B), 8.500% 31-Jan-47 ($2.5B) (LuxSE / MoF, Oct-26, Medium). The ladder is incomplete. A $1.5B sukuk was priced in Oct-25 at 6.375% for 2029 and 7.95% for 2032 (Ecofin, undated, Low). The Cabinet approved $3B of international issuance for FY26/27 (Arab Finance, Medium).

### Fiscal and debt
- The primary surplus was 3.5% of GDP in FY24/25. It was 4.9% in FY25/26 per Moody's and S&P, against 4.0% budgeted by the MoF (Medium). The outturn confirms over-performance. The final MoF accounts were not retrieved.
- Public debt was 81.8% of GDP at Jun-26 per Moody's (Medium). The repo's MoF/IIF figure is 79.7% at Q2-26 for budget-sector debt. The definitions differ.
- Interest is 59% of revenue (Moody's) or 67% (S&P). Either is well above the 30% level at which defaults cluster (framework).
- The IMF WEO 2026E overall deficit is -12.1% of GDP, against S&P's 5.8% (Arab News, 11-Oct-26). The perimeters differ and are unreconciled.
- The debt-stabilising primary balance cannot be computed without the effective interest rate on debt (data gap).

### External position
- CBE net reserves were $57.35B at end-Sep-26 with import cover over 7.5 months (CBE, 07-Oct-26, Medium). IMF gross reserves incl. gold were $53.559B at Jul-26, up 14.7% y/y (IMF IL, High). S&P cites $54.5B at Sep-26. The definitions differ.
- Reserves ex-gold rose from $28.62B (Feb-26) to $36.42B (Jul-26). Gold fell from $21.502B to $17.139B (IMF IL, High).
- Reserves net of one-year drains were $17.224B at May-26, up from $5.8B in Dec-25 (IMF template, High).
- The current account deficit over the last four quarters was $16.76B (IMF BOP, High). The IMF WEO 2026E is -4.2% of GDP and S&P puts it at 5.1% (Medium). Fuel and gas are about 30% of goods imports (S&P via press, Medium).
- Foreign holdings of local securities were $34.5B at Sep-26, up from $22.2B in April, after $9.5B left in the shock (S&P via press, Medium).
- Suez receipts conflict between sources (open item 8).

### Monetary
- The CBE held the deposit rate at 19.00% and the lending rate at 20.00% on 24-Sep-26, unchanged since the February cut (CBE via press, Medium). The next MPC is on 12-Nov-26.
- Sep-26 headline CPI was 12.8% y/y (12.7% in Aug), urban 13.9% (14.5%) and core 14.1% (14.9%) (CAPMAS, CBE via press, Medium). S&P expects average FY26/27 inflation of 12.9% (Medium). The data layer's 14.539% for Aug-26 is the urban series.
- USD/EGP was 47.15 in Feb-26, 51.95 in Mar-26 and 50.25 in Jul-26 (BIS, High). The parallel premium is reported at 0% (Medium).

### IMF and official sector
- The Board completed the Seventh Review with $1.8B approved ($1.5B EFF, $272M RSF), dated 31-Jul-26 in the IMF release (High). A staff-level agreement was announced on 30-Jun-26 (Medium).
- The Eighth and final EFF review, with the third RSF review, is due in Q4-26 and expected by mid-December (press, Medium). The date of 15-Dec-26 is unconfirmed. No successor arrangement is documented.

## Where the models disagreed
1. Moody's rating: the repo and the previous version said Caa1/Positive. The position found the B3 upgrade of 16-Oct-26, confirmed by several outlets. The more recent and specific source wins at Medium confidence, with a primary check requested (item 5).
2. Interest/revenue: 50%+ (MoF/IIF lower bound), 59% (Moody's) and 67% (S&P). All are tier 3 and unreconciled, so all are shown and the definition is asked of management (item 10). No averaging was applied.
3. Fitch date: the repo says Oct-26 and the position found only a 2025 release. Press implies a post-Aug-26 affirmation, so the rating is kept and the date flagged (item 5).
4. Suez receipts: both sources are tier 3 or 4 and conflict, so this is an open item (item 8).
