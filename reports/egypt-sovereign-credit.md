## View
- Egypt is an improving single-B credit with a heavy interest burden. Hold as a carry credit and do not add duration before the Eighth Review closes (target mid-Dec-26). Medium confidence.
- Ratings: Moody's B3/Positive (upgraded from Caa1, 16-Oct-26), S&P B/Stable, Fitch B/Stable. All three are known through press only (Medium).
- Drivers: a primary surplus of 4.9% of GDP in FY25/26, public debt of 81.8% of GDP at Jun-26 (95.2% in 2023), the flexible FX rate and higher reserves (Moody's via press, Medium).
- The IMF anchor is intact. A mission was in Cairo in the week of 29-Oct-26 and the IMF is targeting a Board date by mid-Dec-26. The EFF expires on 15-Dec-26 and no successor arrangement is documented, so the end of the programme is the key event risk.
- Disinflation is slow. Sep-26 headline CPI was 12.8% y/y against a deposit rate of 19.00%, an ex-post real rate of about 6.2pp. The MPC meets on 12-Nov-26 and a hold looks more likely than a cut (Low-Medium, no analyst poll found).
- Biggest risk: the interest burden (59% to 67% of revenue) combined with hot money. Foreign holdings of local securities were $34.5B at Sep-26 (up from $22.2B in Apr-26, after $9.5B left in the shock), against reserves net of one-year drains of $17.2B at May-26. Brent was about $112 on 30-Oct-26 and Egypt is a net energy importer.
- No market levels were supplied, so there is no relative-value call.

## What changed
- The IMF Eighth Review is now more specific. A mission was in Cairo in the week of 29-Oct-26 and the IMF is targeting a Board date by mid-Dec-26. The EFF expires on 15-Dec-26 and a final disbursement of about $2.3B is reported (The National, 29-Oct-26, Medium). The mission is focused on tight monetary policy, the fiscal plan and faster divestment.
- Divestment is now identified as the weakest reform item and the gating item for the final review. It is also S&P's upgrade trigger.
- Oil context is added: Brent was about $112 on 30-Oct-26, and S&P puts fuel and gas at about 30% of Egyptian goods imports.
- A conflict is recorded on the S&P affirmation date: 8-Oct-26 in a search summary against 11-Oct-26 in the repo.
- Open items were consolidated from 11 to 10. The IMF item (6) now covers the staff-level agreement. The 15-Dec-26 date item is folded into it.
- The rest is unchanged: the ratings, the fiscal figures and the unreconciled interest/revenue, reserves and Suez conflicts.

## Analysis

### Ratings
- Moody's: B3/Positive, upgraded from Caa1 on 16-Oct-26 (Daba Finance, 16-Oct-26; Gulf Daily News, Oct-26; Medium). It has the only Positive outlook of the three agencies. The release was not read.
- S&P: B/Stable. The affirmation is dated 11-Oct-26 in the repo (Arab News, 11-Oct-26) and 8-Oct-26 in a search summary (Low on the date). Upgrade trigger: faster debt reduction via FDI and asset sales. Downgrade triggers: less FX flexibility, renewed FX shortages or rising interest costs (Medium).
- Fitch: B/Stable. Press cites FY26/27 growth of 4.7% and a current account deficit below 3.5% of GDP by FY27/28 (Egypt Today, Oct-26, Medium). The affirmation date is unverified.
- Eurobonds listed: 7.600% 01-Mar-29 ($2.0B), 9.450% 04-Feb-33 ($1.5B), 6.875% 30-Apr-40 ($1.25B), 8.500% 31-Jan-47 ($2.5B) (LuxSE / MoF, Oct-26, Medium). The ladder is incomplete. The Cabinet approved $3B of international issuance for FY26/27 (Arab Finance, Medium).

### Fiscal and debt
- The primary surplus was 3.5% of GDP in FY24/25 and 4.9% in FY25/26 per Moody's and S&P, against 4.0% budgeted by the MoF (Medium). The final MoF accounts were not retrieved.
- Public debt was 81.8% of GDP at Jun-26 per Moody's (Medium), 13.4pp below 2023. The repo's MoF/IIF figure is 79.7% for budget-sector debt at Q2-26. The definitions differ.
- Interest is 59% of revenue (Moody's) or 67% (S&P). Tax revenue is 13% of GDP (Moody's). Either interest figure is well above the 30% level at which defaults cluster (framework). The MoF/IIF "more than 50%" is only a lower bound.
- The IMF WEO 2026E overall deficit is -12.1% of GDP, against S&P's overall deficit of 5.8%. The perimeters differ and are unreconciled.
- The debt-stabilising primary balance cannot be computed without the effective interest rate on debt (data gap).

### External position
- CBE net reserves were $57.35B at end-Sep-26 with import cover over 7.5 months (CBE, 07-Oct-26, Medium). IMF gross reserves incl. gold were $54.4B in Aug-26 and $53.559B in Jul-26 (IMF IL, High). S&P cites $54.5B at Sep-26. The definitions differ.
- Reserves ex-gold rose from $28.62B (Feb-26) to $36.42B (Jul-26). Gold fell from $21.502B to $17.139B (IMF IL, High).
- Reserves net of one-year drains were $17.224B at May-26, up from $5.8B in Dec-25 (IMF template, High).
- The current account deficit over the last four quarters to 1Q26 was $16.76B (IMF BOP, High). The IMF WEO 2026E is -4.2% of GDP and S&P puts it at 5.1% (Medium).
- Foreign holdings of local securities were $34.5B at Sep-26, up from $22.2B in Apr-26, after $9.5B left in the shock (S&P via press, Medium). This is the portion of the reserve buffer most exposed to reversal.
- Suez receipts conflict between sources (item 7). Moody's lists Suez recovery as a monitoring factor.

### Monetary
- The CBE held the deposit rate at 19.00% and the lending rate at 20.00% on 24-Sep-26 (CBE via press, Medium). Press reports a fourth consecutive hold in Aug-26 (Enterprise, 23-Aug-26, Medium).
- Sep-26 headline CPI was 12.8% y/y (12.7% in Aug) and 1.5% m/m, urban 13.9% (14.5%) and core 14.1% (14.9%) (CAPMAS, CBE via press, Medium). The data layer's 14.539% for Aug-26 is the urban series.
- A headline of an Ahram piece says the CBE expects inflation to rise in 3Q before easing in 2027. The page was not retrievable (Low). An IMF Oct-26 inflation path of 9.4% by end-2026 is inconsistent with the Sep print and is unverified (Low).
- USD/EGP was 47.15 in Feb-26, 51.95 in Mar-26 and 50.25 in Jul-26 (BIS, High). The parallel premium is reported at 0% (Medium).

### IMF and official sector
- The Board completed the Seventh Review with $1.8B approved ($1.5B EFF, $272M RSF), dated 31-Jul-26 in the IMF release (High).
- The Eighth and final EFF review, with the third RSF review, is due in Q4-26. The IMF is targeting a Board date by mid-Dec-26, the EFF expires on 15-Dec-26, and purchases to date are $7.3B (The National, 29-Oct-26, Medium). The 15-Dec-26 Board date has no primary source.
- A staff-level agreement had not been located at the time of writing. If none is announced by about 20-Nov-26, or an extension is requested, the closing timetable is at risk.
- No successor arrangement is documented.

### External stress
- Brent was 112.44 on 30-Oct-26, up 13% in October (Trading Economics, 30-Oct-26, Medium). S&P (28-Apr-26) named Egypt among the most exposed net energy importers. Fuel and gas are about 30% of goods imports (S&P via press, Medium).
