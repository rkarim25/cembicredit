## View
- Egypt's external liquidity has recovered and the IMF programme is on track, but the debt burden is still heavy. Hold as a carry credit and do not add duration before the Eighth Review (15-Dec-26). Medium confidence.
- Liquidity: CBE net reserves are $57.35B at end-Sep-26 (CBE, 07-Oct-26, Medium). IMF gross reserves incl. gold are $53.559B at Jul-26, up 14.7% in 12 months (IMF IL, Jul-26, High). The parallel FX premium is reported as eliminated (CBE, Oct-26, Medium).
- Programme: the Seventh Review of the $8B EFF was completed on 31-Jul-26 with $1.8B approved (IMF, 31-Jul-26, High).
- Fiscal: the primary surplus was 3.5% of GDP in FY24/25 and is budgeted at 4.0% for FY25/26. Debt is about 79.7% of GDP, but interest takes more than 50% of revenue, and the IMF WEO puts the 2026E overall deficit at -12.1% of GDP (MoF, Sep-26; IMF WEO, Medium).
- Ratings: S&P B/Stable, Fitch B/Stable, Moody's Caa1/Positive. Moody's has been on Positive since 18-Mar-24, so an upgrade is the key rating catalyst (Rating Agencies, Oct-26, Medium).
- Biggest risk: slippage once the EFF ends in Dec-26, with a high interest burden and little room for error. Suez receipts are down 50–60% and the 2026E current account is -4.2% of GDP (Suez Canal Authority, Sep-26; IMF WEO, Medium).
- The USD/EGP move from 47.15 (Feb-26) to 51.95 (Mar-26) shows how exposed the currency is to shocks (BIS, High).
- No market levels were supplied and no new web search was possible in this run, so there is no relative-value call.

## Analysis

### External position
- CBE net international reserves are $57.35B at end-Sep-26, with import cover of over 7.5 months (CBE, 07-Oct-26, Medium). The definition and date differ from the IMF series, so the two are not comparable (see open item 4).
- IMF gross reserves incl. gold were $50.122B in Feb-26 and $53.559B in Jul-26 (IMF IL, Jul-26, High). Over the same months, reserves ex-gold rose from $28.62B to $36.42B and gold fell from $21.502B to $17.139B (IMF data portal, Jul-26, High).
- The cause of the gold decline (price, sales or swaps) cannot be separated from the data (Low, inferred).
- The IMF WEO 2026E current account is -4.2% of GDP, with FDI at 4.231% of GDP in 2025 (IMF WEO; World Bank WDI, Medium).
- Suez Canal receipts are reported down 50–60% YoY, an estimated $5–6B a year (Suez Canal Authority, Sep-26, Medium).
- The reported parallel FX premium is 0% since Mar-24 (CBE / commercial banks, Oct-26, Medium).
- The commodity terms-of-trade index is little changed: -0.7% over 12 months to May-26 (IMF CTOT, Medium).

### Monetary and FX
- The CBE held the overnight deposit rate at 19.00% and the lending rate at 20.00% on 24-Sep-26 (CBE, 24-Sep-26, High). The next MPC is on 12-Nov-26 (CBE, Oct-26, Medium).
- August 2026 CPI was 12.7% nationwide and 14.5% urban (CAPMAS, 10-Sep-26, High). Jul-26 urban CPI was 14.87%, so the urban rate is broadly stable (IMF data portal, Medium).
- The real policy rate is therefore 4.5pp (against urban) to 6.3pp (against headline) ex-post. Expectations are unsourced.
- The EGP weakened about 10% in March 2026 and has since recovered to 50.25 in Jul-26 (BIS, High).
- The drivers of the March move are not documented in the brief (Low).

### Fiscal and debt
- The primary surplus was 3.5% of GDP (EGP 629B) in FY24/25 and is budgeted at 4.0% for FY25/26 (MoF, Sep-26, Medium).
- Debt is about 79.7% of GDP at Q2-26, down from 96.4% in FY23/24. The source mixes MoF and IIF figures, and the perimeter is budget-sector only (MoF / IIF, Jun-26, Medium).
- Interest takes more than 50% of revenue, far above the 30% level at which defaults cluster in the framework (MoF / IIF, Jun-26, Medium).
- The IMF WEO 2026E fiscal balance is -12.1% of GDP, against real growth of 4.2% and nominal GDP of $429.6B (IMF WEO, Medium).
- The debt-stabilising primary balance cannot be computed without the effective interest rate on debt (data gap).

### IMF and official sector
- The Seventh Review was completed on 31-Jul-26, with $1.8B approved ($1.5B EFF, $272M RSF) (IMF, 31-Jul-26, High).
- The Eighth and final review is scheduled for 15-Dec-26 (IMF, Sep-26, Medium). No successor arrangement is documented.
- The previous version reported progress on performance criteria and a gradual divestment pipeline. These were not re-verified (Medium).

### Ratings and market
- S&P is B/Stable (10-Oct-25, affirmed Apr-26). Fitch is B/Stable (01-Nov-24, reported affirmed Oct-26). Moody's is Caa1/Positive (18-Mar-24, affirmed Apr-26) (Rating Agencies, Oct-26, Medium).
- A Fitch affirmation within eight days of this run needs confirmation (see open item 9).
- Benchmark Eurobonds listed are 7.600% 01-Mar-29 ($2.0B), 9.450% 04-Feb-33 ($1.5B), 6.875% 30-Apr-40 ($1.25B) and 8.500% 31-Jan-47 ($2.5B) (LuxSE / MoF, Oct-26, Medium). The ladder is incomplete.
- No market levels are available.
