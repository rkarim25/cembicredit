## View
- **Operations are strong. Jurisdiction is the problem.** Pearl is a very high-margin KRI gas producer: FY24 EBITDA was $521M at an 81% margin (High). The credit is held back by KRG/Baghdad contract risk, physical security and the 2028 maturities.
- **Biggest near-term risk:** the KRG breach allegation (Aug-26) over KM250 gas sold to the Federal MoE. It lands on top of a demonstrated force majeure record: the OMV-reported halt and the Jul-25 drone strikes.
- **Leverage depends on which cash figure you use.** On consolidated cash it is net cash. On cash held at Pearl ($95M, 1Q26) it is about 1.0x net. Until reconciled (item 5), use the conservative figure.
- **The $508M FY24 "FCF" is not after-coupon FCF.** It leaves $13M below EBITDA, while the 13% coupon on $350M alone is about $45.5M a year. True conversion is unknown.
- **Refinancing:** the $350M bond (15-May-28) and the floating facility (08-Sep-28) fall due within four months of each other. The issuer is unrated, and the outcome of the Apr-25 mandate is unconfirmed.
- **Relative value:** at the captured 8.31% / 392 bp the bond is not cheap for unrated, single-jurisdiction risk with a live KRG dispute, so do not add. Re-assess if the live level is near the repo's 11.45%.
- **Confidence: Medium-Low.** Operating quality is High. Pricing, leverage basis and post-Q1-25 numbers are unverified.

## Analysis

### Issuer and rating
- Pearl is the KRI upstream gas and liquids JV that runs Khor Mor and Chemchemal. Dana Gas and Crescent Petroleum operate it, and OMV holds a minority stake (capture, 07-Oct-26, High).
- MOL is also a shareholder (gemini, unsourced, Low). The claim that RWE exited (gemini, unsourced, Low) is not relied on.
- It has no public ratings. Fitch withdrew in Apr-20, and Moody's and S&P have never rated it (capture, High).
- The repo's "B- (Implied) / CCC+" is an internal judgment, not an agency rating (repo pearl.json, Low).
- The "Oslo listing (PPCL)" most likely refers to the bond listing, not equity (both models, Low). This does not affect the credit.

### Operations and earnings
- **Earnings:**
  - FY24: revenue $642M, EBITDA $521M, about 81% margin (capture/BBG, High).
  - Q1-25: revenue $166M, EBITDA $138M, about 83% margin (capture/BBG, High).
  - Q1-25 production was 126.2 kboepd at a realized price of $14.61/boe (capture/BBG, High).
  - The low realized price reflects fixed or formula KRG gas pricing. This caps upside and exposes Pearl to renegotiation (capture, Medium).
- **Lifting cost:** below $2.50/boe per repo commentary (repo pearl.json 2021A note, Low). It is not material to the view.
- **KM250:**
  - Supply of 100 MMscf/d to Iraq's Federal Ministry of Electricity began after Q2-26 under a one-year agreement. This is the first commercial use of KM250 capacity (FAB Securities via capture, 12-Aug-26, Medium).
  - Up to $150M of incremental annual revenue at full ramp (FAB, 13-Mar-26, Medium).
- **Chemchemal:**
  - A 10-year deal for up to 142 MMscf/d to industrial customers in Erbil and Bazian, starting 2H-27 (Bloomberg First Word via capture, 26-Jan-26, Medium).
  - A $160M appraisal and early-development programme, funded internally (FAB, 12-Aug-26, Medium).
- **Collections:** KRI collection was 94–100% across 2025 quarters and 96–100% for 1H–9M25 (FAB, 10-Nov-25, Medium). Nothing covers 2026, when the KRG dispute began. The repo notes a KRG receivables build in 2022–23, so this has reversed before (repo, Medium).

### Balance sheet, cash and FCF
- **Gross debt:** $649M at FY24 and $604M at Q1-25. Consolidated cash was $672M and $612M (capture/BBG, High). On those figures Pearl was net cash of about $23M and $8M.
- **Cash held at Pearl:** $107M at 4Q25 and $95M at 1Q26 (Dana Gas disclosures via capture, High). This matches the repo's 1H26 unrestricted cash of $95M. On that basis net debt is about $500M, or about 1.0x LTM EBITDA (repo: total debt $595M, net leverage 0.98x, Medium).
- **The cash gap is not yet explained:**
  - The consolidated and entity cash figures are for different dates (Q1-25 against 1Q26). Part of the gap may be spending or distributions over that year rather than structurally trapped cash.
  - "Trapped" is an inference (Low) until item 5 is answered.
- **FCF:**
  - The $508M FY24 FCF (97% of EBITDA) cannot be after interest, because EBITDA less FCF is $13M and the coupon alone is about $45.5M. It is likely a pre-capex and/or pre-interest measure (Medium).
  - The repo's $165M FY24 FCF sits on a repo EBITDA of $415M, which conflicts with the tier 1 $521M. It is not adopted as true conversion (Low).
- **Repo data quality:** repo pearl.json FY24 figures (revenue $510M, EBITDA $415M, net leverage 1.15x) conflict with the tier 1 capture. Tier 1 prevails, and the repo model needs updating.

### Debt and refinancing
- **Instruments:**
  - PEAPET 13% senior secured: $350M, due 15-May-28, ISIN NO0013387860 (capture/BBG, High).
  - BL370608 Corp floating: due 08-Sep-28, size not stated (capture/BBG, High on maturity).
  - The other roughly $250M of gross debt is probably DFC debt (the repo cites DFC drawdowns for KM250 in 2023, Medium). Gemini's "$250M 7-year DFC loan, Sep-21" is unsourced (Low).
- **Refinancing:**
  - DNB and Pareto were mandated on 03-Apr-25 for a potential 5Y senior secured bond (Bloomberg First Word, 03-Apr-25, Medium).
  - No outcome is in evidence. This is unconfirmed, not a failed deal.
- **Indenture:** covenants, restricted payments, cross-default and change-of-control terms are all unknown.

### Market pricing
- **Working level:** 8.31% yield, about 392 bp (capture, tier 1, but the capture date of the price is not stated). Tier 1 beats the repo.
- **Repo level:** 103.5 / 11.45% / 625 bp (repo, 07-Oct-26). A roughly 19-month 13% coupon at 103.5 approximates the repo yield. The 8.31% level would imply a price several points higher (judge approximation, Low).
- Both cannot be live, and the relative-value view depends on which is (item 1).

### Political, security and sovereign
- **KRG dispute:** the KRG alleged breach over the Federal MoE supply. Pearl says it still meets its 655 MMscf/d KRG obligation (MT Newswires, 09-Aug-26, Medium).
- **Iran-related halt:** OMV's annual report shows its 15 kboe/d via Pearl stopped because of the Iran conflict (Bloomberg First Word, 02-Apr-26, High on the event). 02-Apr-26 is the disclosure date, not the date of the halt. Duration and recovery are unknown (Low).
- **Drone strikes:** drones shut multiple KRI oil sites (AP, 16-Jul-25, High).
- **Kirkuk–Ceyhan pipeline:** throughput was 40–60k b/d against a 300k b/d target (Bloomberg First Word, 03-Aug-26, High). Pearl's gas revenue is domestic, so the exposure is indirect.
- **Baghdad payment plan:** the Iraqi parliament approved $16/bbl for KRI oil production and transport (Bloomberg News, 02-Feb-25, Medium). This is supportive background for Baghdad–Erbil normalisation.
