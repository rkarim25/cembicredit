## View
- **Neutral on the 13.00% 2028 notes, with the cautious bias removed from the leverage side but not the operating side.** The repo's "Overweight / Secured Carry" rests on stale figures.
- **Leverage is lower than the repo shows.** Pearl's Q1-26 update shows cash of $270.6M, debt of $574M and net debt of $303M. That is about 0.6x on the $510M LTM EBITDA (my arithmetic). The repo shows $500M net debt and 0.98x. $86.6M of the cash is in DSRA (Pearl Q1-26 update, 14-May-26, as relayed by one position, Medium).
- **Q1-26 EBITDA of $265.6M is flattered by a one-off.** It includes $137.5M of KRG back-billing. Excluding it, EBITDA is about $128M a quarter, in line with 1Q25's $137.7M (Medium).
- **Operations remain the weak point.** Khor Mor stopped three times in about eight months: 26-Nov-25, 28-Feb to 13-Apr-26 and 16-Jul to 27-Jul-26 (press, Medium-High).
- **Collections are contested.** The 1H26 KRI collection rate was about 78% per Dana Gas H1-26, against a 100% rate in 1Q26 per press. Pearl says the KRG has been in arrears for over three years.
- **Kirkuk sales** (100 MMscf/d since 04-Aug-26) add a federal payer but triggered a KRG breach claim. Terms and payment record are unverified.
- **Biggest risk:** a long or repeated shut-in while arrears persist, under bond and DFC terms we have not seen. The 15-Nov-26 coupon ($22.75M) is small against Q1 cash. The May-28 bullet refinancing is the structural issue.
- **Pricing is unresolved (item 1).** Do not add until it is.
- **Confidence: Medium-Low.** No Pearl 2Q or 3Q data, no documentation and no live price. A web search this run found no Pearl or Dana Gas news after Aug-26.

## Analysis

### Financials and liquidity
- **Q1-26 (quarter to 31-Mar-26):** revenue $307.9M and EBITDA $265.6M, including $137.5M of additional KRG gas billing for Nov-18 to Mar-24, stated net of prior provisions and on management's recovery estimate. Excluding it, revenue is described as comparable to 1Q25 (revenue $165.9M, EBITDA $137.7M). Production was 125.1k boed against 126.2k a year earlier (Pearl Q1-26 update, 14-May-26, Medium; the PDF was read by one position only).
- **Balance sheet at 31-Mar-26:** cash $271M, of which $86.6M in DSRA; debt $574M; net debt $303M (same source, Medium).
- **Basis of the $95M:** Dana Gas reports "Pearl cash" of $95M at 30-Jun-26. 35% of $270.6M is $94.7M, but that compares June with March, so it is a coincidence until confirmed (Low, inference). If $95M is Dana's share, the repo's net debt of $500M is overstated. If it is a 100% figure, cash fell by about $175M in a quarter, which would need dividends or capex to explain.
- **Dividends:** Pearl paid $61M to Dana Gas in 1H26 (Dana Gas H1-26, 07-Aug-26, Medium). The repo cites $105M each to Dana Gas and Crescent, with no 2026 source.
- **Tier 1 history (07-Oct-26 capture):** FY24 revenue $642M and EBITDA $521M. Q1-25 revenue $166M and EBITDA $138M (High). The repo's 2024A EBITDA of $415M and 2025A of $470M conflict with these and are treated as stale.
- **Coupon burden:** about $45.5M a year ($22.75M semi-annual) (Medium).
- **Chemchemal:** Dana Gas describes a $150M gross programme, revised from $160M, with the first well expected around the turn of the year (Dana Gas H1-26 call transcript, Aug-26, Medium). It competes with dividends and debt service for cash.

### Shut-ins and operations
- **26-Nov-25:** rocket strike on a storage tank, cutting about 3,000 MW of Kurdistan power (Oman Observer, 27-Nov-25, Medium-High).
- **28-Feb to 13-Apr-26:** precautionary suspension on the US/Israel–Iran conflict, about 45 days (Offshore Technology, Mar-26; MEES, 17-Apr-26; PUKmedia, 13-Apr-26, Medium-High).
- **16-Jul to 27/28-Jul-26:** suspension, restored after assurances from Erbil and Baghdad (Peregraf, 27-Jul-26, Medium).
- **KM250:** commissioned, taking capacity to 750 MMscf/d. Dana Gas said utilisation was constrained by regional disruption (Zawya, Aug-26, Medium).
- **Force majeure:** the Q1-26 update refers to force majeure from 28-Feb-26. Whether it was declared under the notes or DFC facility is unconfirmed (Low).

### Kirkuk gas sales
- Deliveries of 100 MMscf/d to the Taza plant began 04-Aug-26 under a one-year agreement with the federal Ministry of Electricity, additional to 655 MMscf/d to the KRG (Iraq Oil Report, Aug-26, Medium-High).
- The KRG alleged breach on 06-Aug-26 and said sales need Erbil's approval. Pearl rejected this on 07-Aug-26, saying Erbil received the agreement in Jan-26 and the volumes use excess KM250 capacity (Rudaw; MEES, 07-Aug-26; Iraq Business News, 07-Aug-26; Peregraf, 07-Aug-26, Medium).
- Dana Gas declined to disclose pricing. No settlement currency, payer record, take-or-pay terms or litigation was found (Data gap).

### Collections and counterparty
- **Dana Gas H1-26 (07-Aug-26):** KRI collection rate 78%, collections of $104M, management calling recent receipts "encouraging" (Medium).
- **Dana Gas 1Q26 press:** KRI receipts of $60M, a 100% collection rate and an $18M advance linked to direct condensate and LPG sales (Zawya, Medium).
- **Repo log:** $68M in 1Q26 and $100M in 2Q26. These conflict with the $60M press figure. The bases (current billing, arrears, advances) may differ, but this is not verified, and neither is adopted (item 4).
- **2025 rates of 94–100%** (FAB, 10-Nov-25, Medium) are above the 1H26 figure.
- **Arrears:** Pearl says the KRG has been in material arrears for more than three years (Peregraf, 07-Aug-26, Medium). The receivable stock is undisclosed.

### Debt, documentation and pricing
- **Notes:** $350M 13.00% senior secured, due 15-May-28, ISIN NO0013387860, issued at 100 with a 3.5-year tenor, unrated (Zawya; bond data pages, Medium).
- **Other debt:** a floating facility, BL370608 Corp, matures 08-Sep-28 (07-Oct-26 capture, High on maturity). The repo cites a $250M U.S. DFC facility. The link between the two is unconfirmed.
- **Not accessed:** covenants, restricted payments, DSRA mechanics, call schedule, the 101% change-of-control put (per the repo) and intercreditor terms.
- **Rating:** no public agency rating; Fitch withdrew in Apr-20. "B- (Implied) / CCC+" is internal (Medium).
- **Pricing:** repo 103.5 / 11.45% / 625 bp (07-Oct-26); capture 8.31% / 392 bp; an aggregator page shows 8.70% YTM, undated (Low). None is verified.

Resolved in favour of the Q1-26 filing as the higher-tier, entity-level source, with the $95M basis left as item 3 (evidence hierarchy: tier 2 primary beats a relayed figure).
2. **Stance.** The position leaned neutral to modestly constructive. Resolved as neutral because nothing sourced supports an upgrade given the outages, the collection conflict and the unseen documentation (rule 2).
3. **Collections.** 100% in 1Q26 against 78% in 1H26. Both rest on Dana Gas results relayed by press, so no winner was picked (item 4).
4. **Price.** The repo, the capture and the aggregator conflict, and nothing is above tier 3 on the live level, so it is open (item 1).
