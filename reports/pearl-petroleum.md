## View
- **Operations are strong, but the "de-risked" thesis in the repo is out of date.** Khor Mor was shut twice in 2026 on the Iran conflict: about 45 days from 28-Feb-26 and about 11 days from 16-Jul-26 (company statements via press, Medium-High).
- **View: neutral to cautious, not Overweight.** The repo's "Overweight / Secured Carry" was written without the outages.
- **Leverage is low, but the basis is unverified.** About 1.0x net on Pearl-entity cash ($95M), or net cash on consolidated cash. The ~$510M LTM EBITDA behind 0.98x has not been reconciled to the outages. FY24 EBITDA of $521M and Q1-25 of $138M are the last tier 1 figures.
- **Counterparty risk persists.** KRI collection was about 78% in 1H26. Dana collected $68M in 1Q26 and $100M in 2Q26. Pearl says the KRG has been in material arrears for over three years.
- **Kirkuk sales route:** the 100 MMscf/d to the Federal MoE (from Aug-26) diversifies the payer. It also brings a KRG breach allegation (06-Aug-26) and an unverified payment channel.
- **Biggest risk:** a longer or repeated shut-in while arrears persist, hitting cash before the May-28 bullet under unseen bond and DFC terms.
- **Pricing:** 103.5 / 11.45% (repo) and 8.31% (capture) conflict and neither is dated. Do not add until item 1 is resolved.
- **Confidence: Medium-Low.** Outage dates are consistent across sources. Pearl-level 2026 financials, bond terms and live pricing are not in hand.

## Analysis

### Shut-ins and operations
- **First shut-in:** production was suspended from 28-Feb-26 as a precaution, coordinated with the KRG ministries, because of the US/Israel–Iran conflict. The suspension was announced 05-Mar-26 for more than 700 MMscf/d and 2.5–3 GW of power. Full resumption was announced 13-Apr-26 after intermittent operations (Offshore Technology, Mar-26; MEES, 17-Apr-26; PUKmedia, 13-Apr-26, Medium-High). About 45 days.
- **Second shut-in:** 16-Jul-26 to 27/28-Jul-26, about 11 days. Production was restored "after guarantees from senior authorities in Erbil and Baghdad" (Peregraf, 27-Jul-26, Medium-High).
- **Force majeure attribution is unclear.** One search summary tied the force majeure to Enerflex, which is the Apr-24 event. Whether Pearl or its lenders treated the 2026 events as force majeure is unconfirmed (Low).
- **KM250:** capacity of 750 MMscf/d was reported commissioned in the repo. Dana Gas H1-26 said the capacity was underutilised and regional disruption constrained utilisation (Zawya, Aug-26, Medium).
- **Kirkuk gas:**
  - 100 MMscf/d to the Federal MoE began in Aug-26, additional to 655 MMscf/d to the KRG.
  - The KRG alleged breach on 06-Aug-26. Pearl rejected this on 07-Aug-26, citing prior international legal proceedings on marketing rights (Peregraf, 07-Aug-26, Medium).
  - No filed litigation or payment terms were found.

### Earnings and cash
- **Last tier 1 earnings (07-Oct-26 capture):** FY24 revenue $642M, EBITDA $521M, about 81% margin. Q1-25 revenue $166M, EBITDA $138M (High).
- **Repo 1H26:** gross debt $595M, cash $95M, net debt $500M, LTM EBITDA $510M, net leverage 0.98x (repo, 07-Oct-26, Medium). 2025A EBITDA is $470M in the repo, which conflicts with the tier 1 FY24 $521M, so the repo series is treated as stale.
- **No 2026 Pearl-level EBITDA is in hand.** The 1H26 EBITDA likely includes about 56 days of full shut-in. Whether the $510M LTM reflects that is unverified (Medium, inference).
- **Cash:** $95M at Pearl at 30-Jun-26 (Dana Gas 2Q26 results, High). It matches the 1Q26 figure. The consolidated versus entity cash gap is unexplained.
- **FCF:** the FY24 $508M "FCF" is not after the roughly $45.5M annual coupon (previous run, Medium). Conversion is unknown.
- **Dividends:** the repo cites $105M each to Dana Gas and Crescent. No 2026 dividend source was found.

### Collections and counterparty
- KRI collection rate was about 78% in 1H26. Dana Gas collected $104M from the KRI in 1H26: $68M in 1Q26 and $100M in 2Q26 (Dana Gas results, Aug-26, High on figures).
- Pearl described the KRG as in "material arrears for more than three years" (Peregraf, 07-Aug-26, Medium). The receivable stock is not disclosed.
- The 2025 collection rates of 94–100% (FAB, 10-Nov-25, Medium) are well above the 1H26 rate, so collections deteriorated in 2026.

### Debt and documentation
- $350M 13.00% senior secured notes due 15-May-28, priced at 100 in a 3.5-year deal. Annual coupon about $45.5M, semi-annual $22.75M, next coupon 15-Nov-26 (Zawya; repo, Medium).
- A floating facility, BL370608 Corp, falls due 08-Sep-28. Its size and lender are unstated and the DFC link is unconfirmed (previous run, High on maturity).
- Covenants, restricted payments, DSRA, force majeure treatment, change-of-control put at 101% and the intercreditor terms were not accessible (Data gap).
- The issuer has no public agency rating. The repo's "B- (Implied) / CCC+" is an internal label (negative search result only, Medium).
