## View
- **Neutral, with a cautious bias on the 13.00% 2028 notes.** The repo's "Overweight / Secured Carry" predates the outages and is stale.
- **Operations are the weak point.** Khor Mor has stopped three times in about eight months: 26-Nov-25 (rocket strike), 28-Feb to 13-Apr-26 (about 45 days) and 16-Jul to 27-Jul-26 (about 11 days) (press, Medium-High).
- **Leverage is low but its basis is unverified.** It is about 1.0x net on Pearl-entity cash of $95M. The $510M LTM EBITDA behind it is not reconciled to the outages. The last tier 1 figures are FY24 EBITDA of $521M and Q1-25 of $138M.
- **Collections are contested.** Sources conflict on 1H26 KRG collection (100% in 1Q26 per Dana Gas press, about 78% in 1H26 per the repo log). Pearl says the KRG has been in material arrears for over three years.
- **Kirkuk sales:** 100 MMscf/d to the Federal Ministry of Electricity since 04-Aug-26 diversifies the payer. It also brought a KRG breach allegation (06-Aug-26). The payment channel is unverified.
- **Biggest risk:** a longer or repeated shut-in while arrears persist, hitting cash before the May-28 bullet under bond and DFC terms we have not seen.
- **Next datapoints:** the 15-Nov-26 coupon ($22.75M) and Dana Gas 3Q26 results (around Nov-26).
- **Pricing is unresolved (item 1).** Do not add until it is.
- **Confidence: Medium-Low.** Outage dates are consistent across sources. Pearl-level 2026 financials, documentation and live pricing are not in hand.

## Analysis

### Shut-ins and operations
- **Third stoppage:** a rocket attack on a storage tank on 26-Nov-25 cut about 3,000 MW of Kurdistan power (Oman Observer, 27-Nov-25, Medium-High).
- **28-Feb to 13-Apr-26:** production was suspended as a precaution on the US/Israel–Iran conflict and announced 05-Mar-26 for more than 700 MMscf/d. Full resumption was announced 13-Apr-26 (Offshore Technology, Mar-26; The Energy Year; MEES, 17-Apr-26; PUKmedia, 13-Apr-26, Medium-High).
- **16-Jul to 27/28-Jul-26:** production was restored after guarantees from Erbil and Baghdad (Peregraf, 27-Jul-26, Medium). One position could not re-source this independently.
- **Force majeure:** whether Pearl or its lenders treated the 2026 events as force majeure is unconfirmed (Low). The earlier Enerflex force majeure relates to the Apr-24 strike.
- **KM250:** the repo reports 750 MMscf/d commissioned. Dana Gas H1-26 said capacity was underutilised and regional disruption constrained utilisation (Zawya, Aug-26, Medium).

### Kirkuk gas sales
- Gas began flowing on 04-Aug-26 to the Taza plant, 100 MMscf/d under a one-year agreement with the federal Ministry of Electricity. It is additional to 655 MMscf/d supplied to the KRG (Iraq Oil Report, Aug-26; Reuters via TradingView, Aug-26, Medium-High).
- The KRG alleged breach on 06-Aug-26. Pearl rejected this on 07-Aug-26, citing exclusive marketing rights and prior legal proceedings (Rudaw; Iraq Business News, 07-Aug-26; Peregraf, 07-Aug-26, Medium).
- No filed litigation, settlement currency, payer record or take-or-pay terms were found (Data gap).
- The payment channel for Federal MoE sales is unverified (Low). Quality of payment from Baghdad is the key unknown.

### Earnings and leverage
- **Last tier 1 earnings (07-Oct-26 capture):** FY24 revenue $642M and EBITDA $521M, about 81% margin. Q1-25 revenue $166M and EBITDA $138M (High).
- **Repo 1H26:** gross debt $595M, cash $95M, net debt $500M, LTM EBITDA about $510M, net leverage 0.98x (repo, 07-Oct-26, Medium).
- **Repo history is inconsistent.** The table shows 2024A EBITDA of $415M and 2025A of $470M, against the tier 1 FY24 $521M. The repo series is treated as stale.
- **1H26 EBITDA** likely includes about 45 days of full shut-in. The $510M LTM figure may not reflect it (Medium, inference).
- **Cash:** $95M at Pearl at 30-Jun-26 (Dana Gas 2Q26 results, High). It is unreconciled with consolidated cash of $612–672M.
- **Coupon burden:** about $45.5M a year, $22.75M semi-annual. The FY24 "FCF" of $508M is not after that coupon (Medium).
- **Dividends:** the repo cites $105M each to Dana Gas and Crescent. No 2026 dividend source was found.

### Collections and counterparty
- **Dana Gas press, 1Q26:** KRI receipts of $60M, a 100% collection rate and an $18M advance linked to direct condensate and LPG sales (Zawya, 1Q26, Medium).
- **Repo log (Dana Gas results, Aug-26):** $68M in 1Q26, $100M in 2Q26, about 78% KRI collection in 1H26 (High on the cited figures, but the sources conflict).
- **Reconciliation:** the sources may use different bases (current billing, arrears, advances), but this is not verified. Neither is adopted over the other (item 3).
- **Arrears:** Pearl says the KRG has been in material arrears for more than three years (Peregraf, 07-Aug-26, Medium). The receivable stock is undisclosed.
- **2025 collection rates of 94–100%** (FAB, 10-Nov-25, Medium) are above the 78% for 1H26. If the 78% is current billing, collections have weakened.

### Debt and documentation
- **Notes:** $350M 13.00% senior secured notes due 15-May-28, ISIN NO0013387860, issued at 100. Next coupon 15-Nov-26 (Zawya; repo, Medium).
- **Floating facility:** BL370608 Corp matures 08-Sep-28. Size and lender are unstated, and the link to the $250M DFC facility is unconfirmed (07-Oct-26 capture, High on maturity).
- **Not accessed:** covenants, restricted payments, DSRA, call schedule, change-of-control put (101% per the repo) and intercreditor terms.
- **Rating:** no public agency rating; Fitch withdrew in Apr-20. The repo's "B- (Implied) / CCC+" is internal (07-Oct-26 capture, Medium).
- **Pricing:** repo 103.5 / 11.45% / 625 bp (07-Oct-26) against the capture's 8.31% / 392 bp. A 13% coupon at 103.5 and 2.6 years to maturity is broadly consistent with about 11.5%, but neither level is verified (Low).
