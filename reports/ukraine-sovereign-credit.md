## View
- Neutral to cautious on the restructured Step Up bonds. The credit rests on official-sector money rather than market access. With no market level there is no relative-value call.
- Near-term liquidity is adequate but eroding. Gross reserves were US$47.1bn on 01-Oct-26, down from US$51.3bn in Jun-26. Net of one-year drains they were US$37.0bn in Sep-26, about 78% of gross. Import cover was 5.6 months (NBU, IMF, High).
- The 2027 financing gap is the central variable. The need is US$52.6bn, of which about US$32.6bn is unconfirmed. The EU is expected to provide about US$17.5bn (Interfax, 16-Oct-26, Medium).
- Inflation has re-accelerated: CPI was 10.0% y/y in Sep-26, from 8.1% in Aug-26. The key rate is 16.0% and the NBU meets on 29-Oct-26 (Ukrstat, IMF CPI database, NBU, High).
- The IMF anchor holds. The 4-year EFF of US$8.1bn has had its first review. The Nov-26 mission and the combined 2nd and 3rd reviews in Dec-26 are the gating items (Medium).
- Fiscal pressure is rising. The IMF WEO 2026E balance is -18.4% of GDP, and the 2027 draft budget deficit is 15% of GDP against 12.4% in 2026 (Medium).
- Biggest risk: a slip in IMF or EU disbursement forcing NBU financing, which would feed inflation and reserve losses. A ceasefire would move prices but would not remove the financing need.
- Confidence: Medium overall. High on reserves, CPI and rates. Medium on the financing gap, the IMF timetable and bond terms. Low on Moody's, the warrant exchange, peace timing and market levels.

## Analysis

### Financing and IMF
- The IMF approved a new 4-year EFF of US$8.1bn in Feb-26. The first review was completed on 20-Jul-26 with about US$690m (SDR 503M) disbursed, bringing the total to US$2.2bn (IMF, 20-Jul-26, High; kmu.gov.ua, Jul-26; BOFIT Weekly 29/2026, Medium).
- A mission is expected in Nov-26. The combined 2nd and 3rd reviews are targeted for Dec-26, conditional on reform resumption and financing assurances (Interfax, 16-Oct-26, https://en.interfax.com.ua/news/economic/1221410.html, Medium).
- The 2027 external financing need is US$52.6bn. About US$32.6bn is unconfirmed, the EU is expected to provide about US$17.5bn, and the 2026–29 gap is about US$136.5bn (Interfax, 16-Oct-26, https://en.interfax.com.ua/news/economic/1213066.html, Medium). Intellinews cites a residual gap of about US$63bn for 2026–27 (Medium-to-Low). The two figures cover different periods and are not reconciled.
- The €90bn EU Ukraine Support Loan was agreed in Dec-25 and covers 2026–27. Reparations-loan status and the "€37bn available through end-2026" figure rest on press only (Low-to-Medium). EU primary documents were not read.

### External position
- Gross reserves were US$47.1bn on 01-Oct-26 and US$48.7bn at end-Aug-26. Import cover was 5.6 months (NBU, 06-Oct-26, High).
- Monthly gross reserves: US$45.7bn (May-26), US$51.3bn (Jun-26), US$51.2bn (Jul-26), US$48.7bn (Aug-26), US$47.1bn (Sep-26). Reserves net of drains: US$41.0bn (Jul-26), US$38.6bn (Aug-26), US$37.0bn (Sep-26). Predetermined drains were US$10.2bn (IMF reserve template, High).
- The decline since Jun-26 is consistent with FX intervention or debt service. The cause is not established. USD/UAH was stable at 44.66 in Sep-26, against 43.76 in Apr-26 (BIS, High).
- The current account was -US$24.8bn over 2025-Q3 to 2026-Q2. In 2026-Q2 it was -US$1.7bn, with goods -US$15.5bn and secondary income +US$16.4bn (IMF BOP, High). FDI liabilities were about US$0.3bn a quarter, so grants and official flows fund the deficit.
- The IMF WEO 2026E current account is -18.9% of GDP. The REER z-score is -0.5 (IMF, High).

### Fiscal and debt
- State debt was 98.4% of GDP at end-2025 (MinFin, Medium). The 2026 projection range of 105%–122.6% is not used because its sources are unclear.
- The IMF WEO 2026E fiscal balance is -18.4% of GDP, nominal GDP is US$225.3bn and real growth is 2.0% (IMF WEO, Medium).
- The 2027 draft budget deficit is UAH 1.67trn, or 15% of GDP, against 12.4% in 2026. The Finance Minister warned of possible cash shortfalls before end-2026 (Kyiv Post/UNN monthly highlights, Oct-26, https://unn.ua/en/news/september-2026-in-ukraine-monthly-highlights-and-what-to-expect-in-october, Medium). The definition differs from the WEO figure and is not reconciled.
- Interest-to-revenue, the debt-stabilising primary balance and gross financing needs cannot be computed from the available sources. A sovereign model from official sources is still to be built.

### Monetary and banks
- The NBU raised the key rate by 50bp to 16.0% on 18-Sep-26 (NBU, High). Sep-26 CPI was 10.0% y/y and 2.1% m/m, with core at 8.8% y/y (Ukrstat via ukranews, https://ukranews.com/en/amp/news/1180578-inflation-stood-at-2-1-in-september-what-saw-biggest-price-increase, and Forbes.ua, Oct-26, Medium; headline corroborated by the IMF CPI series, High).
- Trailing real policy rate is about 6.0% (16.0% less 10.0%, derived). The dashboard's 7.9% uses Aug-26 CPI and is stale.
- The NBU decision on 29-Oct-26 falls after the research date.
- 2026-Q2 bank capital adequacy was 17.63%, NPLs 12.45%, provision cover 74.6% and ROE 22.5% (IMF FSI, High).

### Bonds and calendar
- The Sep-24 exchange covered US$20.5bn at a 37% nominal haircut and issued Step Up A (2029, 2034, 2035, 2036) and Step Up B (2030, 2035, 2036) bonds (White & Case / MinFin, Sep-24, Medium).
- Step Up A pays 1.75% (2024–25), 4.50% (2026), 6.00% (late 2027) and 7.75% (2034). Step Up B pays 0.00% to mid-2027, 3.00% (late 2027) and 7.75% (2034), with a GDP outperformance clause (White & Case, Sep-24, Medium). Exact step dates and clause terms are unverified.
- Near-term cash cost is low next to the official envelope, but coupons step up from late 2027, so the cost is not zero beyond Mar-27.
- A press snippet says GDP warrants were exchanged on 24-Dec (probably 2025), creating Series C 2032 of US$3.497bn and Series B 2030/2034 of US$16.9m each, with 99.06% support (intellinews, undated, Low).
- Calendar: 29-Oct-26 NBU rate decision, Nov-26 IMF mission, Dec-26 combined 2nd and 3rd reviews (target), 31-Mar-27 Paris Club suspension expiry (Medium, to be reconfirmed).

### Ratings and politics
- S&P CCC+/Stable, upgraded from SD on 22-Jan-26. Fitch CCC, upgraded from RD on 22-Dec-25 (Medium; the agency pages were not read). The Fitch outlook is unconfirmed.
- Moody's Ca/stable per the fxempire.com aggregator (28-Oct-26, Low). A UNIAN report of a move to a positive outlook is undated and unverified (Low).
- Zelenskyy said the realistic window for a settlement is late 2026 to summer 2027 (Yeni Safak, Oct-26, Low). No energy-strike truce was agreed in Sep-26 (UNN, Oct-26, Low). Intellinews reported Eurobonds up about 1.5% in early Sep-26 on US-led peace efforts, with the date unverified (Low).

### Quasi-sovereign: Ukrzaliznytsia
- The repo file shows 2024A calculated EBITDA of US$380M, net leverage of 3.86x and about US$70M of donor grants in reported EBITDA (cembicredit database, 19-Sep-26, Medium). The 64c price and 19.25% yield in the file are not Bloomberg levels and are internally inconsistent (Low). The model needs rebuilding from the issuer's filings.
