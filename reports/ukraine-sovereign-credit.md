## View
- Neutral to cautious on the restructured Step Up bonds. The credit rests on official-sector money rather than market access. No market level is available, so there is no relative-value call.
- Liquidity is adequate near term. NBU gross reserves were US$47.1bn on 01-Oct-26, US$38.6bn net of one-year drains in Aug-26, and import cover was 5.6 months (NBU, IMF, High).
- The 2027 gap is the central variable. Press quoting the finance minister puts 2027 external financing needs at US$52.6bn, of which about US$32.6bn is unconfirmed (Interfax, 16-Oct-26, Medium). The EU is expected to supply about US$17.5bn.
- The IMF anchor holds. The new 4-year EFF of US$8.1bn was approved in Feb-26 and the first review was completed in Jul-26. The combined 2nd and 3rd reviews are targeted for Dec-26 (kmu.gov.ua, Jul-26; Interfax, 16-Oct-26, Medium).
- Fiscal metrics are unsustainable without grants: the 2026E balance is -18.4% of GDP and the 2026E current account is -18.9% of GDP (IMF WEO, Medium).
- Coupon relief ends from late 2027, and the Paris Club suspension is dated to expire on 31-Mar-27 (White & Case, Sep-24; Paris Club, Mar-23, Medium).
- Biggest risk: the 2027 gap combined with slippage on IMF conditionality or EU funding. A ceasefire would move prices but would not remove the financing need.
- Confidence: Medium overall. High on reserves, rates and CPI. Medium on the financing gap and the IMF programme, which rest on press. Low on ratings detail, bond terms, the warrant exchange and market levels.

## Analysis

### Financing and IMF
- The IMF approved a new 4-year EFF of US$8.1bn in Feb-26. The first review was completed in Jul-26 with about US$690m disbursed, bringing the total to US$2.2bn (kmu.gov.ua, Jul-26; BOFIT Weekly 29/2026, Medium). The knowledge base separately records SDR 503M disbursed on 20-Jul-26 (IMF, High).
- The combined 2nd and 3rd reviews are targeted by year-end, with a mission expected in Nov-26. They are conditional on reform resumption and financing assurances (Interfax, 16-Oct-26, https://en.interfax.com.ua/news/economic/1221410.html, Medium).
- 2027 external financing need is US$52.6bn. About US$32.6bn is unconfirmed. The EU is expected to provide about US$17.5bn, leaving about US$15bn from "several potential sources". The 2026–29 gap is about US$136.5bn (Interfax, 16-Oct-26, https://en.interfax.com.ua/news/economic/1213066.html, Medium). These are press quotes of Ukrainian officials and IMF estimates. EU primary documents on the €90bn support loan were not read, so EU timing is Medium-to-Low.

### External position
- Gross reserves were US$47.1bn on 01-Oct-26 and US$48.7bn at end-Aug-26. Import cover was 5.6 months (NBU, 06-Oct-26, High).
- Reserves net of predetermined and contingent one-year drains were US$38.6bn in Aug-26, with predetermined drains of US$10.0bn. Gross reserves moved between US$45.7bn (May-26) and US$51.3bn (Jun-26) (IMF reserve template, High).
- The current account was -US$24.8bn over 2025-Q3 to 2026-Q2. 2026-Q2 was -US$1.7bn, with goods -US$15.5bn and secondary income +US$16.4bn (IMF BOP, High). FDI liabilities were about US$0.3bn a quarter, so grants and official flows fund the deficit.
- USD/UAH was 44.66 in Sep-26, against 43.76 in Apr-26 (BIS, High). The REER z-score is -0.5 (IMF, High).

### Fiscal and debt
- State debt was 98.4% of GDP at end-2025 (MinFin, Medium). The 2026 projection range of 105%–122.6% is not used because its sources are unclear.
- The 2026E fiscal balance is -18.4% of GDP, nominal GDP is US$225.3bn and real growth is 2.0% (IMF WEO, Medium).
- Interest-to-revenue, the debt-stabilising primary balance and gross financing needs cannot be computed from the sources available. A sovereign model from official sources is still to be built.

### Bonds and calendar
- The Sep-24 exchange covered US$20.5B at a 37% haircut and issued Step Up A (2029, 2034, 2035, 2036) and Step Up B (2030, 2035, 2036) bonds (White & Case / MinFin, Sep-24, Medium).
- Step Up A pays 1.75% (2024–25), 4.50% (2026), 6.00% (late 2027) and 7.75% (2034). Step Up B pays 0.00% to mid-2027, 3.00% (late 2027) and 7.75% (2034), with a GDP outperformance clause (White & Case, Sep-24, Medium). Exact step dates and clause terms are unverified.
- A press snippet says GDP warrants were exchanged on 24-Dec (year not stated, probably 2025), creating Series C 2032 of US$3.497bn and Series B 2030/2034 of US$16.9m each, with 99.06% support (intellinews, undated, Low).
- Calendar: Nov-26 IMF mission, Dec-26 combined 2nd and 3rd reviews (target), 31-Mar-27 Paris Club suspension expiry (Medium, to be reconfirmed).

### Ratings and market
- S&P CCC+/Stable, upgraded from SD on 22-Jan-26. Fitch CCC, from RD on 22-Dec-25. Moody's Ca/stable (fxempire.com/credit-ratings/ukraine, accessed 19-Oct-26, aggregator; ukranews.com/en/news/1085202, undated). The agency pages were not read (Medium for S&P and Fitch, Low-to-Medium for Moody's).
- Intellinews reported Eurobonds up about 1.5% in early Sep-26 on renewed US-led peace efforts, with no significant progress after visits to Moscow and Kyiv. The date is unverified (Low).

### Monetary and banks
- The NBU raised the key rate by 50bp to 16.0% on 18-Sep-26. CPI was 8.1% y/y in Aug-26 and core was 8.1% (NBU, Ukrstat, High). The ex-ante real rate of 7.9% uses trailing CPI (derived, Medium).
- 2026-Q2 bank capital adequacy was 17.63%, NPLs 12.45%, provision cover 74.6% and ROE 22.5% (IMF FSI, High).

### Quasi-sovereign: Ukrzaliznytsia
- The repo file shows 2024A calculated EBITDA of US$380M, net leverage of 3.86x and about US$70M of donor grants in reported EBITDA (cembicredit database, 19-Sep-26, Medium). The 64c price and 19.25% yield in the file are not Bloomberg levels and are internally inconsistent (Low). The model needs rebuilding from the issuer's filings.
