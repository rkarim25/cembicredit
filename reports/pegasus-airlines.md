## View
Pegasus is a sound franchise hit by an external shock. Both agencies hold their ratings (Fitch BB-, S&P B+) but moved outlooks to negative in May–Jun-26. The 2026 trough comes from Middle East airspace closures and a jet-fuel spike. Q2-26 EBITDA was EUR 80m against EUR 254m in Q2-25. Fitch forecasts EBITDAR net leverage of 6.2x in 2026, falling to 3.8x in 2027.

Liquidity looks adequate. Cash was EUR 1,134m at 30-Jun-26, and the 2026 bond was refinanced into a USD 500m 2031 Eurobond in Oct-24. I found no near-term maturity wall, but the maturity ladder, RCF and covenants are unread.

The biggest risk is that the disruption and high fuel persist into 2027. Fitch calls Pegasus the most Middle East-exposed rated EMEA low-cost carrier. The repo's benchmark bond, quote and 2022A–2024A financials are stale or model-generated and should not be used. Without Bloomberg levels, I make no relative-value call.

Stance: hold with a cautious bias. Confidence is Medium, because the evidence is mostly press and aggregator summaries of tier 2 documents.

## Analysis
### Repo record is stale
- The benchmark "PGSUS 9.25% 2026" (price 103.5, YTM 7.65%, spread 360bp, dated 19-Sep-26) predates a refinancing. In Oct-24 Pegasus issued a USD 500m Eurobond due 2031 at an 8% yield and tendered the 2026 notes from the proceeds (White & Case, Baker McKenzie, Oct-24, Medium). The tender result was not seen, so any remaining stub is unknown.
- The recorded EBITDA (2024A: 950), net debt (1,995) and leverage (2.1x) show a flat 29–30% margin, a constant 5.5% restatement variance and generic commentary. They do not reflect lease liabilities or Pegasus's EBITDAR-based reporting (repo record, Medium). No Cognitive Credit model exists. A model must be built from the audited TFRS/IFRS statements on KAP and the IR site, with IAS 29 and leases.

### Ratings
- Fitch affirmed BB- (senior unsecured BB-/RR4) on 20-May-26 and moved the outlook from positive to negative. It cited "deteriorating operational performance and expected weakening of credit metrics in 2026" (cnbce.com press report of Fitch, 20-May-26, Medium).
- S&P affirmed B+ in Jun-26 and moved the outlook from stable to negative (ekonomim.com, paratic.com, Jun-26, Medium).
- Moody's: nothing found. The agency releases were not retrieved, so downgrade triggers are unseen.

### Earnings and leverage
- Q2-26 EBITDA was EUR 80m (9.2% margin) against EUR 254m (29.0%) in Q2-25. The net loss was EUR 92m against a EUR 122m profit. Fuel expense rose EUR 125m year on year.
- H1-26 revenue was EUR 1,506.7m (H1-25: EUR 1,497.7m). The H1-26 net loss was EUR 244.7m (Quartr summary of the Q2-26 release, Medium).
- Fitch forecasts 2026 EBITDAR of EUR 527m against EUR 816m in 2025. EBITDAR net leverage is forecast at 4.2x (2025), 6.2x (2026) and 3.8x (2027) (Fitch via press, 20-May-26, Medium).
- A secondary source puts net debt/EBITDA at 5.0x at Jun-26 against 3.5x at end-2025 (Low-Medium; definitions unverified).

### Operating exposure
- About 15% of international capacity is tied to the Middle East. Fuel hedging covers 62.5% of 2026 consumption (Fitch via press, 20-May-26, Medium-High).
- In Q2-26 international ASK fell 9% and international passengers fell 11%, while domestic ASK rose 29%. Jet fuel rose about 90% year on year, and fuel CASK rose 63% after hedging (Q2 deck summary and intellinews, Sep-26, Medium). The investor presentation did not render, so these come from a summary.
- No traffic data after Aug-26 was found.

### Liquidity and funding
- Cash was EUR 1,134.4m at 30-Jun-26 against EUR 1,087.1m at 31-Dec-25 (Quartr, Medium).
- The firm 100-aircraft 737-10 order delivers from 2028, so peak capex falls after the current stress (Medium).
- CMB approval for up to USD 250m of foreign debt was reported by ch-aviation in Mar-26 (Low). A cnbce.com headline reports a USD 149.9m bond issue, undated and unopened (Low, unverified).
- The RCF, covenants, maturity ladder and lease liabilities are not sourced.

### Relative value
No current price, yield or spread exists for the 2031 notes. No view is taken until Reza supplies Bloomberg levels.
