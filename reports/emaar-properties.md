## View
Emaar is a strong BBB+/Baa1 credit with a net cash position, a record backlog and rising earnings. The H1-26 primary release confirms revenue of AED 23.9bn (+21%), EBITDA of AED 12.9bn (+24%) and a backlog of AED 164.9bn (+13%). The drivers are off-plan sales (AED 26.6bn in H1-26) and Emaar Development, which supplies most of the profit growth.
The biggest risk is the Dubai residential cycle. Press reports citing broker data show average prices down 1.7% YoY in Aug-26, the first annual fall in about 5.5 years, with off-plan about 75% of sales. About 82% of the backlog is UAE development (AED 135.7bn of AED 164.9bn).
With net cash, a price correction should hit earnings and sentiment before solvency. Spread risk is limited unless collections or cancellations deteriorate.
Recurring revenue was flat (AED 5.1bn), so about 31% of EBITDA is recurring (AED 4.0bn of AED 12.9bn, computed). The earnings mix is cyclical.
The repo record has stale ratings, an unmatched benchmark bond and unusable covenant and maturity blocks. Relative value cannot be assessed until Bloomberg levels arrive.
Confidence: High on credit quality and ratings. Medium on the net cash figure and the Dubai market data. Low on bond pricing, which is unsourced.

## Analysis
### Ratings
- S&P upgraded to BBB+ from BBB and Moody's to Baa1 from Baa2, both stable, in Jun-25. S&P cited record backlog (AED 110bn at Dec-24), UAE presales of AED 65.4bn in 2024, net cash, low leverage and strong adjusted EBITDA margins (Emaar press release, 11-Jun-25, High). https://www.emaar.com/en/press-release-listing/sp-and-moodys-upgrade-emaars-credit-ratings
- Fitch upgraded to BBB, stable, in Jul-23 (Emaar press release, Jul-23, Medium; dated). https://www.emaar.com/en/press-release-listing/fitch-ratings-moodys-investors-service-and-sp-global-upgrade-emaar-properties-pjscs-credit-ratings
- The repo "BBB / Baa2" is stale for S&P and Moody's. A 2026 rating action was not found; see open item 2.

### H1-26 results (to 30-Jun-26)
- Net profit before tax AED 12.8bn (+23%), revenue AED 23.9bn (+21%), EBITDA AED 12.9bn (+24%) (Emaar H1-26 press release, 07-Aug-26, High). https://www.emaar.com/en/press-release-listing/emaars-net-profit-before-tax-increased-by-23-to-aed-12-8-billion-us-3-5-billion-revenue-backlog-reaches-aed-164-9-billion-us-44-9-billion-in-h1-2026
- Property sales were about AED 26.6bn. The revenue backlog is about AED 164.9bn (+13% YoY), of which UAE development is AED 135.7bn (+6% YoY) (same release, High).
- Recurring revenue was AED 5.1bn and recurring EBITDA AED 4.0bn, both similar to H1-25 (same release, High).
- Emaar Development PJSC: revenue AED 13.3bn (+34%), profit before tax AED 7.8bn (+41%) (same release, High).
- Net profit of AED 11.15bn (+26%) and net cash of AED 64.1bn come from press summaries (Gulf Today, 07-Aug-26; WAM, 10-Aug-26; Medium). The release page I read does not show them. The net cash perimeter is unverified (open item 4). https://gulftoday.ae/business/2026/08/07/emaar-revenue-rises-21-per-cent-to-dhs239-billion-during-the-first-half
- The release lists EBITDA (AED 12.9bn) close to profit before tax (AED 12.8bn). The EBITDA definition should be reconciled to the house cash-EBITDA identity from the DFM statements before it is used in leverage.
- Primary DFM PDF: https://feeds.dfm.ae/documents/2026/Aug/7/52433569-0887-4100-ba14-58ee448166f1/Emaar%20Properties%20H1%202026%20Press%20Release%20%20%20English.P.pdf (located, not read in full).

### Balance sheet and liquidity (FY24, audited)
- Cash USD 10.5bn, gross debt USD 2.64bn, net debt USD -7.88bn, EBITDA USD 5.22bn (calculated), interest cover 20.4x (repo record, from FY24 audited statements, KPMG opinion 14-Feb-25, High).
- Sukuk at 31-Dec-24: Series 3 USD 750m due 2026, Series 4 USD 500m 3.875% due 2029, Series 5 USD 500m 3.7% due 2031. RCF AED 3.67bn largely undrawn (repo data notes, High).
- Series 3 carries a 3.635% profit rate and matured 15-Sep-26 (ISIN XS1488480333) (search result, Medium). Repayment is unconfirmed (open item 3). The amount is small relative to cash.
- FCF is not yet extracted (capex and CFO are missing from the repo). Under the house identity FCF cannot be stated.

### Dubai market risk
- The sector primer flags off-plan sales and escrow rules as the key drivers. Instalment collections, not launches, determine cash conversion of the backlog.
- Khaleej Times (Aug/Sep-26, Medium, citing Cavendish Maxwell) reports average Dubai residential prices of AED 1,636/sq ft, -1.7% YoY in Aug-26, with off-plan about 75% of sales. Only 44 of 717 off-plan projects launched since Jul-23 cut prices by 5% or more since Feb-26. I did not read the Dubai Land Department series.
- H1-26 sales were still strong (about AED 26.6bn), so a slowdown is not yet visible in Emaar's numbers. Q3-26 sales are the next test.

### Repo data quality
- The "debt_maturities" block (USD 320bn), the covenant block (100x leverage "breached") and the bond quote are template or legacy content and should not be used. Replace them from the prospectus and filings.
- No Cognitive Credit model exists. A model must be built from the DFM statements (FY24, FY25, H1-26).

### Ownership
- Emaar is a Dubai government-related entity (Investment Corporation of Dubai is the largest holder per the sector primer). The ICD stake is unverified (Low). The Jun-25 upgrades cited standalone metrics, so government support is not the basis of the rating.
