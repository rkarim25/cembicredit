## View
The credit is distressed but serviced. Every bond coupon in 2026 has been paid, and the $428mn April 2026 maturity was repaid from internal cash (Interfax-Ukraine, 23-Apr-26; Forbes.ua, 02-Mar-26 and 18-May-26, Medium).
H1 26 net debt of $866mn (1.2x LTM EBITDA ex-JVs) predates the August–September strikes and is stale for forward leverage (Metinvest H1 26, 14-Sep-26, High).
Zaporizhstal has been idle since 11-Aug-26 after four strikes, and force majeure is to be declared on part of the contracts. No restart date is public (SteelOrbis, 17-Sep-26; Kurs.com.ua, Sep-26, Medium).
Barclays forecasts H2 26E EBITDA ex-JVs of $98mn against $300mn in H1, FY26E of $398mn, net leverage of 2.4x in 2026E and 2.5x in 2027E, and H2 26E FCF of -$79mn (Barclays, Oct-26, Medium).
Cash of $191mn at end-June 26 compares with $332mn of 7.65% notes due 01-Oct-27. Closing the gap needs positive FCF or an extension. Barclays expects an extension of 2–3 years at 9–10% coupons without haircuts (Medium).
The 2027 and 2029 notes are priced near distressed levels by Barclays (c.87 cash / 23% yield and c.77 cash / 18% yield). The curve is inverted, which points to expected liability management. I have no Bloomberg levels, so the Barclays switch into MHPSA 6.25% 2029s is reported, not endorsed.
The biggest risk is further strikes on Zaporizhstal and Kamet, with cash below Moody's $200mn buffer ahead of the 2027 maturity.
Overall confidence is Medium-Low. The repo record is internally inconsistent, and the forecast and market levels are broker-sourced.

## Analysis

### Operations and the Zaporizhstal outage
- Zaporizhstal has been idle since 11-Aug-26. It was struck on 11-Aug, 27-Aug, 12-Sep and 17-Sep, with 17 missiles in total, 8 killed and 28 injured (SteelOrbis, 17-Sep-26, Medium). Metinvest holds 49.99%, and the JV sits outside EBITDA ex-JVs.
- Force majeure is to be declared on part of the contracts after the Zaporizhstal and Kamet stoppages (Kurs.com.ua, Sep-26; Forbes.ua, 15-Sep-26, Medium).
- Barclays reports Kamet Steel (2.0mt) hit on 05-Sep-26 and fully shut down (Barclays, Oct-26, Medium).
- Barclays forecast (Medium):

| US$mn | FY25 | H1 26 | H2 26E | FY26E | FY27E |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| EBITDA ex-JVs | 643 | 300 | 98 | 398 | 387 |
| Simple FCF | -1 | 213 | -79 | 134 | 35 |
| Net debt | 1,065 | 866 | 965 | 965 | 957 |

- FY27E assumes iron ore at $100/t. Barclays gives c.$300mn at $90/t.
- FY25 EBITDA is $765mn per Interfax-Ukraine and $643mn ex-JVs per Barclays. The definitions differ and are not reconciled (Medium).
- Whether FY27E assumes a Zaporizhstal restart is unknown (open item 5).

### Liquidity
- The $428mn 8.5% notes due 23-Apr-26 were repaid from internal cash, taking wartime bond service above $1bn (Interfax-Ukraine, 23-Apr-26; Metinvest H1 26, 14-Sep-26, High/Medium).
- End-June 26: gross debt $1,057mn ($168mn short-term, $889mn long-term), cash $191mn, net debt $866mn (Metinvest H1 26, High).
- Cash is below Moody's $200mn minimum buffer (Moody's, Aug-26, via Barclays, Medium).
- H2 26E: operating cash flow of $98mn, capex of $129mn including $130mn of repairs, and interest of $48mn give FCF of -$79mn (Barclays, Medium).
- No data was found on undrawn committed facilities, covenants or supplier finance.
- The United Coal disposal (H1 26 EBITDA of -$16mn) is assumed to bring no material cash (Barclays, Medium).

### Capital structure
- 2027 notes: $332.354mn, 7.650%, due 01-Oct-27, coupons 1-Mar and 1-Sep (Metinvest IR, 09-Oct-26, High). Total 2027 debt service is $351mn (Interfax-Ukraine, 23-Apr-26, Medium).
- 2029 notes: $500mn, 7.750%, due 17-Oct-29, coupons 17-May and 17-Nov (metinvestholding.com, High). Total 2029 debt service is $550mn (Interfax-Ukraine, Medium).
- Annual coupons are about $25mn on the 2027s and about $39mn on the 2029s (my arithmetic).
- Interfax reported earlier talks with the largest bondholders on extending the April 2026 notes, which is precedent for liability management (Interfax-Ukraine, Apr-26, Medium).
- Barclays levels (Oct-26, Medium): 2027s c.87 cash, 23% yield; 2029s c.77 cash, 18% yield.
- Barclays' base case is a 2–3 year extension at 9–10% coupons without haircuts. Higher coupons would absorb cash at $90–100/t iron ore (Medium).

### Relative value
- Barclays recommends switching METINV 2027s (23% yield) into MHPSA 6.25% 2029s (c.12% yield). The reasons are lower refinancing risk at MHP, offshore poultry at about a quarter of MHP EBITDA, and no Eurobond maturity before 2029. Barclays has Metinvest at Market Weight and MHP at Overweight (Medium).
- This remains unverified until open items 1–3 are answered.

### Asset cushion
- The European re-rolling assets (Italy, Bulgaria, UK, Romania) produced 1.5mt and c.$150mn EBITDA in 2025, about 23% of group EBITDA ex-JVs (Barclays, Oct-26, Medium).
- They partly depend on Ukrainian feedstock. Zaporizhstal had shipped over 22,000t of hot-rolled steel to Metinvest Tubular Iași, with plans for up to 180,000t in 2026 (Romania-Insider, Feb-26, Medium). The outage therefore also affects this buffer.
- JORC iron ore reserves are 2.1bn tonnes (July-21 audit) against c.16mt of 2025 output (Barclays, Medium).
- Rail exports to Europe are the fallback if Black Sea ports are disrupted. Barclays assumes 70–80% of external ore volumes are preserved into 2027 (Medium).

### Ratings
- Moody's Caa3 Stable (Aug-26), S&P CCC+ Negative and Fitch CCC- are recorded via Barclays (Oct-26, Medium).
- Press reports S&P raised the rating to CCC+ after the April repayment and that Fitch cut to CCC- earlier in 2026 (BondBlox, 2026, Low). Interfax carries an earlier Moody's Caa3 stable confirmation, undated (Low).
- Agency pages were not accessed. All three ratings are unverified.

### Repo data quality
- The benchmark label "METINV 15.65% 2029" conflicts with the 7.75% coupon. The stored 93.27 price and 14.98% yield (27-Sep-26) conflict with Barclays' c.77.
- The 2024A row shows cash $191mn, gross debt $1,057mn and net debt $866mn. These equal the end-June 26 figures.
- Barclays shows 2024 total debt of $1,705mn, net debt of $1,048mn, revenue of $8,050mn and EBITDA ex-JVs of $898mn. Interfax's end-2024 total debt of $1,705mn agrees (Medium).
- The stored 2024A EBITDA of $737mn also does not fit Interfax's FY25 $765mn being down 24.2% (implied FY24 of about $1.0bn, my arithmetic, definition unverified).
- The 2021A FCF of $4,013mn is reconcilable only with EBITDA incl. JVs of $7,044mn. The ebitda_reconciliation comments are boilerplate.
- The knowledge theme page lists the 2027s and 2029s at 7.75%. The issuer page says 7.65% for the 2027s.
- No Cognitive Credit model exists. A model must be built from the FY24 and FY25 annual reports and the H1 26 financial statements. These were not read directly this run.

## Where the models disagreed
1. **Repo benchmark and coupons.** The 2027 and 2029 coupons and the repo record's reliability were not disputed in substance. Both reads agree the 7.65% and 7.75% issuer-page coupons prevail over the repo label, per the evidence hierarchy (tier 2 over stale repo data).
2. **Barclays figures.** The position treats them as Medium and unverified. This is consistent with the prior resolution (tier 3, no Bloomberg confirmation).
3. **Ratings.** No source above tier 3 was found by either side. The point stays open and is not resolved.
