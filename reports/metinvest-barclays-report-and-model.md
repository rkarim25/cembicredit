## View
The credit is distressed but serviced. All 2026 bond coupons found have been paid, and the $428mn April 2026 maturity was repaid from internal cash (Interfax-Ukraine, 23-Apr-26; Forbes.ua, 02-Mar-26 and 18-May-26, Medium).
H1 26 net debt of $866mn (1.2x LTM EBITDA ex-JVs) predates the August–September strikes and is stale for forward leverage (Metinvest H1 26, 14-Sep-26, High).
Zaporizhstal has been idle since 11-Aug-26 after four strikes. Force majeure is to be declared on part of the contracts. No restart date is public (SteelOrbis, 17-Sep-26; Kurs.com.ua, Sep-26, Medium).
Barclays forecasts H2 26E EBITDA ex-JVs of $98mn against $300mn in H1, FY26E of $398mn and net leverage of 2.4x in 2026E and 2.5x in 2027E (Barclays, Oct-26, Medium).
Cash of $191mn at end-June 26 compares with $332mn of 7.65% notes due 01-Oct-27. Barclays expects a 2–3 year extension at 9–10% coupons without haircuts (Medium).
Barclays prices the 2027s at c.87 cash / 23% yield and the 2029s at c.77 / 18%. The inverted curve points to expected liability management. I have no Bloomberg levels, so the switch into MHPSA 6.25% 2029s is reported, not endorsed.
The biggest risk is further strikes on Zaporizhstal and Kamet while cash sits below Moody's $200mn buffer ahead of the 2027 maturity.
Overall confidence is Medium-Low. The repo record is internally inconsistent, and the forecasts and market levels are broker-sourced.

## Analysis

### Operations and the Zaporizhstal outage
- Zaporizhstal has been idle since 11-Aug-26. It was struck on 11-Aug, 27-Aug, 12-Sep and 17-Sep, with 17 missiles in total, 8 killed and 28 injured (SteelOrbis, 17-Sep-26, Medium). Metinvest holds 49.99%, and the JV sits outside EBITDA ex-JVs.
- Force majeure is to be declared on part of the contracts (Kurs.com.ua, Sep-26; Forbes.ua, 15-Sep-26, Medium).
- Barclays reports Kamet Steel (2.0mt) hit on 05-Sep-26 and fully shut down (Barclays, Oct-26, Medium).
- Barclays forecast (Medium):

| US$mn | FY25 | H1 26 | H2 26E | FY26E | FY27E |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| EBITDA ex-JVs | 643 | 300 | 98 | 398 | 387 |
| Simple FCF | -1 | 213 | -79 | 134 | 35 |
| Net debt | 1,065 | 866 | 965 | 965 | 957 |

- FY27E assumes iron ore at $100/t. Barclays gives c.$300mn at $90/t.
- FY25 EBITDA is $765mn per Interfax-Ukraine and $643mn ex-JVs per Barclays. The definitions differ and are not reconciled (Medium).
- H1 26 segment EBITDA: mining $119mn (down 30%), metallurgy $249mn (up 17%) (GMK Center, 14-Sep-26, Medium).

### Liquidity
- The $428mn 8.5% notes due 23-Apr-26 were repaid from internal cash (Interfax-Ukraine, 23-Apr-26; Metinvest H1 26, 14-Sep-26, High/Medium).
- End-June 26: gross debt $1,057mn, cash $191mn, net debt $866mn. Total debt is down 27% since the start of the year (Metinvest H1 26, via GMK Center, 14-Sep-26, High).
- Cash is below Moody's $200mn minimum buffer (Moody's, Aug-26, via Barclays, Medium).
- H2 26E: operating cash flow of $98mn, capex of $129mn including $130mn of repairs, and interest of $48mn give FCF of -$79mn (Barclays, Medium).
- No data was found on undrawn committed facilities, covenants or supplier finance.

### Capital structure
- 2027 notes: $332.354mn, 7.650%, due 01-Oct-27, coupons 1-Mar and 1-Sep (Metinvest IR, 09-Oct-26, High). Total 2027 debt service is $351mn (Interfax-Ukraine, 23-Apr-26, Medium).
- 2029 notes: $500mn, 7.750%, due 17-Oct-29, coupons 17-May and 17-Nov (metinvestholding.com, 16-Oct-26, High). Total 2029 debt service is $550mn (Medium).
- Annual coupons are about $25mn on the 2027s and about $39mn on the 2029s (my arithmetic).
- The 1-Sep-26 coupon on the 2027s was not evidenced this run. It should be confirmed (see open item 9 and the Bloomberg check).
- Barclays levels (Oct-26, Medium): 2027s c.87 cash, 23% yield; 2029s c.77 cash, 18% yield.

### Relative value
- Barclays recommends switching METINV 2027s into MHPSA 6.25% 2029s (c.12% yield). The reasons are lower refinancing risk at MHP and no Eurobond maturity before 2029. Barclays has Metinvest at Market Weight and MHP at Overweight (Medium).
- This remains unverified until open items 1–3 are answered.

### Asset cushion
- The European re-rolling assets produced 1.5mt and c.$150mn EBITDA in 2025, about 23% of group EBITDA ex-JVs (Barclays, Oct-26, Medium). They partly depend on Ukrainian feedstock, so the outage also affects this buffer (Romania-Insider, Feb-26, Medium).
- JORC iron ore reserves are 2.1bn tonnes (July-21 audit) (Barclays, Medium).

### Ratings
- Moody's Caa3 Stable (Aug-26), S&P CCC+ Negative and Fitch CCC- are recorded via Barclays (Oct-26, Medium). No agency page was read. All three are unverified.

### Repo data quality
- The benchmark label "METINV 15.65% 2029" conflicts with the 7.75% coupon. The stored 93.27 price and 14.98% yield (27-Sep-26) conflict with Barclays' c.77.
- The 2024A row shows cash $191mn, gross debt $1,057mn and net debt $866mn. These equal the end-June 26 figures. Barclays and Interfax show end-2024 total debt of $1,705mn.
- `fcf_2024a` of -550 is the FY22 value. The 2024A capex is negative. The 2021A FCF of $4,013mn is not credible. The EBITDA reconciliation comments are boilerplate.
- The theme page shows 7.75% for the 2027s. The issuer page shows 7.65%.
- No Cognitive Credit model exists. A model must be built from the FY24 and FY25 annual reports and the H1 26 statements. The filings themselves were not read this run.

## Where the models disagreed
1. **Ratings.** The position treated them as unverified. Neither side had a source above tier 3, so the point stays open (rule 2).
2. **Repo reliability.** No material dispute. The issuer-page coupons (tier 2) prevail over the stale repo labels.
