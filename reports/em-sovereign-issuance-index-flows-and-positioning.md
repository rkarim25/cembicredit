## View
- No quantitative view is possible this run. The brief has no captures, and no 2026 issuance, flow or positioning data was verified. This document is a framework and a data request.
- Supply should be judged net: gross issuance less amortisation, buybacks and liability management (Low).
- Frontier and HY issuers come to market when spreads allow, so heavy HY issuance is more likely a signal of demand than a cause of weak spreads (Low).
- Flows reach spreads mainly through the fast-money channel (retail funds, ETFs). Passive and institutional money is the stickier base (Low).
- The seven CEEMEA sovereigns added to the universe change the benchmark mix. The size of the effect is unverified.
- Biggest risk: a US rates or dollar shock that closes the primary market while flows turn to outflows, hitting HY and frontier names hardest.
- Confidence in the overall view: Low. It rests on method, not data.
- Next step: Open items 1 to 5 are the minimum needed to form a view.

### Supply
- The sovereign framework (knowledge/frameworks/sovereign-credit.md, section 8) lists forthcoming issuance and buyback or liability-management history as technicals. Section 3 defines gross financing need as fiscal deficit plus amortisation (repo framework, Medium as method).
- Net supply, not gross, is the relevant measure. The market is most sensitive when net supply surprises, or when funding is front-loaded into a window of tight spreads (Low).
- Access is spread-dependent. The framework's access test is the last Eurobond date and spread, and market access is binary near distress (repo framework, Medium as method). Applied to 2026, this is Low.

### Flows
- Flow data splits by fund type: hard currency, local currency, blended and ETF. Retail and ETF flows are the faster channel (Low).
- Index changes move passive money mechanically. The repo records that seven EMBI CEEMEA sovereigns (GCC IG, Kazakhstan, Israel, Lebanon) were added to the knowledge universe in commit 06c0ade (repo, tier 2, High for the fact). Their effect on benchmark weights is unverified, because weights are captured per page from the index report and not typed (knowledge/README.md, High).

### Positioning
- Positioning should be read against benchmark and against local-currency carry. Local-currency carry crowding is the larger tail risk than a hard-currency spread long (Low).
