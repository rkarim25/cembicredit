## View
- Supply is large but absorbed. Gross hard-currency EM sovereign issuance was $214.2bn for Jan–Sep-26 against $208.1bn a year earlier, roughly 3% higher (Fortun tracker, 08-Oct-26, Medium).
- Issuance is concentrated in IG and Gulf names. Only Türkiye and Brazil among the top ten are clearly HY, so net supply for HY is lighter than the gross figure implies (Medium).
- The marginal driver of spreads in September was US duration, not supply. Gramercy describes a quality-neutral repricing, with IG and HY within 2bp of each other (12-Sep-26, Medium).
- Index changes in 2026 are mainly local-currency: the GBI-EM GD issuer cap cut and the new frontier GBI-EM Edge index. None forces hard-currency selling (Medium).
- The flows and positioning legs remain unsupported: no verified 2026 flow or survey data. Items 1–5 and 7 are the minimum needed to form a positioning view.
- Biggest risk: a further leg higher in US yields and oil closing the primary market for HY and frontier names while heavy IG and Gulf supply continues.
- Confidence: Medium on supply and the rates-driven spread read; Low on flows and positioning.

## Analysis
### Supply
- Jan–Sep-26 gross hard-currency issuance by EM governments was $214.2bn against $208.1bn in Jan–Sep-25. The tracker covers USD and EUR Eurobonds plus Samurai, CHF and panda bonds: 191 bonds from 49 issuers (Jonathan Fortun EM Sovereign Bond Issuance Tracker, 08-Oct-26, https://jonathanfortun.com/data/em/sovereign-issuance, Medium; single secondary compilation, no stated methodology beyond issuer documents).
- September issuance was $16.9bn against a 10-year September average of $17.3bn (same tracker, 08-Oct-26, Medium). Supply was normal, not elevated.
- Year-to-date top ten: Mexico $20.8bn, Saudi Arabia $14.8bn, Türkiye $12.1bn, Brazil $10.4bn, Poland $9.8bn, Indonesia $9.3bn, Hungary $8.1bn, Kuwait $8.0bn, Chile $7.9bn, Abu Dhabi $7.5bn (same tracker, 08-Oct-26, Medium). The ratings of Mexico and the other top-ten names were not verified this run.
- JPM Asset Management expected IG-driven sovereign issuance in 2026 (Q1-26 EMD investment themes, https://am.jpmorgan.com/gb/en/asset-management/adv/insights/portfolio-insights/fixed-income/investment-themes-for-emerging-market-debt-q1-2026/, Medium).
- The primary market is open to HY. The week to 12-Sep-26 saw about $18.6bn across 30 tranches from 22 issuers (CEEMEA 57%, Latin America 24%, Asia 19%), including Jamaica's $1bn at 6.25% (Gramercy EM Weekly, 12-Sep-26, http://www.gramercy.com/2026/09/em-weekly-september-12-2026/, Medium).
- Net supply, not gross, is the relevant measure: gross issuance less amortisation, buybacks and liability management (repo framework, sovereign-credit.md sections 3 and 8, Medium as method). No net figure was found.

### Spreads and rates
- Hard-currency sovereigns fell 0.80% in the week to 12-Sep-26. The 10Y+ bucket fell 0.74% against 0.07% for short duration. Gramercy calls it "a broad repricing of global duration" with a "narrowing margin for error with every leg higher in U.S. yields and oil" (12-Sep-26, Medium).
- The IG-HY differentiation was minimal in that week, which points to rates rather than credit or supply as the driver (Gramercy, 12-Sep-26, Medium). One week of data; the read should be tested against current spread levels (open item 1).

### Index changes
- J.P. Morgan is lowering the GBI-EM GD issuer cap to 9% from 10% in 2026. Indonesia, Mexico, Malaysia, China and India lose weight; Thailand, Poland, South Africa and Brazil gain. Saudi Arabia (about 2%) and the Philippines (about 1%) are on watch (Advisor Perspectives and SCMP coverage, 2025–26, Medium; the J.P. Morgan notice itself was not seen).
- J.P. Morgan announced a frontier GBI-EM Edge index covering about $328bn across 26 markets, with Nigeria at 7.4% (Nairametrics, 14-Sep-26, https://nairametrics.com/2026/09/14/j-p-morgan-includes-nigeria-in-new-emerging-market-bond-index-with-7-4-weight/, Medium).
- Argentina's bonds reportedly left EMBI+ effective 30-Sep-25 under the five-year age rule and remain in EMBI GD (iProfesional, https://www.iprofesional.com/economia/438992-cambio-para-riesgo-pais-jp-morgan-excluyo-argentina-de-indice-clave, Low; date inconsistency, see open item 8).
- The repo records seven CEEMEA sovereigns (GCC IG, Kazakhstan, Israel, Lebanon) added to the knowledge universe in commit 06c0ade. Effects on benchmark weights are unverified (repo, High for the fact; weights are captured per page from the index report, knowledge/README.md).

### Flows and positioning
- No usable 2026 flow data. An EPFR/Nomura result citing $5bn of October and $2.8bn of September outflows carried no year and conflicts with reports of record EM bond fund inflows in 2025; it is discarded (Low).
- No survey, ETF holdings or dealer inventory data. Any positioning call is unsupported.
- Flows reach spreads mainly through the fast-money channel (retail funds, ETFs); passive and institutional money is stickier (Low, judgement).
