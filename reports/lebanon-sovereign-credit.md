## View
- Lebanon has been in sovereign default since Mar-20 (repo knowledge page). There is no restructuring framework, so the bonds trade on the odds and timing of an IMF programme and a Eurobond restructuring, not on carry.
- The binding constraint is domestic. The IMF mission of 15-18 Sep-26 ended without a staff-level agreement (SLA). The financial gap law, a 2027 budget and a medium-term fiscal framework are prerequisites, and Eurobond treatment follows them.
- The external gap is structural. The 2025 current account was -9.16 USD bn (-22.5% of GDP, 2025E), financed by remittances and FDI, not by market debt.
- The reserves record ends in Mar-25: 38.16 USD bn gross, of which 28.34 USD bn is gold and 9.82 USD bn ex gold. Current usable FX cannot be assessed.
- The headline fiscal balance (+3.3% of GDP, 2025E) is not the restructuring problem. The problem is debt of 139.4% of GDP and the allocation of losses across the state, BdL, banks and depositors.
- The BLOM Bond Index is +26.0% YTD to 30.36 on 01-Oct-26. This is an index, not a bond price. It suggests the market already prices some IMF progress.
- Biggest risk: a harsher haircut than the market implies if the Eurobond claim ranks below depositor claims. A security shock delaying the legislative sequence is the second risk.
- Stance: a restructuring-timing and recovery-value situation. No value call is possible without bond prices, legal terms and a recovery assumption.
- Confidence: Medium on status and sequencing, Low on value.

## Analysis

### IMF and legislative sequence
- The IMF mission was in Beirut 15-18 Sep-26 and left without an SLA (Reuters via The Star, 28-Sep-26, Medium).
- Finance Minister Jaber said a full programme requires the final financial gap law (Reuters via The Star, 28-Sep-26, Medium).
- Other prerequisites are a 2027 budget and a medium-term fiscal framework (Enterprise, 30-Sep-26, Medium).
- Cabinet approved the draft gap law in Dec-25. Press summaries say it lets depositors withdraw up to USD 100,000 over four years and converts larger balances into bonds backed by central bank assets (press summary, Sep-26, Low: not verified against the law text).
- Status is High confidence as reported but tier 3, because the IMF release was not retrieved. Timing is Medium.
- I could not retrieve the IMF release of 13-Feb-26 (pr-26050) or The National's 01-Oct-26 piece on the bank resolution law.

### External position
- The current account was -1.47, -2.32, -1.71 and -3.66 USD bn in 2025-Q1 to Q4 (IMF BOP, repo record, 09-Oct-26, High).
- The goods balance ran -3.08 to -5.01 USD bn a quarter. Secondary income was +1.43 to +1.74 USD bn a quarter. FDI liabilities were +0.42 to +0.54 USD bn a quarter (IMF BOP, High).
- Services fell to +0.006 USD bn in Q4 from +0.47 USD bn in Q3. Seasonality is a plausible reading, not established (Low).
- Gross reserves including gold were 38.16 USD bn in Mar-25, of which gold 28.34 USD bn and ex gold 9.82 USD bn (IMF International Liquidity, High on the figures, Low on the current position).
- Gross reserves rose 31.2% from Mar-24 to Mar-25. Between Dec-24 and Mar-25, gold rose by 4.24 USD bn and ex-gold reserves by 0.58 USD bn, so the rise is mostly gold. I read it as largely valuation, but the source does not separate price from volume (Low).
- The exchange rate was fixed at 89,500 LBP per USD from Oct-25 to Mar-26 (BIS, repo record, High). Import cover was 13.6 months in 2023 (World Bank WDI, Medium: dated, and distorted by gold).
- Commodity terms of trade fell 4.6% from May-25 to May-26 (IMF CTOT, Medium).

### Fiscal and debt
- Debt was 139.4% of GDP, the fiscal balance +3.3% of GDP and nominal GDP 34.5 USD bn, all 2025E (IMF WEO, High on the figures, Medium as estimates).
- The debt-stabilising framework and interest-to-revenue cannot be computed, as the repo has no interest or revenue series.
- Whether the Eurobond claim is a residual after depositor claims is an unresolved legal and policy question and decides recovery. See open items 4 and 7.

### Monetary
- CPI y/y was 17.3% (Mar-26), 20.0% (Apr), 19.0% (May), 17.3% (Jun), 15.7% (Jul) and 16.7% (Aug-26) (IMF CPI database, High). Inflation is high and not converging, which I read as a fragile anchor (Medium).
- Real GDP growth was 4% in 2025E (IMF WEO, Medium).

### Market
- The BLOM Bond Index rose 1.88% in the week to 01-Oct-26, to 30.36, and was +26.04% YTD. Its weighted yield was 61.88% and the spread 5,748 bp (BLOMINVEST via Libnanews, 01-Oct-26, Medium).
- The index mixes instruments and does not identify bond-level moves or the curve. The same source calls risk exceptionally high.
- No Bloomberg levels were supplied this run.

### Data gaps
- Ratings, Eurobond list and terms, arrears and accrued interest since Mar-20, the IMF DSA, the gap law text, reserves after Mar-25, the 2026 budget, bond prices, index weight and holder composition.
- A sovereign model needs updating from official sources once the reserve and fiscal data are refreshed.
