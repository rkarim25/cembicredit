## View
- Positive external and banking fundamentals; fiscal leverage and security risk are the constraints. No relative-value view is possible because ratings and market levels are not sourced.
- Gross reserves are USD 241.6bn (Aug-26), up 4.9% y/y, and USD 232.2bn net of one-year drains. Import cover is 14.4 months.
- The current account is near balance, with a 4-quarter sum of USD +4.5bn. FDI liabilities of USD 14-16bn a quarter fund the balance of payments.
- Government debt is 69.8% of GDP and the deficit 5.9% of GDP for 2026E (IMF WEO). This is the weak leg, and the WEO may be stale against the latest budget.
- The policy rate has fallen from 4.0% (Apr-26) to 3.25% (Sep-26) with CPI at 1.54%. The ex-ante real rate of about 1.7% uses trailing CPI, so easing room exists.
- The broad real exchange rate has a 10-year z-score of +2.67, which is a competitiveness risk. It is not a standalone signal.
- Biggest risk: security escalation that worsens the fiscal path and triggers rating or outlook action.
- Confidence: Medium. Data is tier 2 and unrefreshed, with no news search and no market data.

## Analysis
### External position
- Gross reserves incl. gold were USD 241.6bn in Aug-26, up from USD 229.4bn in Mar-26 (IMF International Liquidity, 09-Oct-26, High). The 12-month change is +4.9% (derived, High).
- Reserves net of predetermined one-year drains are USD 232.2bn. Drains were USD 9.2bn in Aug-26 (IMF reserve template, High).
- Import cover is 14.36 months (World Bank WDI, 2025, High).
- Current account, last four quarters: USD +4.53bn (IMF BOP, 2025-Q3 to 2026-Q2, High). Quarterly prints range from -0.74bn to +5.31bn (IMF BOP, High).
- Services run a surplus of USD 10.1bn against a goods deficit of USD 2.2bn and a primary income deficit of USD 7.8bn in 2026-Q2 (IMF BOP, High).
- FDI liabilities were USD 14.2bn in 2026-Q2 and USD 16.0bn in 2026-Q1 (IMF BOP, High). Persistence is Medium.
- WEO 2026E current account is +1.9% of GDP, with nominal GDP of USD 719.8bn (IMF WEO, Medium).
- Commodity terms of trade are down 1.6% over 12 months to May-26 (IMF CTOT, derived, Medium).

### Fiscal position
- Gross government debt is 69.8% of GDP and the fiscal balance -5.9% of GDP for 2026E (IMF WEO, Medium).
- Real growth is 3.5% for 2026E (IMF WEO, Medium).
- pb*, interest to revenue and the maturity profile cannot be computed. The effective interest rate and revenue data are not in the brief.

### Monetary and financial
- The policy rate path was 4.0% (Apr-26), 3.75% (May and Jun), 3.5% (Jul and Aug), 3.25% (Sep-26) (BIS, High).
- CPI was 1.54% y/y in Aug-26, down from 1.94% in Apr-26 (IMF CPI database, High). The ex-ante real rate is 1.71%, on trailing CPI (derived, Medium).
- The BIS broad real EER was 112.58 in Aug-26, down from a peak of 115.2 in May-26. The 10-year z-score is +2.67 (BIS, derived, Medium).
- FX moved from 2.90 per USD (May-26) to 3.03 (Sep-26) (BIS, High).
- Banking, 2026-Q1: NPL 0.62%, CAR 13.82%, tier 1 11.13%, FX open position to capital -2.73% (IMF FSI, High). ROE fell to 12.88% from 15.18% in 2025-Q4 (IMF FSI, High). The banks are not a contingent-liability concern on this data.
