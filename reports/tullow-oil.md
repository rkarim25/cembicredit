## View
Overweight / Selective Add on TLWLN 10.25% 2028 notes at ~97.0¢ (~16.5% YTM, 1,180 bps Z-spread).
1H26 operational turnaround is robust: Derived EBITDA rebounded to $455.9M (+53.9% YoY) on 71kboepd Jubilee output and >99% FPSO uptime.
Comprehensive April 2026 refinancing pushed senior maturities to Nov-2028 and secured $100M Glencore cargo liquidity.
The 22-Sep-26 ICC arbitration loss awarding Ghana $393.09M (including 100% penalties) erases 2026E FCF ($170-250M) and delays 2027 refi plans.
Outright operational disruption is mutually destructive; Ghana MinFin has explicitly committed to non-disruptive enforcement.
High 15.00% all-in quarterly coupon carry (10.25% cash + 3.00% PIK + 1.75% PIYC) provides substantial 375 bps quarterly downside cushion.
Biggest risk: Unilateral Ghana GRA bank garnishments against offshore escrow accounts prior to a negotiated instalment schedule.
Least sure about: The exact duration, cash profile, and gas netting mechanism agreed with the Ministry of Finance.
Confidence: Medium, supported by tier 2 operational filings and government releases balancing headline sovereign tax litigation.

## Analysis

### Operational Recovery & Field Delivery
Tullow's 1H26 operating turnaround demonstrates fundamental cash-generating capability across core Ghanaian offshore assets (Cognitive Credit, 07-Oct-26, High). Revenue reached $496.3M and Derived EBITDA rebounded to $455.9M, representing a 53.9% YoY increase compared to $296.3M in 1H25 (Cognitive Credit, 07-Oct-26, High). Performance was underpinned by gross Jubilee field production expanding to ~71kboepd (+16% YoY) with >99% FPSO uptime (Tullow 1H26 Report, 07-Oct-26, High). Partner Kosmos Energy guides a 2H26 exit rate of 82-87kboepd following the hookup of the J77-P producer and J73-WI water injection wells (Brief, 07-Oct-26, Medium). Proved plus probable (2P) commercial reserves rose 22% to ~122mmboe (vs ~100mmboe at YE25) following license extensions to 2040, delivering a reserve replacement ratio exceeding 380% (Tullow 1H26 Report, 07-Oct-26, High). Gross Ghana gas receivables fell from $165M to $65M in 1H26 after $73M was collected, with the remainder resolved by late September, unlocking vital working capital (Brief, 07-Oct-26, Medium). Low operating cash lifting costs of $8-$18/boe ensure positive cash generation even during severe commodity downturns (Wood Mackenzie, 07-Oct-26, High).

### Capital Structure, Refinancing & Liquidity Buffer
Comprehensive capital structure refinancing completed on 27-Apr-2026 resolved immediate 2026 maturity cliffs and secured structural trader support (Tullow Filing, 27-Apr-26, High). Senior secured Eurobonds were extended to November 2028 ($1,162M outstanding), while the existing $400M Glencore facility was converted into subordinated junior notes maturing May 2030 ($423M outstanding, coupon SOFR + 12.75% PIK with step-up to 13.50% if Brent > $65/bbl) (Tullow Filing, 27-Apr-26, High). Concurrently, Glencore committed a new $100M super-senior cargo prepayment facility, and Tullow redeemed $100M of notes at par (Tullow Filing, 27-Apr-26, High). As of 1H26, unrestricted cash stood at $187.5M against total debt of $2,330.0M, resulting in net leverage of 2.9x (Cognitive Credit, 07-Oct-26, High). Under maintenance and incurrence covenants, net leverage of 2.30x provides 1.45x headroom against the 3.75x ceiling, while interest coverage of 3.42x clears the 2.50x covenant floor (Tullow Covenant Compliance, 07-Oct-26, High). Operating lease exposure remains substantial via the TEN FPSO ($351M lease liability, ~$120M annual cash drain); however, the Feb-2026 sales and purchase agreement to buy the vessel for ~$126M net upon closing in 1Q27E will permanently eliminate this annual cash drain (Tullow 1H26 Report, 07-Oct-26, High).

### ICC Tax Arbitration Ruling & Fiscal Headwinds
On 22-Sep-2026, the International Chamber of Commerce (ICC) arbitral tribunal issued a final ruling upholding the Ghana Revenue Authority's (GRA) tax assessment on $561.5M in historic business interruption insurance proceeds (ICC Award, 22-Sep-26, High). The ruling confirmed $196.5M in core tax liability plus a 100% statutory penalty of $196.5M, totaling $393.09M (ICC Award, 22-Sep-26, High). The liability effectively absorbs the company's full 2026E organic FCF guidance of $170-250M (Tullow 1H26 Report, 07-Oct-26, Medium). Furthermore, a second pending arbitration regarding the disallowance of $190.5M in loan interest deductions plus penalties remains postponed to 2027, creating potential cumulative tax exposure of over $700M under worst-case assumptions (Brief, 07-Oct-26, Medium). Despite the severe headline ruling, systemic default or operational disruption is mitigated by mutual alignment with the sovereign: Ghana Finance Minister Dr. Cassiel Ato Forson publicly confirmed that the state will enforce the award while explicitly ensuring that collection does not disrupt ongoing Jubilee and TEN operations (Ghana MinFin Statement, 29-Sep-26, High). Crucially, 100% of crude and gas export revenues flow through offshore escrow accounts, insulating Eurobond debt service from local onshore account freezes prior to repatriation (Morgan Stanley / Cognitive Credit, 07-Oct-26, High). Negotiations are actively focused on multi-year phased instalments or offsets against domestic gas deliveries (Ghana MinFin Statement, 29-Sep-26, Medium).

### Credit Recommendation & Valuation Thesis
TLWLN 10.25% 2028 senior secured notes trade at ~97.0¢ to yield 16.5% YTM with a Z-spread of 1,180 bps (Secondary Market Marks, 07-Oct-26, Tier 1, High). The security features an all-in quarterly coupon structure of 15.00% (10.25% cash + 3.00% PIK + 1.75% PIYC), which delivers ~375 bps of excess quarterly carry buffer against capital volatility (Indenture Terms, 27-Apr-26, High). In an extreme distress scenario under local insolvency frameworks, asset-level recovery is anchored at 55¢–94¢ based on 2P reserve coverage and critical offshore infrastructure (Model Recovery Analysis, 07-Oct-26, Medium). The market selloff from par to 97¢ over-discounts the $393.09M ruling, as sovereign counterparty dynamics dictate a phased settlement rather than a destructive field shutdown. An Overweight / Selective Add stance is justified for accounts capable of accommodating CCC+ / Caa2 ratings and sovereign litigation volatility (Desk View, 08-Oct-26, Medium).
