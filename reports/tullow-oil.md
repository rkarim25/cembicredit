## View
Neutral to cautious on TLWLN 10.25% Nov-28. The repo's Overweight / Selective Add stays withdrawn: its "over-discounted" basis and 15.00% all-in carry are not sourced to tier 1-2 material.
The ICC tribunal upheld the full $393.09m assessment (tax plus 100% penalty). That is about 30% of the forecast year-end net debt of $1.2bn and above the 2026 FCF guidance of $170-250m.
Operations were strong before the award. The Apr-26 refinancing removed maturities before Nov-28.
Ghana's Finance Ministry says it will implement the award while preserving Jubilee and TEN operations and investment. No amounts, schedule or dates are public.
Ratings are CCC+ (S&P), Caa3 (Moody's) and CCC+ (Fitch), all stable and all set before the award. No post-award action was found.
Biggest risk: a cash-settled or unstaged payment uses up liquidity (about $0.3bn headroom at 30-Jun-26) and returns the credit to restructuring.
Least sure about: payment mechanics, the documentation treatment of the award, and the live bond price.
Confidence: Medium. The award is well sourced; settlement terms and current pricing are not.

## Analysis

### Ghana ICC award
- The ICC tribunal rejected Tullow's challenge to tax on business interruption insurance proceeds and upheld the GRA assessment of US$393,091,993.70 in full ($196.5m tax plus an equal penalty). The GRA issued the assessment in Dec-22 and Tullow went to arbitration in Feb-23 (Graphic Online, 30-Sep-26, https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/ghana-wins-us-393m-tullow-tax-arbitration.html, Medium; Global Arbitration Review, Sep-26, https://globalarbitrationreview.com/article/uk-oil-company-wins-tax-dispute-ghana, Medium; Foley Hoag, 29/30-Sep-26, High on the figure).
- Ghana's Finance Ministry said it would work with Tullow to implement the award under Ghanaian law, taking into account operations at Jubilee and TEN and the investment needed to sustain them (GNA, Sep-26, https://gna.org.gh/2026/09/ghana-wins-393-million-tullow-tax-arbitration/, Medium; MyJoyOnline, Oct-26, https://www.myjoyonline.com/ghana-will-secure-tullow-tax-revenues-while-protecting-jubilee-ten-investments-ato-forson/, Medium). The wording points to instalments or offsets rather than a waiver (judgement).
- Shares fell 45.1% to 10.7p on 30-Sep-26 (HL, 30-Sep-26, Medium); other outlets report about 50% (Nairametrics, 30-Sep-26, Medium).
- A second dispute, on loan-interest deductions (c.$190.5m plus penalties, deferred to 2027), rests on the repo record only (Low). The aggregate exposure above $700m is unverified.
- Searched on 05-Nov-26 for settlement terms, GRA enforcement and post-award rating actions: nothing found.

### Operations and cash
- 1H26 net production was 43.7 kboepd. FCF guidance was raised to $170-250m (from $70-175m per a company update seen in a search summary, Medium). 1H26 FCF was $4m. Capex is $200m. Year-end net debt is forecast at $1.2bn (H1 call via MarketBeat, 28-Sep-26, Medium; the RNS has not been read).
- Cognitive Credit derived EBITDA was $455.9m in 1H26 against $296.3m in 1H25. Cash was $187.5m at 30-Jun-26 (Cognitive Credit, 07-Oct-26, High).
- Leverage depends on definition: Cognitive Credit 1.84x (derived EBITDA); company net debt about $1.4bn; repo 2.9x on net debt of $2,142m and LTM EBITDA of $747m. These are not reconciled (open item 6).
- Liquidity headroom was about $0.3bn at 30-Jun-26, against an award of $393m (company 1H26 via summaries, Medium).
- Asset sales (Kenya, Gabon) appear in a search summary of a Tullow trading update whose date could not be confirmed (Low); not relied on.

### Capital structure
- One senior secured note: $1.210bn, 10.25% cash coupon, due 15-Nov-28 (Tullow IR page, 16-Oct-26, High).
- The Apr-26 refinancing extended the notes to Nov-28 and the Glencore facility to May-30, and added a $100m Glencore cargo prepayment line. The cash interest run-rate is about $125m (company release 27-Apr-26 via summaries, Medium).
- Indenture terms, cross-default or judgment-default provisions, the repo's 101.0 call price, and a delay to the PXF/RBL refinancing into 2027 are unverified (Low; open item 4).
- The TEN FPSO purchase for about $126m net (closing 1Q27E) is supported only by the repo (Medium).

### Ratings
- S&P CCC+ stable (08-May-26); Moody's Caa3 stable (08-May-26 per repo; 27-Apr-26 per a search result of the IR page); Fitch CCC+ stable (15-Jun-26) (Tullow IR page, 16-Oct-26, High on levels, Medium on the Moody's date). The notes are shown as CCC+/Caa2. All are pre-award, and a negative action is more likely than an upgrade (judgement).

### Valuation
- The stored quote of 97.0 / 16.5% YTM / 1,180bp is dated 07-Oct-26 and is not live. A blog shows 103 on 28-Sep, 99 on 30-Sep, 97.75 on 01-Oct and 95.64 on 09-Oct (Chiltern Capital, Oct-26, https://chilterncap.substack.com/p/tullow-oil-waiting-for-ghana, Low). No view on price is taken without a live level and settlement terms (open item 1).
- Upside case: a staged settlement or gas offset. Downside case: cash settlement plus the second dispute against about $0.3bn of liquidity.
