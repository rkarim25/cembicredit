## View
Neutral to cautious on TLWLN 10.25% Nov-28. The previous Overweight / Selective Add is withdrawn: its basis (an "over-discounted" price, 15.00% all-in carry, escrow insulation) is not sourced to tier 1-2 material.
The ICC tribunal (award dated 22-Sep-26, published 29/30-Sep-26) upheld the full $393.09m assessment, tax plus 100% penalty. That is about 30% of the forecast year-end net debt of $1.2bn and well above 2026 FCF guidance of $170-250m.
Operations were strong before the award: 1H26 FCF guidance was raised, and the April-26 refinancing removed maturities before Nov-28.
Ghana's Finance Minister says the award will be enforced while preserving the Jubilee and TEN operations. No amounts, schedule or dates are public.
Equity fell about 45-50% to 10.7p, which reads as a solvency signal.
Ratings are CCC+ (S&P), Caa3 (Moody's) and CCC+ (Fitch), all stable, and all set before the award.
Biggest risk: a cash-settled or unstaged payment uses up liquidity (about $0.3bn headroom at 30-Jun-26) and returns the credit to restructuring.
Least sure about: the payment mechanics, and whether the documentation treats the award as a default trigger.
Confidence: Medium. The award is tier 2; the settlement outlook and the current bond price are unverified.

## Analysis

### Ghana ICC award
- The tribunal dismissed all of Tullow's claims and upheld the $393,091,993.70 assessment on business interruption insurance proceeds, including the 100% penalty. It found the assessment did not breach the Petroleum Agreements and that the GRA's enforcement was lawful (Foley Hoag, 29/30-Sep-26, https://www.foleyhoag.com/news-and-insights/news/2026/september/victory-for-the-republic-of-ghana-in-icc-tax-arbitration-against-tullow-ghana-limited/, High; GNA, Sep-26, https://gna.org.gh/2026/09/ghana-wins-393-million-tullow-tax-arbitration/, Medium).
- The award is dated 22-Sep-26, with a London seat (Global Arbitration Review, Sep-26, https://globalarbitrationreview.com/article/uk-oil-company-wins-tax-dispute-ghana, Medium).
- Shares fell 45.1% to 10.7p on 30-Sep-26 (HL, 30-Sep-26, https://www.hl.co.uk/shares/stock-market-news/company--news/tribunal-rules-against-tullow-oil-in-$196.5m-tax-dispute,-shares-tank, Medium).
- Tullow won an earlier ICC case in Jan-25 on a $320m branch profit remittance tax assessment (Africa Energy, https://www.africa-energy.com/news-centre/article/tullow-oil-wins-320m-ghana-icc-tax-case-investors-back, Medium). The current loss is a separate matter.
- The Finance Minister said the award would be implemented while protecting Jubilee and TEN investments, and that talks continue (MyJoyOnline, Oct-26, https://www.myjoyonline.com/ghana-will-secure-tullow-tax-revenues-while-protecting-jubilee-ten-investments-ato-forson/, Medium). The statement carries no terms.
- A second dispute, on loan-interest deductions (c.$190.5m plus penalties, deferred to 2027), rests on the repo record only (Low). Combined exposure above $700m is an unverified aggregate.
- Searches since 29-Sep-26 found no GRA enforcement notice, no published settlement and no agency action.

### Operations and cash
- 1H26 net production was 43.7 kboepd, up 7% YoY. FCF guidance was raised to $170-250m from $70-175m. 1H26 FCF was $4m after $64m cash interest and $70m refinancing costs. Capex is $200m. Realised oil price was about $95/bbl. Year-end net debt is forecast at $1.2bn (H1 call transcript via MarketBeat, 28-Sep-26, https://www.marketbeat.com/instant-alerts/transcript-tullow-oil-h1-earnings-call-highlights-2026-09-28/, Medium; the RNS has not been read).
- Cognitive Credit shows derived EBITDA of $455.9m in 1H26, against $296.3m in 1H25, and cash of $187.5m at 30-Jun-26 (Cognitive Credit, 07-Oct-26, High).
- Cognitive Credit net leverage is 1.84x (derived EBITDA basis). The repo's 2.9x uses LTM EBITDA of $747m and total debt of $2,330m. Net debt definitions differ between the company and Cognitive Credit and need reconciling (open item 6).
- Gross Jubilee output of about 71 kboepd is consistent with 43.7 kboepd net, though the gross-to-net bridge is not confirmed (Medium).
- Pricing sensitivity: guidance assumes oil near $95. The hedge book beyond the stated 60% downside-cover policy was not found.

### Capital structure
- One senior secured note, $1.210bn, 10.25% cash coupon, due 15-Nov-28 (Tullow IR page, accessed 16-Oct-26, https://www.tullowoil.com/investors/corporate-bond-and-credit-rating/, High).
- The Apr-26 refinancing extended the notes to Nov-28 and the Glencore facility to May-30, and added a $100m Glencore cargo prepayment line. Cash interest fell by about $50m a year, to a run-rate near $125m (company release 27-Apr-26 via search summaries, Medium).
- The previous version's Glencore junior note terms ($423m, SOFR + 12.75% PIK) and the 3.75x covenant ceiling come from the repo only and are unverified (Low).
- The PIK/PIYC components of a 15.00% all-in coupon are not on the issuer page (Low; open item 8).
- The indenture was not reviewed. Whether a tax award or judgment is a default trigger is unknown (open item 4).
- The TEN FPSO purchase for about $126m net (SPA Feb-26, closing 1Q27E per repo) would end a lease drain of about $120m a year. Only the repo supports this (Medium).

### Ratings
- S&P CCC+ stable (08-May-26), Moody's Caa3 stable (08-May-26), Fitch CCC+ stable (15-Jun-26) (Tullow IR page, 16-Oct-26, High). These are pre-award; a negative action is more likely than an upgrade (judgement).

### Valuation
- The stored quote of 97.0 / 16.5% YTM / 1,180bp is dated 07-Oct-26 and is not live. No fresh level is available (open item 1).
- Recovery of 55-94c in the repo model is not reproduced here (Low).
- No view on price is taken without a live level and settlement terms. The upside case is a staged settlement or gas offset. The downside case is cash settlement plus the second dispute, with liquidity of about $0.3bn.
