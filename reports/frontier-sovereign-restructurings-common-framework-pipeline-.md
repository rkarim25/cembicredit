## View
- The Common Framework (CF) cohort is largely done. The G20 progress report lists Ethiopia, Ghana and Zambia, plus Sri Lanka and Suriname, as largely completed. Ethiopia's $1bn Eurobond is the last item of size (IMF GSDR, 15-Apr-26, High).
- Ethiopia shows that official creditors police comparability of treatment (CoT). The first bond deal was rejected by the Official Creditor Committee (OCC). The 29-Jun-26 deal swaps the bond for an $880m 6.15% 2029 bond, a 12% face haircut. It pays $99.37m of missed coupons and a 0.5% consent fee, and adds a tradable New Money Warrant. The OCC validated CoT on 22-Aug-26 but called net relief relatively low (Medium-High).
- Precedent timelines are long: bond exchange closure 16 to 24 months after the standstill (GSDR, High). Plan for that range in any new case.
- The next wave is mostly outside the CF. Senegal is "reprofiling" under a new IMF programme (staff-level agreement 01-Sep-26). Mozambique (Fitch CC) will negotiate only after an IMF deal. Malawi is stalled (Medium).
- Biggest risk: a distressed exchange labelled as reprofiling in Senegal, which would set the template for others. Second risk: preferred-creditor claims by regional lenders shrinking the bondholder pool.
- Confidence: High on the closed CF cohort and timelines. Medium on Senegal and Mozambique, because primary documents (IMF release and DSA, OCC text) were not read. Low on Gabon, Cameroon and Tunisia, which were not researched.

## Analysis
### Closed CF cohort and timelines
- The 6th GSDR Co-Chairs Progress Report (15-Apr-26) lists Ethiopia, Ghana, Zambia, Sri Lanka and Suriname as largely completed. Remaining commercial creditors are residual except Ethiopia's bondholders. Zambia's final programme review was completed in Jan-26 (IMF GSDR, 15-Apr-26, High; positions' reading of the PDF, not independently re-fetched).
- Timeline ranges: standstill to programme approval 2.1 to 10.5 months; approval to first review 6.0 to 17.7 months; bond exchange closure 16.0 to 24.0 months. Bilateral agreements are targeted within 12 months of the MoU, but most are unsigned (same source, High).
- Structure from the repo: IMF programme and DSA, OCC co-chaired by the Paris Club and usually China, MoU, bondholder deal under CoT, then bilateral implementation. The DSA targets size the bondholder ask. IMF lending into arrears lets a programme proceed while bonds are in default (repo knowledge base, tier 2, High).
- The CF covers only DSSI-eligible countries. Middle-income cases used ad hoc committees. Senegal is not CF-eligible on that basis (repo, Medium).

### Ethiopia
- Jan-26: OCC rejected a first deal for too little private haircut. A second restricted period ran 05-28 Jun-26, and the agreement in principle came on 28-29 Jun-26 (Capital Ethiopia, 22-Aug-26; The EastAfrican; Addis Fortune, Jun-26, Medium-High).
- Terms: new $880m 6.15% bond due 15-Jul-29. $99.37m of three missed coupons paid in full. A consent fee of 0.5% of the $1bn nominal. A New Money Warrant gives rights to participate in a future issue of up to $1bn.
- The OCC validated CoT on 22-Aug-26 and warned that net relief remains relatively low. Documentation is still being finalised (Capital Ethiopia, Medium-High).
- The IMF is reported to have backed the deal (The EastAfrican, headline only, Medium).

### Senegal
- Staff-level agreement on a 36-month ECF of about $2.2bn (SDR 1,537.1m, 475% of quota) after an IMF mission on 19-Aug to 01-Sep-26. It replaces the arrangement suspended after over $11bn of undeclared debt emerged (dabafinance.com, capmad.com, Sep-26; Engineering News, 25-Sep-26, Medium).
- The government chose reprofiling over restructuring (africa.com; Afronomics Law News Update 163; serrarigroup.com, Sep-26, Medium). Analysts float a "third way" of large maturity extensions and coupon cuts without deep face haircuts (Afronomics Law, Medium).
- The 13-Sep-26 Eurobond coupon was paid (capmad.com, 04-Sep-26, as cited by the position, Medium).
- Roughly $1.1bn of Eurobonds fall due in 2026-28, and bonds were reported at 50 to 70 cents (Engineering News, 25-Sep-26, Medium).
- Moody's cut Senegal to Caa2 (capmad.com; date not captured, Medium).
- The PM said Senegal must clear arrears of about $3.5bn (Times Live, 09-Sep-26; the figure in the URL "35bn" is ambiguous, so the amount and currency are unconfirmed, Low).
- Whether principal extension or coupon cuts reach Eurobonds is not established. If they do, this is a distressed exchange regardless of label.

### Mozambique
- Fitch cut to CC and affirmed it. It expects any IMF programme to require a liability management operation on the sole $900m 9% 2031 Eurobond, which it would treat as a distressed debt exchange (african-markets.com; clubofmozambique.com; businessday.ng, 2026, Medium; dates not captured).
- Fitch expects an IMF agreement by early 2027. Deficit about 4.6% of GDP, public debt above 90% of GDP, arrears accumulating (ecofinagency; Times Live, 05-May-26, Medium).
- The government has hired financial advisers. A Fitch analyst said restructuring is possible before an IMF agreement (Medium). President Chapo says creditor talks follow the IMF deal (360mozambique.com, Medium). These two positions conflict on sequencing; see disagreements.

### Malawi and preferred creditors
- Restructuring launched in mid-2022 is stalled. The IMF ECF lapsed in May-25. About $1bn is in scope: $887m commercial (Afreximbank and TDB dominant) and $99m bilateral (mwnation.com, dates not captured, Medium).
- Regional lenders claiming preferred status are a main impediment (Credendo; repo note on Afreximbank in Ghana and Zambia, Medium). The larger their share, the smaller the bondholder pool.

### Not researched
- Gabon, Cameroon, Tunisia, Zambia's post-programme status, Ghana's residual creditors and Bolivia were not searched. No CF membership change was verified. Absence from this note is not absence of events.

## Where the models disagreed
Only one position was supplied, so there was no dispute. One tension was kept as an open point: Mozambique's President says talks follow the IMF deal, while a Fitch analyst says restructuring is possible earlier. Both are tier 3, so no winner is picked. Open item 4 and 6 cover it.
