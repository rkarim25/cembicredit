## View
- Turkey is BB-/Ba3 with stable outlooks at all three agencies. Public debt is low (29.5% of GDP at Q2-26) and the policy rate is tight: 37% against 29.73% CPI in Sep-26 (High).
- S&P's scheduled review of 16-Oct-26 produced no new assessment, so BB-/Stable stands. The next scheduled agency catalyst is Moody's in Jan-27, day unconfirmed (Medium).
- Disinflation has stalled near 30%. The TCMB raised its end-2026 inflation forecast to 28% from 26%. A cut to 36% was the consensus for the 22-Oct-26 MPC, but the outcome was not found (Medium on path, Low on today's decision).
- Reserves have turned down: gross incl. gold fell from USD 188.2bn (28-Aug-26) to USD 167.4bn (week to 02-Oct-26). GEFR of about USD 208bn is about 124% of that gross figure (Medium).
- The fund liquidation (131 funds, about USD 18.3bn) is a domestic confidence shock. S&P saw it as unlikely to pressure the rating. Bank exposure is unverified.
- Biggest risk: a lira or oil shock while the TCMB is cutting into about 30% inflation, forcing a reversal and testing reserves.
- Overall confidence is Medium. Market pricing is unsourced, so there is no positioning call.

## Analysis

### Ratings and calendar
- Fitch affirmed BB-/Stable on 17-Jul-26, with 2026 growth of 2.8% and end-2026 CPI of 29.5%. It flagged a possible early election in late 2027 (Hürriyet Daily News, 18-Jul-26, Medium).
- S&P affirmed BB-/B, stable, on 17-Apr-26. It published nothing on its scheduled 16-Oct-26 date. Agencies are not obliged to publish on calendar dates, so this is a non-event rather than a reaffirmation (Foreks, 17-Oct-26, Medium).
- Moody's was Ba3/Stable at the periodic review of 24-Jul-26, with no action (Hürriyet Daily News, Jul-26, Medium).
- Primary agency releases were not read.
- Calendar: MPC 22-Oct-26 (decision not found) and 10-Dec-26.

### Inflation and monetary policy
- Sep-26 CPI was 29.73% y/y (+1.84% m/m) and core 28.70%, against 31.51% in Aug-26 (TurkStat, 03-Oct-26, High).
- The TCMB held the 37.00% rate for the fifth time on 10-Sep-26 and raised its end-2026 forecast to 28% (Intellinews, Sep-26, Medium). The ex-post real rate is about 7.3pp. The data record's ex-ante figure is 12.5pp, on 24.5% expectations (TCMB survey, Sep-26, Medium).
- The October survey shows year-end inflation expectations of 29.63% (Hürriyet Daily News, Oct-26, Medium). That exceeds the TCMB's 28% forecast and implies a credibility gap.
- 15 of 17 analysts in a Bloomberg HT survey expected a cut to 36% on 22-Oct-26 (Milliyet Uzmanpara, Oct-26, Medium).
- The rate instrument is unresolved (open item 2).

### External position
- Gross reserves incl. gold: USD 188.2bn (28-Aug-26), USD 174.4bn (18-Sep-26), USD 167.4bn (week to 02-Oct-26; FX USD 60.3bn, gold USD 107.1bn) (TCMB via Türkiye Today, Medium). Part of the fall is gold revaluation, so it is not all intervention.
- Reserves net of predetermined one-year drains were USD 137.1bn at Aug-26 (IMF template, High but dated).
- Short-term external debt by remaining maturity was USD 170.7bn and GEFR about USD 208bn at Jun-26 (TCMB/HMB, High).
- Fitch said reserve coverage remains below the BB average and projected USD 167bn at end-2026 (Hürriyet Daily News, 18-Jul-26, Medium).
- The current account was USD -23.6bn in Q1-26 and USD -10.9bn in Q2-26, USD -38.9bn over four quarters. FDI liabilities were only USD 1.2–1.3bn a quarter in H1-26, so financing leans on portfolio and other flows (IMF BOP, High).
- The FY26 deficit is projected at 2.6–2.8% of GDP (TCMB, Jul-26).
- USD/TRY was 48.65 in Sep-26, up from 44.80 in Apr-26 (about 1.6% a month). The BIS REER z-score is +0.31, so there is no overvaluation signal (BIS, High).

### Fiscal
- Central government debt was 29.5% of GDP at Q2-26. The FY26 deficit is tracking about 5.2% of GDP, flagged as approximate (HMB, Medium). Gross external debt was USD 539.0bn (31.8% of GDP) at 30-Jun-26 (HMB, High).
- Fitch cited a different debt measure for 2025 (23.8% of GDP), on a different definition (Medium).
- pb* and interest-to-revenue are not computed because no sourced effective interest rate or primary balance was found.

### Fund crisis and banks
- SPK ordered 131 funds from seven managers into liquidation on 17–18-Sep-26. The funds hold about USD 18.3bn (TL890bn) (Daily Sabah; Yeni Şafak, Sep-26, Medium). The investor count is unresolved: about 350,000 in the press against 455,758 in an earlier capture.
- 17 funds were settled on 08-Oct-26, about 44,000 investors, with a TL1m per-investor cap. Interim payouts were approved for the Tera, Pusula, Atlas and Hedef funds, and about half of the funds were to be settled by 16-Oct-26 (Daily Sabah, Oct-26, Medium).
- Vice President Yılmaz said funds carry no state guarantee and TMSF covers banks only (Daily Sabah, 12-Oct-26, Medium).
- S&P said on 02-Oct-26 that the process is unlikely to pressure the rating (Türkiye Today, 02-Oct-26, Medium).
- No source shows bank balance-sheet exposure (open item 8).

### Banking
- Deposit-taker CAR fell to 16.52% in Q1-26 from 19.69% in Q4-25, and Tier 1 to 14.04% from 16.89%. NPL was 2.48% (IMF FSI, High, one quarter). This predates the fund event and its cause is not established.
