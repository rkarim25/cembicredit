## Analysis

### Book exposure and framing (new)
- The Turkey exposure in the book is mostly Tier 2 bank bonds (Reza, 07-Oct-26, High).
- Reza treats the FX and liquidity measures as macroprudential, not capital controls (Reza, 07-Oct-26, High as his stated judgement, not independently verified).
- The framing would need to be revisited if measures restricted external debt service or non-resident repatriation. Nothing in the evidence shows that.
- The Turkey captures are old notes (Reza, 07-Oct-26, High). The figures below should be refreshed before any trading use.
- Position sizes, issuer split, prices and call terms for the Tier 2 holdings are not in the brief, so the exposure is unquantified.

### Inflation
- Sept-26 headline CPI was 29.73% y/y, down from 31.51% in Aug-26, against a 30.3% consensus. It is the lowest since Nov-21 (Trading Economics and Turkish Minute, 05-Oct-26, Medium).
- CPI rose 1.8% m/m. Core C was 28.7% y/y and 2.1% m/m (Tradingpedia, 06-Oct-26, Medium). Core rising faster m/m than headline is the thing to watch.
- This view breaks if Oct-26 CPI prints above about 2.5% m/m.

### Monetary policy
- The CBRT held at 37% for a fifth straight meeting on 10-Sep-26 (Central Banking, Sep-26, Medium). That source calls 37% the overnight lending rate. The repo/corridor mapping has no cited source (open item 2).
- Commerzbank expects a 100bp cut in October (Tradingpedia, 06-Oct-26, Medium). The MPC date of 22-Oct-26 has no cited source (Low, open item 3).
- Ex-post real rate: 37.00% − 29.73% = +7.27%. This is arithmetic on the figures above (Medium).
- The ex-ante real rate on 12M forward CPI is not available (open item 4).

### Fund crisis and reserves
- SPK ordered 131 funds run by seven managers liquidated: more than USD 18bn of assets and 455,758 investors. Interim payouts are capped at TRY 1m per investor per fund, and banks are getting extra lending support (Al-Monitor and Yusuf Kanli, Oct-26, Medium). Search summaries only, not full pages.
- Gross reserves fell four weeks running, from USD 188.2bn (28-Aug-26) to USD 174.4bn (18-Sep-26). That includes a USD 4.3bn drop in the week to 18-Sep-26 (Turkish Minute, 25-Sep-26, Medium).
- Most of the decline came before the formal liquidation order, so it cannot all be put down to the fund event (Low on cause).
- Bank exposure to the funds is unverified (open item 7). It matters most for the Tier 2 holdings.

### Lira and market pricing
- The lira is "propped up by central bank and state bank intervention" (Tradingpedia, 06-Oct-26, Medium).
- There is no sourced USD/TRY, CDS or local-curve level. These go to open items 1 and 5.

### Ratings
- Fitch is BB-/Positive. It raised the outlook on 23-Jan-26, citing reserves and lower FX contingent liabilities (Intellinews and FXEmpire, Medium).
- Moody's is Ba3/Stable and S&P is BB-/Stable (FXEmpire, Oct-26, Medium).
- Risk: the reserve drain undercuts the reserve rationale behind Fitch's positive outlook.
