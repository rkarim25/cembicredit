## View
- Turkey is BB-/Ba3/BB- with stable outlooks at all three agencies. Public debt is low (29.5% of GDP at Q2-26) and the policy rate is tight: 36% against 29.73% CPI in Sep-26 (High).
- The TCMB cut the one-week repo rate by 100bp to 36% on 22-Oct-26, the first move after five holds at 37%. It said the underlying trend of inflation is decelerating (Hürriyet Daily News, Oct-26, Medium).
- Disinflation has stalled near 30%. Year-end expectations were 29.63% in the Oct-26 survey, against a TCMB forecast of 28% (Medium).
- Reserves are weakening: gross incl. gold was reportedly USD 162.2bn at 23-Oct-26, from USD 188.2bn at 28-Aug-26. That is below Fitch's USD 167bn end-2026 projection. GEFR of about USD 208bn is above gross reserves (Medium).
- The fund liquidation (131 funds, about USD 18.3bn) is a domestic confidence shock. S&P saw it as unlikely to pressure the rating. Bank exposure is unverified.
- Biggest risk: a lira or oil shock while the TCMB is cutting into about 30% inflation and falling reserves, forcing a reversal.
- Overall confidence is Medium. Market pricing is unsourced, so there is no positioning call.

## What changed
- The 22-Oct-26 MPC outcome is now found: a 100bp cut to 36%, with the overnight lending rate cut to 39% from 40% and the overnight borrowing rate to 34.5% from 35.5% (Hürriyet Daily News, Oct-26). The prior decision item is closed.
- Gross reserves are updated to USD 162.2bn at 23-Oct-26 (second-hand, Medium). The 15-Oct-26 figure of USD 171.6bn is in the data record.
- Added the Medium-Term Programme of 06-Sep-26, which raised the end-2026 inflation forecast to 28.4% (single secondary source, Low-Medium).
- Moody's forecasts of 29% inflation at end-2026 and 3.4% growth are added.
- Open items stay at 11. The decision item is closed. Two new items cover net reserves on the TCMB definition and the repo-rate confirmation. The reserve-driver and policy-text questions are folded into the [REZA] items.
- The Oct-26 CPI print is due 03-Nov-26 and is the next datapoint.

## Analysis

### Ratings and calendar
- Fitch affirmed BB-/Stable on 17-Jul-26, with 2026 growth of 2.8% and end-2026 CPI of 29.5%. It flagged a possible early election in late 2027 (Hürriyet Daily News, 18-Jul-26, Medium).
- S&P affirmed BB-/Stable on 17-Apr-26. Its scheduled 16-Oct-26 review produced no new assessment, so this is a non-event rather than a reaffirmation (Foreks, 17-Oct-26, Medium). A ratings-aggregator snippet showing "S&P B/positive" is undated and unreliable, and is disregarded.
- Moody's was Ba3/Stable at the periodic review of 24-Jul-26, with no action. It expects 29% inflation at end-2026 and 24% at end-2027 (Hürriyet Daily News, Jul-26, Medium).
- Primary agency releases were not read.
- Calendar: Oct-26 CPI on 03-Nov-26, MPC on 10-Dec-26, Moody's Jan-27 (day unconfirmed).

### Inflation and monetary policy
- Sep-26 CPI was 29.73% y/y, against 31.51% in Aug-26 (TurkStat, High).
- The TCMB cut the one-week repo rate by 100bp to 36% on 22-Oct-26 (Governor Karahan's MPC). The overnight lending rate went to 39% and the borrowing rate to 34.5% (Hürriyet Daily News, Oct-26, Medium). The consensus of 15 of 17 analysts was a cut to 36% (Milliyet Uzmanpara, Oct-26, Medium).
- The ex-post real rate is about 6.3pp on Sep-26 CPI. The data record's ex-ante real rate is about 12pp, on 23.9% 12-month expectations (TCMB survey, Oct-26, Medium). Year-end expectations of 29.63% exceed the TCMB's 28% forecast, a credibility gap.
- The 2027–29 Medium-Term Programme (06-Sep-26) reportedly lifts the end-2026 inflation forecast to 28.4% (Low-Medium, paywalled preview).

### External position
- Gross reserves incl. gold: USD 188.2bn (28-Aug-26), USD 174.4bn (18-Sep-26), USD 167.4bn (week to 02-Oct-26), USD 171.6bn (15-Oct-26) and reportedly USD 162.2bn at 23-Oct-26 (gold about USD 103bn, FX about USD 59bn) (TCMB via Turkish press, Medium). Part of the swing is gold revaluation, so it is not all intervention.
- Reserves net of predetermined one-year drains were USD 137.1bn at Aug-26 (IMF template, High but dated). This predates the fall.
- Short-term external debt by remaining maturity was USD 170.7bn and GEFR about USD 208bn at Jun-26 (TCMB/HMB, High).
- Fitch said reserve coverage is below the BB average (Hürriyet Daily News, 18-Jul-26, Medium).
- The current account was USD -23.6bn in Q1-26 and USD -10.9bn in Q2-26, USD -38.9bn over four quarters. FDI liabilities were only USD 1.2–1.3bn a quarter in H1-26, so financing leans on portfolio and other flows (IMF BOP, High). The FY26 deficit is projected at 2.6–2.8% of GDP (TCMB, Jul-26).
- USD/TRY was 48.65 in Sep-26, up from 44.80 in Apr-26. The BIS REER z-score is +0.35, so there is no overvaluation signal (BIS, High).

### Fiscal
- Central government debt was 29.5% of GDP at Q2-26. The FY26 deficit is tracking about 5.2% of GDP, flagged as approximate (HMB, Medium). Gross external debt was USD 539.0bn (31.8% of GDP) at 30-Jun-26 (HMB, High).
- pb* and interest-to-revenue are not computed because no sourced effective interest rate or primary balance was found.

### Fund crisis and banks
- SPK ordered 131 funds from seven managers into liquidation on 17–18-Sep-26. The funds hold about USD 18.3bn (TL890bn) (Daily Sabah; Yeni Şafak, Sep-26, Medium).
- 17 funds were settled on 08-Oct-26, with a TL1m per-investor cap. The Vice President said funds carry no state guarantee and TMSF covers banks only (Daily Sabah, 12-Oct-26, Medium).
- S&P said on 02-Oct-26 that the process is unlikely to pressure the rating (Türkiye Today, 02-Oct-26, Medium).
- No source shows bank balance-sheet exposure (open item 7).

### Banking
- Deposit-taker CAR fell to 16.52% in Q1-26 from 19.69% in Q4-25, and Tier 1 to 14.04% from 16.89%. NPL was 2.48% and ROE 24.79% (IMF FSI, High, one quarter). This predates the fund event. The cause is not established and could be a reporting change.
