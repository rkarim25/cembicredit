## View
- Turkey is a BB-/Ba3 sovereign with stable outlooks across Fitch, S&P and Moody's, and low public debt (29.5% of GDP at Q2-26). Policy is tight: 37% against 29.73% CPI in Sep-26 (High).
- Disinflation continues but is slow. The consensus path is about 100bp of cuts at 22-Oct-26 and 10-Dec-26 (Medium).
- The reserve trend has turned negative. Gross reserves incl. gold fell to USD 167.4bn in the week to 02-Oct-26, from USD 188.2bn on 28-Aug-26, a USD 20.8bn fall in five weeks (Medium).
- The fund liquidation (131 funds, about USD 18.3bn) is the main near-term risk. S&P (02-Oct-26) sees it as isolated. Payouts have started: 17 funds were settled on 08-Oct-26 (Medium).
- The book is mostly Tier 2 bank bonds (Reza, 07-Oct-26). They are the most exposed to a liquidity or fund-linked bank shock. Bank exposure to the funds is unverified.
- The macro view is Medium confidence. Market pricing is unsourced, so there is no positioning call (Low).
- The next dated catalysts are S&P on 16-Oct-26 (hold expected) and the MPC on 22-Oct-26.

## Analysis

### Ratings and calendar
- Fitch affirmed BB-/Stable on 17-Jul-26, with 2026 growth of 2.8% and end-2026 CPI of 29.5%. It flags an early-election risk for late 2027 (Voice of Emirates, 18-Jul-26, Medium).
- Moody's completed a periodic review at Ba3/Stable on 24-Jul-26 with no action (Hürriyet Daily News, Jul-26, Medium).
- S&P affirmed BB-/B, stable, on 17-Apr-26. Its next scheduled review is 16-Oct-26, with a hold expected. On 02-Oct-26 it said the fund process is unlikely to pressure the rating (Türkiye Today, 02-Oct-26, Medium).
- Fitch's outlook history in 2026 is reported inconsistently. A Positive outlook in Jan-26 is reported by one source, and Stable is confirmed from Jul-26 (Medium).
- Dates: S&P 16-Oct-26, MPC 22-Oct-26, MPC 10-Dec-26.

### Inflation and monetary policy
- Sep-26 CPI was 29.73% y/y (+1.84% m/m) and core CPI-C 28.70% y/y, against 31.51% in Aug-26 (TurkStat, 03-Oct-26, High).
- The 37.00% policy rate was held at the 10-Sep-26 MPC, the fifth hold (TCMB; Central Banking, Sep-26, High/Medium). The ex-post real rate is about 7.3pp. The data record's ex-ante figure is 12.5pp, on 24.5% expectations (TCMB survey, Sep-26, Medium).
- Consensus expects 100bp cuts at each of the last two meetings. BBVA Research expects 36% in October (Intellinews, Sep-26, Medium).
- The rate instrument is unresolved (open item 2).

### External position
- Gross reserves incl. gold were USD 167.4bn in the week to 02-Oct-26, down USD 3.8bn. FX was USD 60.3bn (from USD 61.6bn) and gold USD 107.1bn (from USD 109.7bn) (Turkiye Today, Oct-26, Medium).
- The 02-Oct-26 level is USD 20.8bn below 28-Aug-26 (USD 188.2bn). Part of the fall is gold revaluation and part is FX, so the fall is not all intervention. The cause is unproven (Low).
- Reserves net of one-year drains were USD 137.1bn at Aug-26 (IMF template, High but dated). Short-term external debt by remaining maturity was USD 170.7bn at 30-Jun-26, and GEFR is about USD 208bn, or 119% of the 18-Sep-26 gross figure (TCMB/HMB, Jun-26, High).
- The current account over the last four quarters was USD -38.9bn (IMF BOP, High). The FY26 deficit is projected at 2.6–2.8% of GDP (TCMB, Jul-26).
- USD/TRY was 48.65 at Sep-26, with depreciation of about 1.6% a month (BIS, High). The 10-year REER z-score is +0.31, so the lira is not overvalued on that measure.

### Fiscal
- Central government debt was 29.5% of GDP at Q2-26. The FY26 deficit is tracking about 5.2% of GDP (HMB, High/Medium). Gross external debt was USD 539.0bn (31.8% of GDP) at 30-Jun-26 (HMB, High).
- Moody's cites a low debt burden. Fitch sees debt at about 25% of GDP by 2028 (Medium). pb* and interest-to-revenue are not computed because no sourced r or primary balance was found.

### Fund crisis and banks
- SPK ordered 131 funds from seven managers into liquidation on 17–18-Sep-26. The funds hold about USD 18.3bn (TL890bn), 7.7% of fund portfolio value (Yeni Şafak; Daily Sabah, Sep-26, Medium). Investors number about 350,000 in the press, against 455,758 in the earlier capture, so the count is unresolved.
- Payments to about 44,000 investors in 17 funds (A1 Capital, Bulls, Pardus) were made on 08-Oct-26. Payouts are capped at TL1m per investor. Vice President Yılmaz stated that funds carry no state guarantee and TMSF covers banks only (Daily Sabah, 12-Oct-26, Medium).
- Is Bankasi handles the Tera Portfoy funds and Ziraat handles the A1 Capital funds and others (Daily Sabah, Oct-26, Medium). Asset sales are awaiting buyers (Enterprise, 05-Oct-26, Medium).
- Borsa Istanbul had its worst month since 2008 in Sep-26 (Daily Sabah, Oct-26, Medium).
- No source shows bank balance-sheet exposure. This matters most for the Tier 2 holdings (open item 7).

### Banking
- Deposit-taker CAR fell to 16.52% in Q1-26 from 19.69% in Q4-25, and Tier 1 to 14.04% from 16.89%. NPL was 2.48% (IMF FSI, High, one quarter). The cause is not established.
