## View
- Neutral to modestly constructive on hard-currency Turkey at BB-/Ba3, all three agencies Stable. No market levels are in hand, so the view is not priced against any spread (Medium).
- Drivers: orthodox policy, with the repo rate at 37.00% against CPI of 29.73% (Sep-26) and a 12.5pp ex-ante real rate; central government debt of 29.5% of GDP; a narrow FY26 current-account deficit of 2.6% to 2.8% of GDP (TurkStat, TCMB, HMB, High to Medium).
- Reserve adequacy is better than the previous version stated. The $61.56B (25-Sep-26) is FX ex gold, not gross. Gross incl. gold was $174.4B at 18-Sep-26, about 1.02x short-term external debt of $170.7B and about 0.84x the $208B GEFR (my arithmetic; Medium).
- Net reserves ex swaps are unknown. That is the main gap in the external view.
- Weak points: a deficit of about 5.2% of GDP; $170.7B of short-term external debt; a $13.8B fall in gross reserves from 28-Aug-26 to 18-Sep-26, unexplained.
- Biggest risk: a domestic confidence shock from the liquidation of 131 funds (over USD 18bn, 455,758 investors), hitting bank liquidity, dollarisation and reserves together. Sources are tier 3 (Low).
- Overall confidence: Medium. High on levels from official sources, Low on market pricing and the fund crisis.

## Analysis

### External position
- Gross external debt was $539.0B, 31.8% of GDP, at 30-Jun-26 (HMB, 30-Jun-26, High). Implied GDP is about $1,695B against the IMF WEO 2026E $1,640B; the denominators differ (my arithmetic, Medium).
- Short-term external debt by remaining maturity, all sectors, was $170.7B at 30-Jun-26 (TCMB, 30-Jun-26, High). The sovereign share is not known.
- GEFR for the next 12 months is about $208B (TCMB/HMB, Jun-26, Medium; underlying components not in hand).
- Reserve definitions, which must not be mixed:

- The dashboard figure "GEFR/reserves 337.9%" uses the ex-gold number and overstates stress. Gold is liquid but is valued at market, and swaps are unknown, so net reserves ex swaps are the correct adequacy measure (open item 1).
- Gross reserves rose 4.7% over twelve months to Aug-26 (IMF, High). The $13.8B fall to 18-Sep-26 partly reflects gold valuation (gold rose from $100.6B to $117.1B Jul to Aug-26). The decomposition is not available (Low).
- The current account was a $36M surplus in Jul-26. The FY26 deficit is projected at 2.6% to 2.8% of GDP (TCMB, Jul-26, High on the print; Medium on the projection).
- FDI was 0.81% of GDP in 2025 (World Bank, High). Financing leans on portfolio flows and bank rollovers.
- The lira crawled from 44.80 (Apr-26) to 48.65 (Sep-26) per USD, about 8.6% (BIS, High). The BIS broad REER is 108.09 (Aug-26), z-score +0.31 against the ten-year mean (BIS, High). The currency is not overvalued by its own history.

### Monetary policy
- CPI was 29.73% YoY (+1.84% MoM) in Sep-26, core CPI-C 28.70% (TurkStat, 03-Oct-26, High). The IMF series shows 31.51% for Aug-26, down from 32.61% in May-26.
- The 1-week repo rate was 37.00%, held at the Sep-26 MPC (TCMB, Sep-26, High). The BIS series shows 37.0% from Apr-26 to Sep-26; the IMF series shows 35.5% in every month. The IMF series is probably a different instrument. The definition is unstated (open item 8).
- 12-month inflation expectations are about 24.5% (TCMB Survey of Market Participants, Sep-26, Medium; the document states 24-25%). The ex-ante real rate is 12.5pp.
- Disinflation is slow: monthly CPI is still 1.84%, and services stickiness is cited by the previous run (Medium). The policy-error risk is an early cut. The MPC date and consensus are unverified (open item 10).

### Fiscal
- Central government debt was 29.5% of GDP at Q2-26 (HMB, 30-Jun-26, High).
- The budget deficit is tracking about 5.2% of GDP for FY26 (HMB, Aug-26, Medium). The figure is "approximate". The earthquake-reconstruction attribution has no primary source in hand.
- The stock is low and the flow is wide, so debt rises on current settings. Primary balance, interest-to-revenue and the debt-stabilising balance are not computable from the brief. The previous version's "pb* about -1.2% of GDP" is unverified and withdrawn (open item 13).
- Contingent liabilities are open: the fund liquidation and bank capital (below).

### Banking and the fund liquidation
- Deposit-taker FSIs, Q4-25 to Q1-26 (IMF, High): CAR 19.69% to 16.52%; Tier 1 16.89% to 14.04%; NPL 2.30% to 2.48%; provisions/NPL 74.77% to 75.75%; ROE 24.79% in Q1-26; FX open position +1.14% to -1.6% of capital. Capital remains above typical minima, but a 3.2pp one-quarter fall is unexplained (open item 12).
- SPK ordered 131 funds liquidated, over USD 18bn and 455,758 investors (run 2026-10-07_2235, tier 3, Low). Liquidator banks, asset mix and redemption terms are not known. If fund assets are mostly liquid government paper, the sovereign impact is limited. If redemptions convert to FX demand, reserves are exposed (open item 9).

## Where the models disagreed

1. Reserve level. The previous version used $61.56B as "gross"; the data record and the 07-Oct-26 run show it is FX ex gold and that gross incl. gold is $174.4B to $188.2B. Decided by tier 2 data with explicit definitions over the unqualified label. Adequacy remains open pending net reserves (open item 1).
