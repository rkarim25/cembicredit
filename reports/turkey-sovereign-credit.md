## View
- Neutral to modestly constructive on hard-currency Turkey at BB-/Ba3, all Stable. No rating catalyst is scheduled in the next 90 days (Medium).
- The TCMB cut the repo rate by 100 bp to 36.00% on 22-Oct-26. Sep-26 CPI of 29.73% was the first print below 30% since Nov-21 (Hürriyet Daily News, 22-Oct-26, High on the decision). The ex-ante real rate is still about 11.5pp after the cut (my arithmetic, Medium).
- This is a liquidity and credibility credit, not a solvency one. Central government debt is 29.5% of GDP and external debt 31.8% (HMB, High). The FY26 deficit is tracking about 5.2% of GDP (Medium).
- The external buffer is thinner than in August but is stabilising. Gross reserves were USD 171.6bn at 15-Oct-26, the first rise after six weekly declines (search summary of TCMB data, Medium-Low). The 4-quarter current account deficit is USD 38.9bn (IMF BOP, High). Net reserve quality is unresolved (open item 1).
- Fiscal slippage is a watch item. The Sep-26 central government deficit was TRY 532.2bn against TRY 309.6bn a year earlier, with spending +34.1% and revenue +22.7% y/y (Alo Maliye, 15-Oct-26, Medium).
- Biggest risk: oil and Hormuz. Brent was 111 on 22-Oct-26, which widens the current account, drains reserves and stalls disinflation. Second risk: domestic confidence after the fund liquidation.
- Overall confidence: Medium. High on official levels, Low on market pricing and net reserves.

## What changed
- The 22-Oct-26 MPC is now verified as a 100 bp cut to 36.00%, with the corridor at 39.00% / 34.50% (Hürriyet Daily News, 22-Oct-26, read directly). The previous version only expected it. Old open item 7 is closed.
- The cut to 36% was not a pure surprise. The ex-ante real rate falls from about 12.5pp to about 11.5pp. The view stays neutral to modestly constructive.
- New reserve print: gross USD 171.6bn at 15-Oct-26, from USD 167.4bn. The source is a search summary, so it is Medium-Low until confirmed.
- New fiscal data: the Sep-26 budget deficit and the spending/revenue split.
- Ratings: S&P's 16-Oct-26 slot is reported by Foreks as no new assessment. The previous version said "no change". It is now treated as "no review published", and the primary release is still not read. Fitch and Moody's are unchanged.
- Added Brent at 111 (22-Oct-26) as the main external risk. The IMF Oct-WEO assumes USD 95.74 for 2026 and USD 87.39 for 2027.
- Not closed: Eurobond list, EMBI weights, IMF status, bond pricing, net reserves ex swaps, fund-liquidation bank exposure.

## Analysis

### Ratings
- S&P BB-/Stable, affirmed 17-Apr-26. The 16-Oct-26 slot passed without a new assessment (Foreks, 17-Oct-26, Medium). S&P also said on 02-Oct-26 that the fund probe would not pressure the rating (press, Medium).
- Fitch BB-/Stable, affirmed 17-Jul-26 (Hürriyet Daily News, 18-Jul-26, Medium). Fitch projects 2.8% growth, 29.5% CPI at end-2026 and reserves of USD 167bn at end-2026. It flags a possible early election in late 2027.
- Moody's Ba3/Stable, last action Jan-26. A periodic review on 24-Jul-26 produced no action (Hürriyet Daily News, Jul-26, Medium).
- Next scheduled agency dates are not confirmed beyond Jan-27 (Forbes Türkiye calendar, 2026, Medium-Low).

### Monetary policy
- The 22-Oct-26 decision cut the one-week repo rate to 36.00%. The MPC cited weak domestic demand and elevated energy prices. It said policy would be tightened "in case of a significant and persistent deterioration in the inflation outlook", with decisions meeting by meeting and a 5% medium-term target (Hürriyet Daily News, 22-Oct-26, High on the decision).
- CPI was 29.73% y/y and core 28.70% in Sep-26 (TurkStat, 03-Oct-26, High). The TCMB year-end forecast is 28% (Intellinews, Sep-26, Medium). The Oct-26 survey shows 29.63% year-end and 23.89% 12-month expectations (Hürriyet Daily News, date unconfirmed, Medium-Low).
- Real rate: 36.00% less 23.89% expected is about 12pp. Less 29.73% trailing is about 6pp. The 12.5pp in the data record is the pre-cut Sep-26 measure.
- The 10-Dec-26 MPC is the last of 2026. The pace of cuts there is unconfirmed (open item 7). The policy risk is easing faster than reserves rebuild.

### External position
- Gross external debt was USD 539.0bn, 31.8% of GDP, at 30-Jun-26 (HMB, High). Short-term external debt by remaining maturity was USD 170.7bn (TCMB, High). GEFR is about USD 208bn (TCMB/HMB, Jun-26, Medium).
- Reserve measures, which must not be mixed:

| Gross incl. gold | USD 174.4bn | 18-Sep-26 | TCMB, Medium |
| Gross incl. gold | USD 167.4bn (FX 60.3bn, gold 107.1bn) | week to 02-Oct-26 | TCMB via Türkiye Today, Medium |
| Gross incl. gold | USD 171.6bn | 15-Oct-26 | Search summary of TCMB, Medium-Low |
| Net (definition unclear) | USD 57.7bn, from 53.8bn | 15-Oct-26 | Search summary, Low |
| Gross incl. gold | USD 186.7bn | Aug-26 | IMF, High |
| Net of predetermined 1y drains | USD 137.1bn | Aug-26 | IMF template, High |

- Cover (my arithmetic, Medium): USD 171.6bn is about 1.0x short-term debt (USD 170.7bn) and about 0.82x GEFR (USD 208bn). The net figure of USD 57.7bn is about 0.34x short-term debt. It rests on a Low source and an unclear definition.
- The current account was USD -23.6bn in 2026-Q1 and USD -10.9bn in 2026-Q2, USD -38.9bn over four quarters (IMF BOP, High). TCMB projects 2.6% to 2.8% of GDP for 2026 (Jul-26, Medium). FDI liabilities were USD 1.2-1.3bn a quarter in 2026 (IMF, High), so financing leans on portfolio flows and bank rollovers (my inference, Medium).
- S&P named Turkey among the most exposed to the oil shock (S&P via fi-desk, 28-Apr-26, Medium). Brent was 111.11 on 22-Oct-26 (Trading Economics, Medium).
- The lira moved from 44.80 (Apr-26) to 48.65 (Sep-26), about 8.6% depreciation against CPI near 30%. The BIS broad REER is 108.26 (Sep-26) with a 10-year z-score of +0.35 (BIS, High). It is not stretched.

### Fiscal
- Central government debt was 29.5% of GDP at Q2-26 (HMB, High). The FY26 deficit is tracking about 5.2% of GDP (HMB, Aug-26, Medium).
- Sep-26 central government deficit was TRY 532.2bn (TRY 309.6bn a year earlier). The primary deficit was TRY 269.2bn. Spending rose 34.1% (current transfers +48.7%) and revenue 22.7% (Ministry of Finance data via Alo Maliye, 15-Oct-26, Medium). Year-to-date figures against the Medium-Term Programme and interest-to-revenue are not computed (open item 11).
- Market access: EUR 2bn 8-year Eurobond due Mar-34 at 5.15%, EUR 6.5bn orders (AGBI, Feb-26, Medium). Pricing after September is not sourced.

### Banking and the fund liquidation
- Deposit-taker FSIs, Q4-25 to Q1-26 (IMF, High): CAR 19.69% to 16.52%, Tier 1 16.89% to 14.04%, NPL 2.30% to 2.48%, ROE 24.79%. The CAR fall is unexplained (open item 10).
- SPK ordered 131 funds (about USD 18.3bn, TL890bn) liquidated on 17-18 Sep-26. 17 funds settled on 08-Oct-26, with payouts capped at TL1m per investor. Is Bankasi and Ziraat are named as liquidators for some funds (Daily Sabah, 12-Oct-26, Medium). Borsa Istanbul had its worst month since 2008 in Sep-26 (Medium).
- Judgement: a domestic confidence risk. It becomes a sovereign event only if state banks fund payouts at scale or redemptions turn into FX demand (Low, bank exposure unknown).

## Where the models disagreed
No material disagreement; only one position was submitted, and no rebuttals exist. Two points were resolved:
1. Net reserves. The position quoted 57.7bn as net and 44.2bn as net ex swaps. The search I ran labels 57.7bn as "excluding swaps" and shows no 44.2bn. Neither is above tier 3. The point is left open (item 1) and the 44.2bn figure is not used. Rule: no source above tier 4 means an open item.
2. S&P status. The position says S&P "skipped" its review. The previous document said "no change". Foreks reports no new assessment, so "no review published" is used. Resolved by the more specific source, with the primary release still open.
