## View
- Neutral on hard-currency Turkey at BB-/Ba3, all Stable. The tone is less constructive than in the 27-Oct-26 version because disinflation has stalled and reserves are lower. No rating catalyst is scheduled in the next 90 days (Medium).
- October CPI was 29.77% y/y and 2.58% m/m, against 29.73% in September and a 29.56% consensus (TurkStat via RTÉ and Daily Sabah, 03-Nov-26, High). The repo rate is 36.00% after the 100 bp cut on 22-Oct-26. The ex-ante real rate is about 12pp on the 23.89% expectation and about 6pp on trailing CPI (my arithmetic, Medium).
- This is a liquidity and credibility credit, not a solvency one. Central government debt is 29.5% of GDP and external debt 31.8% (HMB, High). The FY26 deficit is tracking about 5.2% of GDP (Medium).
- The external buffer is thinner. Gross reserves incl. gold were USD 162.2bn at 23-Oct-26, down USD 26bn from 28-Aug-26. That is second-hand, and about 0.95x short-term external debt (Medium-Low). The four-quarter current account deficit is USD 38.9bn (IMF BOP, High).
- Biggest risk: oil and Hormuz. Brent was USD 112.44 on 30-Oct-26 and S&P names Turkey among the most exposed importers. This widens the current account, drains reserves and delays disinflation. Second risk: domestic confidence after the fund liquidation.
- Overall confidence: Medium. High on official levels, Low on market pricing and net reserves.

## Analysis

### Ratings
- S&P BB-/Stable, affirmed 17-Apr-26. No new assessment was published at the 16-Oct-26 slot (Foreks, 17-Oct-26, Medium). S&P said on 02-Oct-26 that the fund probe would not pressure the rating (press, Medium).
- Fitch BB-/Stable, affirmed 17-Jul-26 (Hürriyet Daily News, 18-Jul-26, Medium). Fitch forecasts 2.8% growth, 29.5% CPI at end-2026 and reserves of USD 167bn at end-2026, and flags a possible early election in late 2027.
- Moody's Ba3/Stable, last action Jan-26 (day unconfirmed). A periodic review on 24-Jul-26 produced no action. Moody's forecasts 29% inflation at end-2026 and 3.4% growth (Hürriyet Daily News, Jul-26, Medium).
- Next scheduled agency dates are not confirmed beyond Jan-27 (Forbes Türkiye calendar, 2026, Medium-Low).

### Inflation and monetary policy
- October CPI was 29.77% y/y, 2.58% m/m, against 29.56% expected. September was 29.73% y/y and 1.84% m/m (TurkStat via RTÉ and Daily Sabah, 03-Nov-26, High). Fitch (29.5%) and Moody's (29%) end-2026 forecasts imply little further decline.
- The 22-Oct-26 MPC cut the one-week repo rate by 100 bp to 36.00%, with the corridor at 39.00% / 34.50%. It cited weak domestic demand and energy prices, and said it would tighten "in case of a significant and persistent deterioration in the inflation outlook" (Hürriyet Daily News, 22-Oct-26, High on the decision).
- The Oct-26 survey shows 23.89% 12-month expectations (Hürriyet Daily News, date unconfirmed, Medium-Low). Real rate: about 12pp on expectations and about 6pp on trailing CPI (my arithmetic, Medium).
- The 10-Dec-26 MPC is the last of 2026. An above-consensus October print makes a further 100 bp cut harder to defend. The policy risk is easing faster than reserves rebuild, or a pause that damages credibility. The market reaction is unsourced (open item 6).

### External position

- Cover (my arithmetic, Medium): USD 162.2bn is about 0.95x short-term debt (USD 170.7bn) and about 0.78x GEFR (USD 208bn). The fall of USD 26.0bn from 28-Aug-26 to 23-Oct-26 is about 14%. The data record shows a 12-month change of -7.8% to Sep-26. Fitch's end-2026 forecast of USD 167bn is already above the latest print.
- Part of the fall is gold valuation or sales: gold was USD 117.1bn in Aug-26 and about USD 103bn in late Oct-26. The split between FX sales and gold price is not sourced (open item 1).
- Current account: USD -23.6bn in 2026-Q1 and USD -10.9bn in 2026-Q2, USD -38.9bn over four quarters (IMF BOP, High). TCMB projects 2.6% to 2.8% of GDP for 2026 (Jul-26, Medium). FDI liabilities were USD 1.2-1.3bn a quarter in 2026, so financing leans on portfolio flows and bank rollovers (my inference, Medium). Rollover behaviour is not evidenced.
- Brent was USD 112.44 on 30-Oct-26, +13% in October (Trading Economics, Financefeeds, Medium). The IMF Oct-WEO assumes USD 95.74 for 2026 and USD 87.39 for 2027. S&P named Turkey among the most exposed to the oil shock (S&P via fi-desk, 28-Apr-26, Medium).
- The lira moved from 44.80 (Apr-26) to 48.65 (Sep-26), about 8.6% depreciation against CPI near 30%. The BIS broad REER is 108.26 (Sep-26) with a 10-year z-score of +0.35 (BIS, High). It is not stretched.

### Fiscal
- Central government debt was 29.5% of GDP at Q2-26 (HMB, High). The FY26 deficit is tracking about 5.2% of GDP (HMB, Aug-26, Medium).
- The Sep-26 central government deficit was TRY 532.2bn (TRY 309.6bn a year earlier), with a primary deficit of TRY 269.2bn. Spending rose 34.1% (current transfers +48.7%) and revenue 22.7% (Ministry of Finance via Alo Maliye, 15-Oct-26, Medium). The Medium-Term Programme path, the 2027 budget and interest-to-revenue are not computed (open item 10).
- Market access: EUR 2bn 8-year Eurobond due Mar-34 at 5.15%, EUR 6.5bn orders (AGBI, Feb-26, Medium). No newer issue was found, and pricing after September is not sourced.

### Banking and the fund liquidation
- Deposit-taker FSIs, Q4-25 to Q1-26 (IMF, High): CAR 19.69% to 16.52%, Tier 1 16.89% to 14.04%, NPL 2.30% to 2.48%, ROE 24.79%. The CAR fall is unexplained (open item 9).
- SPK ordered 131 funds (about USD 18.3bn, TL890bn) liquidated on 17-18 Sep-26. 17 funds settled on 08-Oct-26, with payouts capped at TL1m per investor. Is Bankasi and Ziraat are named as liquidators for some funds (Daily Sabah, 12-Oct-26, Medium). Borsa Istanbul had its worst month since 2008 in Sep-26 (Medium).
- Judgement: this is a domestic confidence risk. It becomes a sovereign event only if state banks fund payouts at scale or redemptions turn into FX demand (Low, bank exposure unknown).
