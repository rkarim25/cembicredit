## View
- Neutral. The A1 / A+ ratings recorded in the repo rest on Aramco's 70% ownership and the Saudi sovereign. I could not verify the current agency ratings or outlooks.
- Standalone credit weakened in 2026. Q2-26 adjusted EBITDA was about $0.90bn (down 18% q/q), adjusted EBIT about $110m (down 72% q/q) and the adjusted net loss about $0.10bn (MEED, 29-Jul-26, Medium-High).
- The balance sheet is the largest unresolved point. Company-reported net debt of about $0.73bn at 30-Jun-26 and the repo's $6.67bn at 2024A cannot both be on the same definition. Until open item 2 is closed, leverage figures should not be quoted.
- Disposals of about $950m EV (Europe petrochemicals to AEQUITA, ETP to Mutares) help liquidity and remove loss-making assets, but are small against the earnings shortfall.
- About $880m of H1-26 distributions exceeded H1 earnings. This favours Aramco over creditors.
- Biggest risk: the regional conflict and supply disruption cited for the Q2 loss, combined with a petrochemical oversupply cycle. Aramco and sovereign support limit the rating downside but do not remove the earnings risk.
- Relative value cannot be judged without a current Bloomberg level.
- Confidence: Low-Medium. The view rests mainly on secondary press and unread primary filings, and the repo financials need rebuilding.

## Analysis

### Operating performance
- Q2-26 revenue was $6.62bn (down 5% q/q), adjusted EBITDA $0.90bn (down 18% q/q) and adjusted EBIT $110m (down 72% q/q). The company attributes the result to the regional conflict (MEED, 29-Jul-26, https://www.meed.com/sabic-posts-100m-loss-in-q2-2026-due-to-regional-conflict, Medium-High).
- Net loss attributable to shareholders was reported at SAR 833m (about $222m) in a search summary of the release. It is on a different basis from the adjusted loss of about $0.10bn, and the primary release was not read (Saudi Exchange filing, 29-Jul-26, Medium).
- MEED reports a 2025 full-year loss of about $6.87bn. The cause (impairment versus operations) is unsourced and absent from the repo (MEED, Low-Medium).
- Q1-26 profit was about $3.5m (MEED, 29-Jul-26, Medium).

### Balance sheet and the data conflict
- Company net debt was SAR 2.73bn ($0.73bn) at 30-Jun-26, versus SAR 2.77bn ($0.74bn) at 31-Mar-26 (MEED / Q2-26 release summary, Low-Medium).
- The repo shows 2024A gross debt $11,020m, cash $4,350m and net debt $6,670m, with net leverage 1.15x (`database/issuers/sabic.json`, 19-Sep-26, tier 2, not tied to filings).
- Possible explanations are a different definition (leases, broader cash and deposits) or changes after the 2025 loss and disposals. None is verified.
- The repo history is not safe as a credit basis, for these reasons:
  - Reconciliation variance is exactly 1.5% every year.
  - The commentary is generic.
  - The repo FCF of -$399m to -$2,151m in 2022–24 is driven by large working-capital builds.
  - Cognitive Credit does not cover the issuer.
- A filings-based model is needed, built from the FY25 annual report and the Q2-26 interims. No cash EBITDA / FCF reconciliation was possible.

### Portfolio actions
- The combined EV of the Europe and Americas sales is $950m: AEQUITA for Europe petrochemicals ($500m) and Mutares for ETP ($450m) (Reuters via BusinessDay, 08-Jan-26, https://www.businessday.co.za/world/international-companies/2026-01-08-saudis-sabic-sells-assets-in-europe-americas-worth-950m/, Medium-High).
- The ETP sale to Mutares completed in August. The European Commission cleared AEQUITA's sole control of SABIC Europe, and closing is expected in 4Q26 (Enterprise AM, 30-Sep-26, https://enterpriseam.com/ksa/2026/09/30/sabic-is-almost-out-of-europe/, Medium-High).

### Ownership, ratings and distributions
- Moody's affirmed A1 and withdrew the BCA after Aramco bought 70% (Yahoo Finance, 20-Jul-20, https://finance.yahoo.com/news/saudi-basic-industries-corporation-moodys-141008938.html, Medium because dated).
- Search summaries of SABIC's ratings page gave S&P A+ stable (Mar-25) and Fitch A+ stable (Oct-25). The page itself (https://www.sabic.com/en/investors/corporate-credit-ratings) could not be loaded, and the Moody's detail was garbled. No 2026 rating action was found (Low).
- H1-26 distributions of about $880m were announced while Q1 profit was about $3.5m and Q2 was a loss (MEED, 29-Jul-26, Medium).

### Not covered
- The Saudi sovereign context (ratings, reserves, fiscal) was not searched this pass.
- Bond pricing is the repo's 19-Sep-26 level: 4.50% 2028 at 98.8, YTM 5.05%, spread 100bp. It is unsourced and unverified.
- The debt maturity schedule, RCF terms and covenants, and supply-chain financing are unsourced.
