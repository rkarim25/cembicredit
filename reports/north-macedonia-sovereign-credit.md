## View
No investment view is published this run, because no figure was sourced to tier 1 or 2.
The structure is consistent across both positions: a small open economy with a de facto euro peg, a crossover rating, EU accession as the political anchor, and a motorway capex programme (Corridors 8 and 10D) as the fiscal pressure point (all Low).
Gemini asserts BB+/BB- ratings, €5.1bn reserves, debt at about 55% of GDP and 2026 refinancing already done. The judge cannot verify these, so they stay as hypotheses to confirm (Low).
Peg and reserve stress is the least likely risk. The most plausible risks are political drift from stalled EU accession and fiscal slippage from the capex programme.
Biggest unknown: the Mar-27 maturity and any pre-funding, plus the post-2026 fiscal path.
Confidence in the overall view: Low. Treat this run as a scoping pass.

### Unverified claims, carried as hypotheses (Low)
- **Ratings:** Fitch BB+ Stable (11-Sep-26) and S&P BB- Stable (02-Oct-26), per Gemini only. A one-notch agency split would be material for index and fund eligibility, so it must be confirmed.
- **External:** FX reserves of €5,101m (Aug-26), policy rate 4.25%, and a 61.5 MKD/EUR peg. The peg level is consistent with long-standing practice but is not sourced here.
- **Fiscal:** debt/GDP of 55.2% (Q1-26), with an expectation of drift toward 60%. The perimeter is not stated, and the Gemini summary ("55-60%") does not match its single data point.
- **Capex:** Corridor 8/10D motorways with Bechtel-Enka, contract value of about €1.3bn. No document is cited.
- **Eurobonds:** a €500m 3.675% bond due 03-Jun-26 (XS2181690665), a Jan-26 dual-tranche issue of about €1.0bn, and a €500m 6.96% bond due 13-Mar-27 (XS2582522681). Gemini's rebuttal gives an issue date of 13-Mar-23 and a Dec-26 call date. Both models agree the 03-Jun-26 bond has matured, so pricing it is moot.
- **IMF:** PLL concluded Nov-24, per Gemini only.

### Framework tests to apply once data is in hand
- The primary balance against the debt-stabilising balance, and interest/revenue against the 20% warning level (knowledge/frameworks/sovereign-credit.md, Low as method).
- Reserves net of swaps and short-term debt, compared with the 2027 external amortisation.
- The size of the Mar-27 maturity relative to reserves, and whether it is pre-funded.
