The working structure to test once sourced: an IMF programme (EFF plus RSF) anchors policy, and Gulf and Chinese rollovers cover external gaps.
Binding constraints to size: interest absorbing federal revenue, thin net reserve cover, and EMBI-relevant Eurobond amortisation.
Biggest risk to test: programme slippage (revenue, energy arrears, provincial fiscal discipline), which would put bilateral rollovers at risk.
Second risk: security and political shocks that the IMF framework does not capture.

## Analysis
No claim below is verified in this run. Each is tier 4 and Low until a primary source is attached.

### Official sector and IMF
- Recalled: an EFF of about USD 7bn (37 months, approved Sep-24) and an RSF of about USD 1.4bn (approved May-25) (IMF press releases as recalled; not re-verified, Low). Review status, next review date and DSA are unknown (open item 5).

### Ratings
- Recalled: S&P B-, Fitch B-, Moody's Caa1 as of the last actions remembered, after upgrades from 2023 distress levels (agency releases as recalled, Low). Current levels and outlooks are unverified (open item 1).

### External
- Recalled direction: the current account moved from large deficits to near balance or a small deficit, helped by remittances and import compression. Reserves were rebuilt from a very low base but cover is thin against debt service (SBP and IMF documents as recalled, Low). No figures can be stated.
- Saudi, UAE and Chinese deposits and rollovers are the main bilateral support and need repeated renewal (press and IMF financing assurances as recalled, Low).
- Framework tests to run once data is sourced: gross external financing requirement against net reserves, and short-term external debt by remaining maturity (`knowledge/frameworks/sovereign-credit.md`).

### Fiscal
- The programme requires a primary surplus, and interest to revenue is the key stress ratio (the framework flags above 20% as a warning and above 30% as the default zone). The pb* gap and interest to revenue cannot be computed without MoF Fiscal Operations data (open item 8).

### Monetary
- Recalled: inflation fell sharply from the 2023 peak and the SBP eased from 22% (SBP MPC statements as recalled, Low). Ex-ante real policy rate is not computable until the policy rate and forward CPI are sourced (open item 9).

### Politics and security
- Military-civil balance, opposition politics and the Afghan and Indian borders are the likeliest source of a shock outside the programme (qualitative judgement, Low).

### Data gaps
- No news search was possible, so nothing is established for the last 90 days. The searched-and-found-nothing statement cannot be made. Paywalled items are not identified.
- No Bloomberg data was supplied: spreads, yields, CDS, index weight.
