## View
- Provisional view: a small, low-income, remittance-dependent sovereign with a single hard-currency Eurobond, the 2027 bullet. No trading conclusion is supported until the open items are filled.
- This run had no web research and no new captures. Every claim below rests on prior knowledge (tier 4) and is Low confidence unless stated.
- Key drivers: remittances (concentrated on Russia), gold and aluminium export receipts, multilateral and Chinese official financing, and reserves against the 2027 bullet.
- Biggest risk: a Russian economic or migration shock, or sanctions-related banking friction, cutting FX inflows and weakening the somoni just before the maturity.
- Willingness to pay is probably not the binding constraint. Ability, data transparency and the refinancing route are.
- Near-term political risk is low but not zero, because the succession arrangement is a tail risk.
- Confidence in the overall view: Low. Ratings, reserves, bond terms, IMF status and the debt composition are all unverified.

## Analysis
### Hard-currency debt
- The sovereign is believed to have one Eurobond: about USD 500m, 7.125%, maturing around Sep-27, issued in 2017 to fund Rogun (recollection, not verified, Low).
- Market access is likely closed or costly. Repayment therefore depends on reserves, official support and possible liability management (inference, Low).
- The next 12 months should be tracked for a buyback, an exchange or a pre-funding signal. None has been verified.

### External position
- The external account is carried by remittances and by gold and aluminium exports, with high concentration on Russia (structural knowledge, Medium on structure, Low on magnitudes).
- Remittance-to-GDP, the current account balance and the reserve level need NBT or World Bank data. None is stated here.
- No parallel-market premium or exchange-rate regime check was done.

### Fiscal and debt
- Public debt is likely mostly external and owed to official creditors (multilaterals, China Exim, bilaterals) (Low).
- The main off-balance-sheet risks are Rogun capex and state-owned enterprise liabilities (Barki Tojik, Talco) (Low).
- The debt-stabilising primary balance and interest-to-revenue cannot be computed without MoF and IMF data.

### Politics and willingness
- No Eurobond default is known to me. Willingness is not the constraint, but this is an inference (Low).
- Watch for arrears to contractors, delayed multilateral disbursements, and unrest in Gorno-Badakhshan or on the Afghan border.
