Q1 Where exactly does the 2030 Eurobond sit in the Zorlu capital structure, and how does structural subordination affect recovery? CAPITAL STRUCTURE HIERARCHY
▼

Understanding where the ZOREN 11.000% 2030 Senior Notes (ISIN: XS2926261426) sit in the legal and corporate hierarchy is the foundation of any credit workout thesis. Zorlu Enerji is not a simple operating business; it is a listed intermediate parent that holds diverse operating assets through dedicated subsidiaries.

📌 The Five Defining Indenture Realities for ZOREN 2030 Eurobond Holders:
  1. Direct Legal Obligor (Tier 2 Issuer): The notes are direct, senior, unsecured obligations of Zorlu Enerji Elektrik Üretim A.Ş. (the Borsa Istanbul-listed corporate parent). They are governed by English Law under a trust indenture (BNY Mellon as Trustee).
  2. The Structural Subordination Gap (No Upstream OpCo Guarantees): Unlike Western investment-grade utility issues, ZOREN's primary cash-generating subsidiaries—specifically Zorlu Yenilenebilir Enerji A.Ş. (which holds the 305 MW geothermal fleet) and Osmangazi Elektrik Dağıtım A.Ş. (OEDAŞ)—do NOT provide downstream upstream guarantees to the Eurobonds.
  3. Seniority of OpCo Project Debt ($380M–$543M): Creditors of the operating subsidiaries—specifically Multilateral DFIs ($115M ADB & EBRD) and Turkish Commercial Project Finance Syndicates ($265M Akbank, Garanti, İşbank)—hold direct 1st mortgages over power plants and statutory account pledges over EPİAŞ USD settlements. Under bankruptcy and contract law, all plant-level revenue is trapped and applied to service OpCo project debt before a single dollar can be upstreamed as dividends to ZOREN.
  4. Pari Passu Ranking at the Issuer Level ($1,491.2M Claims Pool): At the Zorlu Enerji corporate level, the $800M–$1,100M Eurobond claims rank strictly pari passu in right of payment with $228.0M in Local TL Corporate Bonds (ÖST) and $163.2M in Turkish bank bilateral credit lines.
  5. Insulation from Holding-Level Creditors (Non-Recourse to Zorlu Holding): Parent Zorlu Holding A.Ş. owns ~56.8% of ZOREN common equity. However, Zorlu Holding creditors have no direct contractual claim on ZOREN's assets. Their only recourse is to foreclose on the 56.8% share pledge—which immediately triggers the 101% Change of Control Put (§4.08) in favor of Eurobond holders.
The Strategic Restructuring Takeaway: Because the Eurobond is structurally subordinated to OpCo debt but contractually pari passu with local banks, Eurobond recovery depends entirely on de-leveraging the OpCo layer. Closing the AlJomaih $450M renewable stake sale eliminates the $115M DFI debt and pre-funds the 2027 Eurobond amortisation in segregated escrow, permanently removing the structural subordination wall and elevating Eurobonds to de facto priority.

Q4 How do domestic Turkish banks exercise super-priority over foreign Eurobonds in practice? LEGAL MECHANICS
▼

While foreign Eurobonds ($1,100M ZOREN 2030s) are governed by English law and rank pari passu in form with general unsecured obligations, domestic Turkish banks possess absolute practical and structural super-priority under Turkish jurisdiction:

1. Registered Commercial Enterprise Pledges (Ticari İşletme Rehni)

Turkish commercial banks (Akbank, Garanti BBVA, Türkiye İş Bankası, Halkbank, VakıfBank) hold registered notarized pledges over operating bank accounts where statutory YEKDEM hard-currency feed-in tariffs from EPİAŞ and retail collections from OEDAŞ are deposited daily. Banks execute automated debt service sweeps before cash ever reaches holding accounts.

2. BDDK Financial Restructuring Framework (FYY)

Under Turkish banking authority (BDDK) framework agreements (*Finansal Yeniden Yapılandırma*), domestic lenders form a statutory Consortium. If lenders representing 2/3 of bank claims agree, the agreement binds all Turkish institutions. BDDK rules grant local banks legal preference, standstill protections, and regulatory forbearance that offshore 144A/Reg S investors cannot contest.

3. Structural Subordination of HoldCo Eurobonds

Eurobonds were issued at the parent level without direct mortgages on geothermal steam turbines or transmission substations. Total Turkish bank debt of $428.2M plus multilateral DFI loans (ADB/EBRD) of $115.0M represents $543.2M in senior priority claims that sit directly between operational utility cash flows and Eurobond holders.

Restructuring Reality: In any workout, Turkish banks will not take a nominal principal haircut. They demand 100c par reinstatement or substantial equity stakes. Foreign Eurobond holders are the flexible tranche that absorbs maturity extensions and yield step-downs.
Q5 What are the four contractual and covenant cross-default triggers between Zorlu Holding and ZOREN Eurobonds? INDENTURE ANALYSIS
▼

Institutional investors often assume that ring-fenced utility subsidiaries are insulated from parent bankruptcy. For Zorlu Enerji, four legally enforceable indenture and regulatory triggers ensure immediate acceleration upon parent distress:

Trigger 1: Share Pledge Enforcement & Change of Control Put (101% of Par) INDENTURE §4.08

Zorlu Holding owns ~56.4% of ZOREN, but has pledged massive share blocks to Turkish banks as collateral for parent margin loans. If Holding defaults, banks foreclose on pledged shares. This drops Holding voting control below 50.0%, triggering Section 4.08 of the Eurobond Indenture (*Mandatory Change of Control Offer at 101% plus accrued interest*). Since ZOREN cannot fund $1.1B in cash, failure to close the put within 30 days becomes a non-curable Event of Default.

Trigger 2: Bank Cross-Acceleration Threshold ($40.0M Indenture Carve-out) INDENTURE §6.01(e)

Under Section 6.01(e), default on or acceleration of any Indebtedness of the Issuer or any Significant Subsidiary exceeding $40.0M constitutes an immediate Event of Default. Turkish banks accelerating local bilateral facilities ($163.2M) instantly breaches this threshold, granting Eurobond trustees or 25% of noteholders the statutory right to accelerate the entire $1,100M note principal.

Trigger 3: SPK Arm's-Length Advance Violation & Going-Concern Audit Qualification SPK II-17.1 REGULATION

The $756.8M advance to Zorlu Holding breaches Capital Markets Board rules on related-party transfers. In a Holding moratorium, independent auditors must take a 100% ECL impairment and attach a "Going Concern Material Uncertainty" qualification, which breaches loan covenants across domestic bank facilities and triggers unilateral loan cancellations.

Trigger 4: Working Capital Freeze & Essential Vendor License Revocation OPERATIONAL CRISIS

Operating with a -$126.0M working capital deficit, freezing credit lines prevents ZOREN from paying BOTAŞ for natural gas ($207.4M payables) and TEİAŞ for grid transmission. Non-payment to BOTAŞ shuts down gas cogen plants within 14 days, while default on TEİAŞ triggers EPDK regulatory revocation of generation licenses.

Q7 How do Local TL Corporate Bonds ($228M ÖST) and bank equity kickers interact in a consensual restructuring? LOCAL MARKET WORKOUT
▼

In addition to bank debt, Zorlu Enerji has approximately $228.0M (TRY 8.7B) in domestic corporate bonds (*Özel Sektör Tahvili* - ÖST) held by Turkish mutual funds, private pension funds (BES), and domestic insurance companies. These bonds carry astronomical coupon rates (35%–45% in nominal TRY) and short 3–12 month maturities.

In a comprehensive consensual restructuring, domestic bondholders cannot be left unaddressed:

  • The Equity Kicker Package (ÖST Rollover): Rather than suffering principal haircuts, domestic funds receive an Equity Kicker (10%–15% warrant coverage or newly issued shares representing ~5%–7% of ZOREN) in exchange for rolling short-dated paper into a 3-year standardized maturity note (due 2029) with an indexed coupon capped at BIST TLREF + 300 bps.
  • Bank & ÖST Syndicate Alignment: Turkish banks and domestic bondholders form a unified local creditor class (*Yerli Alacaklılar*). Combined, banks ($428M) and local bondholders ($228M) total $656M in claims. Offering a pooled 30%–33% equity stake across both groups completely aligns domestic creditors against demanding hostile liquidation.
  • Protection for Foreign Eurobonds: By satisfying domestic bondholders with equity kickers, ZOREN avoids immediate local cash insolvency, halts the TRY 3.5B annual cash interest bleed on ÖST paper, and ensures that Eurobond holders retain a clean, unimpaired claim on hard-currency YEKDEM cash flows.
