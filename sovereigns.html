Sovereign dashboard

Comparable key data for the covered sovereigns. Every value is sourced and dated in database/sovereigns/<country>.json; derived ratios are computed. Colours: green good, amber middle, red weak, by the thresholds in the pipeline's indicator table. Under each value is its period or as-of date; hover for the source. Click a country for its history and links. Workbooks: models/sovereigns/.
