# Knowledge base

Durable reference material for an EM debt fund manager active across hard-currency sovereigns, local-currency rates and FX, quasi-sovereigns, corporates and EM macro. Written for two readers: Reza, and any AI running the research pipeline. Views are not kept here; they live in `reports/`. Facts here carry a date and a source, or they are frameworks (method, not data).

## Layout

| Folder | Holds | Maintained by |
|---|---|---|
| `frameworks/` | How to analyse: sovereign credit, local-currency rates and FX, corporate credit, restructuring, relative value, macro regimes, index mechanics, crisis history, glossary | Hand-written; revised when a run proves a method wrong |
| `sovereigns/` | One page per sovereign (34 HY CEEMEA plus the 14 other GBI-EM constituents): snapshot template, structural facts, data sources, and a **dated facts log** that every pipeline run appends to from its "Knowledge base updates" section | Pipeline appends; humans or AIs edit the structural sections |
| `issuers/` | Same for corporates and quasi-sovereigns that merit a page beyond `database/issuers/*.json` | Same |
| `themes/macro-monitor.md` | One dated table per region with the latest official numbers for all 48 sovereigns (inflation, policy and real rates, reserves and drains, current account, REER z-score, terms of trade, debt, fiscal, growth, bank NPLs and capital); generated by `pipeline/macro_monitor.py` | Weekly data refresh; never hand-edited |
| Data layer (`database/sovereigns/*.json`, shown on `sovereigns.html`) | Per sovereign: sourced point indicators, IMF WEO and World Bank annual history (17 series), monthly BIS policy rate / FX / REER and IMF CPI, policy rate, reserves, REER, commodity terms of trade, quarterly IMF bank soundness, derived signals (ex-ante real rate, REER z-score, reserve and terms-of-trade momentum, pb*) | Pipeline runs and the weekly `SovereignDataRefresh` task; never hand-typed |
| `themes/` | Cross-cutting topics: oil, Fed cycle, China, Common Framework, EM flows | Same |
| `sources/` | Where official data lives, by country and by institution; refresh cadences | Hand-written; verify a URL when it fails |

`knowledge/index.json` is rebuilt by every run and drives `knowledge.html` on the site.

## How the pipeline uses this

- The brief for a run includes the matching `sovereigns/` or `issuers/` page and the sovereign framework when the topic is a sovereign. Models treat this content as tier 2 unless a line cites a tier 1 source, and must check dates.
- The adjudicated document's "Knowledge base updates" section (sourced facts only) is appended to the matching page under "Dated facts log" with the run id, so the base compounds without anyone re-reading old reports.
- Open items in reports are the questions; the knowledge base never holds unresolved questions.

## Conventions

- Dates dd-mmm-yy. Currency and units exactly as the source states them. Fiscal-year convention stated per country.
- A fact without a source and date is not a fact; delete it or source it.
- Ratings are written agency by agency, with the date of the last action, never as a single blended letter.
- Country page file names are the slug of the common English short name: `egypt.md`, `cote-divoire.md`, `south-africa.md`.

## Frameworks (method)
- `frameworks/sovereign-credit.md`: hard-currency sovereign analysis, section by section, with the house ratios.
- `frameworks/local-currency-rates-fx.md`: ex-ante real rates, reaction functions, ToT/REER, curve, execution.
- `frameworks/em-corporate-credit.md`: sovereign ceiling, cash EBITDA and FCF identity, capital structure, quasi-sovereigns.
- `frameworks/em-bank-credit.md`: sovereign and system, franchise and funding, asset quality, capital, the instrument's rank, resolution precedent.
- `frameworks/restructuring.md`: process, legal terms, recovery arithmetic, precedents.
- `frameworks/relative-value.md`: what to compare and how to read it.
- `frameworks/macro-regimes.md`: the five global drivers and the regime table.
- `frameworks/index-mechanics.md`: EMBI, GBI-EM, CEMBI rules and flow effects.
- `frameworks/crisis-history.md`: episodes and the lesson each one still teaches.
- `frameworks/glossary.md`: terms and conventions.

## Themes
- `themes/calendar.md`: rolling catalysts. `themes/official-sector-and-common-framework.md`: IMF, Paris Club, Common Framework, China, Gulf anchors. `themes/commodities-and-em.md`: exposure map. `themes/data-and-tools.md`: what the house has and where.

## Reading order for a new analyst or model

1. `frameworks/sovereign-credit.md`, then `frameworks/local-currency-rates-fx.md`, then `frameworks/restructuring.md`.
2. `sovereigns/_universe.md` for the coverage list and where each credit sits.
3. The country page for the credit at hand, then its latest report in `reports/`.
