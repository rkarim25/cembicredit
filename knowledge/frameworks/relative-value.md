# Relative value toolkit

How the house compares credits and instruments. Levels always come from Reza via [BBG]; the toolkit says what to compare and how to read it.

## 1. Sovereign hard currency

- Spread versus rating bucket: z-spread or OAS against the EMBI GD sub-index for the same rating (BB, B, CCC). A credit trading more than one bucket cheap is either mispriced or about to be downgraded; decide which.
- Spread versus own history: 1-year and 5-year percentile of the z-spread. Combine with the direction of fundamentals from the knowledge page.
- Curve: slope 5s10s and 10s30s in spread; inverted curves price near-term default and are read through cash price, not yield.
- Cash price and recovery: below about 70, think recovery and timeline, not yield. Compare price with the restructuring framework's recovery estimate.
- Supply: issuance calendar, buybacks, liability management; a sovereign that has just issued is usually cheap for a month.
- Peer sets the house uses: Gulf (Oman, Bahrain), North Africa (Egypt, Morocco, Tunisia), SSA oil (Nigeria, Angola, Gabon), SSA non-oil (Kenya, Ghana, Côte d'Ivoire, Senegal, Benin, Rwanda), Balkans (Serbia, North Macedonia, Albania, Montenegro), CIS (Uzbekistan, Azerbaijan, Georgia, Armenia), Turkey and South Africa on their own.

## 2. Sovereign local currency

- Ex-ante real rate ranking, ToT/REER quadrant, non-resident share trend, and FX-implied carry versus forward (see `local-currency-rates-fx.md`).
- Rates versus FX: when real rates are high and the currency is cheap, receive and stay unhedged; when real rates are high and the currency is rich, receive hedged.

## 3. Corporates

- Spread to sovereign: the normal range for a well-run exporter is inside the sovereign on a spread basis when the sovereign is HY; a corporate wide of its sovereign by more than its standalone merits is either a liquidity or a governance story.
- Peers by sector and rating in CEMBI; the screener's net leverage and spread filters.
- Yield to worst versus yield to maturity; call schedules and make-whole terms.
- Price versus recovery for distressed names using the calculators.

## 4. Cross-asset

- EM sovereign HY spread versus US HY and Xover: the Transatlantic basis the CDX desk tracks. EM HY wide of US HY by more than its historical range with stable fundamentals is the asset-allocation signal.
- CDS versus bond basis for sovereigns with liquid CDS (Turkey, South Africa, Egypt, Brazil, Mexico, Colombia, Indonesia).

## 5. Output standard

An RV call names the comparator, the measure (z-spread, OAS, yield, price), the current level and percentile (from [BBG]), the fundamental reason the gap should close or widen, and the catalyst with a date.
