# Glossary and conventions

Terms used across the knowledge base, reports and models. House conventions where a term has more than one meaning.

- **Basic balance**: current account plus net FDI. The part of the balance of payments that does not reverse quickly.
- **CAC (collective action clause)**: bond term letting a qualified majority bind all holders to a restructuring. Single-limb aggregated (one vote across series), two-limb (aggregate plus per-series), series-by-series.
- **Comparability of treatment**: Paris Club and Common Framework principle that private creditors give relief at least comparable in PV terms to official creditors.
- **Common Framework**: G20 process (2020) for debt treatments of DSSI-eligible countries, bringing China alongside the Paris Club.
- **DSA**: IMF debt sustainability analysis. LIC-DSF for low-income countries (risk of debt distress: low, moderate, high, in distress); MAC-DSA for market-access countries.
- **DV01**: dollar value of one basis point; house standard trade size 10,000 USD DV01.
- **EFF / ECF / SBA / RSF / PCI**: IMF Extended Fund Facility (medium-term, conditional), Extended Credit Facility (concessional, low-income), Stand-By Arrangement (short-term), Resilience and Sustainability Facility (climate, long-term), Policy Coordination Instrument (no money, signalling).
- **Ex-ante real policy rate**: policy rate minus 12-month-ahead expected inflation. The house measure; ex-post uses current inflation and is only a fallback.
- **Exit yield**: the yield at which new bonds trade after a restructuring; recovery value is the PV of new instruments at this yield.
- **GEFR**: gross external financing requirement = current account deficit + external amortisation due within 12 months.
- **GFN**: gross financing need = fiscal deficit + domestic and external amortisation.
- **Interest to revenue**: central government interest payments over revenue; the house's primary fiscal stress ratio.
- **LIA / LIOA**: IMF lending into arrears (private creditors) / lending into official arrears policies; they let a programme proceed during a default when the debtor negotiates in good faith.
- **Net reserves**: gross reserves minus swaps, forwards, IMF credit and other encumbrances; the house always states which definition is used.
- **PDI**: past-due interest, treated in restructurings by capitalisation, partial payment or write-off.
- **Primary balance**: fiscal balance before interest. pb* is the debt-stabilising primary balance.
- **Quasi-sovereign**: majority state-owned or policy entity; analysed as standalone plus support.
- **REER**: real effective exchange rate; valuation stated as a z-score against a ten-year history.
- **Tiers of evidence**: 1 Reza's Bloomberg and management captures; 2 primary sources and Cognitive Credit; 3 sell-side and major press; 4 everything else. See `pipeline/prompts/rules.md`.
- **ToT**: terms of trade, export prices over import prices.
- **Value recovery instrument**: contingent instrument paid on an index (GDP, oil, revenue) after a restructuring.

Conventions: dates dd-mmm-yy; currency as stated by the source; fiscal year stated per country; ratings agency by agency with dates; levels only from Reza via [BBG].
