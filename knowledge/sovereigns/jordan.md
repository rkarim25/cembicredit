## 1. External position
- Central Bank of Jordan gross foreign exchange reserves reached all-time high of $28.5bn at end-September 2026 (+11.7% YTD), providing 9.2 months of import cover (Central Bank of Jordan, Sep-26). [08-Oct-26]
- US 7-year Bilateral Memorandum of Understanding guarantees $1.45bn annually across FY2023–FY2029 ($10.15bn total) in economic and military assistance (US Department of State, Sep-22). [08-Oct-26]
## 2. Fiscal position
- Central government debt stood at 82.1% of GDP in 2024 and reached 83.6% in 2025, targeted to reach 80.0% of GDP by 2028 under IMF EFF program (IMF Country Report, Jun-26). [08-Oct-26]
- Central government primary deficit recorded 2.8% of GDP in 2024 with headline fiscal deficit at 5.2% of GDP (Ministry of Finance, Dec-25). [08-Oct-26]
## 3. Monetary and financial
- Currency peg maintained at fixed rate of 0.7090 JOD per USD (1 JOD = 1.4104 USD) with CBJ policy discount rate at 6.00% (Central Bank of Jordan, Sep-26). [08-Oct-26]
