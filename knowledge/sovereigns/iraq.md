## 1. External position

## 2. Fiscal position
- Domestic public debt reached IQD 109.5trn ($83.8bn) at end-July 2026, up from IQD 90.5trn at end-2025, held predominantly by state banks Rafidain and Rasheed. (Central Bank of Iraq, 31-Jul-26) [08-Oct-26]
- Fiscal breakeven oil price estimated above $90/bbl due to public sector wage and pension bill consuming over 75% of government expenditures. (IMF, 20-May-25) [08-Oct-26]
## 3. Monetary and financial
- Official exchange rate devalued by 15.2% on 07-Oct-26 from 1,320 to 1,520 IQD per USD (CBI buying at 1,500, selling to banks at 1,510, public selling at 1,520). (Central Bank of Iraq, 07-Oct-26) [08-Oct-26]
## 4. Growth and structure
- Real GDP projected to contract by 6.8% in 2026 under OPEC+ crude production quotas (~3.9 mbpd) and pipeline closures. (IMF, 20-May-25) [08-Oct-26]
