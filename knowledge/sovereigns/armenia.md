## 2. Fiscal position
- Total public debt 5.12trn AMD (~USD 13.9bn), below 50% of GDP (Ministry of Finance, 30-Jun-26) [08-Oct-26]
- 2026 state budget deficit projected at 3.4% of GDP (Ministry of Finance, Oct-26) [08-Oct-26]
- 2027 draft budget projects revenues 3.8trn AMD, expenditures 4.2trn AMD, deficit 468.2bn AMD (Ministry of Finance, Oct-26) [08-Oct-26]
- Secured €250m bilateral credit facility from French Development Agency (Ministry of Finance, Oct-26) [08-Oct-26]
## 3. Monetary and financial
- Refinancing rate raised 25 bps to 6.75% (CBA, 15-Sep-26) [08-Oct-26]
- Headline CPI inflation 4.3% YoY, core inflation 4.8% YoY (Armstat, Sep-26) [08-Oct-26]
## 4. Growth and structure
