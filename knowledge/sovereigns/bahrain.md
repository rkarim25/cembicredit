## 1. External position
- Gross official FX reserves cover slightly over two months of prospective non-hydrocarbon imports (IMF Article IV, 23-Jan-26). [08-Oct-26]
- Bilateral $10.0bn GCC Financial Support Package (Saudi Arabia, UAE, Kuwait) concluded initial disbursement envelope (IMF / Ministry of Finance, Jan-26). [08-Oct-26]
## 2. Fiscal position
- Gross government debt reached 134% of GDP at end-2024 and remains above 133% across 2025–2026 (IMF Article IV, 23-Jan-26). [08-Oct-26]
- General government fiscal deficit recorded 11.0% of GDP in 2024 and estimated at 10.8%–11.0% in 2025 (IMF Article IV, 23-Jan-26). [08-Oct-26]
- Sovereign fiscal breakeven oil price estimated at ~$90–$100/bbl (Rating Agencies / IMF, Jan-26). [08-Oct-26]
## 3. Monetary and financial
- Central Bank of Bahrain government overdraft facility exceeded 25% of GDP in 2024 (down 8% by Oct-25) (IMF Article IV, 23-Jan-26). [08-Oct-26]
- Currency peg maintained at fixed rate of 0.3760 BHD per USD since 1980 (Central Bank of Bahrain, cbb.gov.bh, Sep-26). [08-Oct-26]
