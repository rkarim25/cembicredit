## 1. External position
- Central Bank of Oman gross foreign assets and gold stood at OMR 7.068bn ($18.38bn) at end-2024, providing ~5 months of import cover (Central Bank of Oman, Dec-24). [08-Oct-26]
- Oman Investment Authority (OIA) total assets stood at OMR 23bn ($59.7bn) in 2025 (Oman Investment Authority, Jun-25). [08-Oct-26]
- Current account registered surpluses of ~2.5%–3.3% of GDP across 2024–2025 (Central Bank of Oman / IMF, Jun-26). [08-Oct-26]
## 2. Fiscal position
- Public debt reduced to OMR 14.6bn ($37.9bn, 35.0% of GDP) at end-2024 and ~34.7% of GDP at end-2025 from 68% in 2020 (Ministry of Finance, Dec-25). [08-Oct-26]
- General government recorded consecutive fiscal surpluses of 3.3% of GDP in 2024 and ~0.6%–0.7% of GDP in 2025 (Ministry of Finance, Dec-25). [08-Oct-26]
- Fiscal breakeven oil price reduced structurally to ~$65–$70/bbl (Ministry of Finance / IMF, Jun-26). [08-Oct-26]
- Royal Decree No. 56/2025 enacted 5% Personal Income Tax on net taxable income above OMR 42,000 effective 01-Jan-28 (Official Gazette, Jul-25). [08-Oct-26]
## 3. Monetary and financial
- Currency peg maintained at fixed rate of OMR 0.3845 per USD since 1986 (Central Bank of Oman, Sep-26). [08-Oct-26]
- CBO repo rate maintained in alignment with US Federal Reserve policy rate corridor (Central Bank of Oman, Sep-26). [08-Oct-26]
## 4. Growth and structure
- Real GDP grew 2.1% in 2024 with non-hydrocarbon growth expanding at ~3.5% (National Centre for Statistics and Information, Jun-25). [08-Oct-26]
