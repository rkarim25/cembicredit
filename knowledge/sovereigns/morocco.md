## 1. External position
- Bank Al-Maghrib official foreign exchange reserves projected at MAD 502.8bn (~$55.65bn) by end-2026 and MAD 515.3bn by end-2027, covering 5.5 months of imports (Bank Al-Maghrib, Sep-26). [08-Oct-26]
## 2. Fiscal position
- Central government budget deficit narrowed to 3.5% of GDP in 2025 (matching Finance Law target) and budgeted at 3.0% of GDP in 2026 (Ministry of Economy and Finance, Sep-26). [08-Oct-26]
- Treasury debt-to-GDP stood at 67.7% in 2024, declined to 67.2% in 2025, and projected at 66.5% in 2026 (Ministry of Economy and Finance, Sep-26). [08-Oct-26]
## 3. Monetary and financial
- Bank Al-Maghrib key policy rate held at 2.25% with 2026 headline inflation projected at 0.7% and 2027 inflation at 1.5% (Bank Al-Maghrib, Sep-26). [08-Oct-26]
- Moroccan Dirham (MAD) pegged to currency basket of 60% EUR and 40% USD within a +/-5% fluctuation band (Bank Al-Maghrib, Sep-26). [08-Oct-26]
