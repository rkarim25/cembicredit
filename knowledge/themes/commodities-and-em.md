# Commodities and EM credit: exposure map

Which commodity moves which credit, and in which direction. Sensitivities are directional; the sizes belong in each country page with a source (export share, fiscal revenue share, breakeven oil price).

## Oil
- Exporters (benefit from higher prices): Saudi Arabia, UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman, Bahrain, Iraq, Azerbaijan, Kazakhstan, Nigeria, Angola, Gabon, Cameroon (partly), Ghana (partly), Algeria, Colombia, Ecuador, Mexico (fiscal), Brazil (Petrobras), Russia. Fiscal breakeven oil prices are the key metric: Bahrain and Oman high, Iraq high, Gulf core lower.
- Importers (hurt by higher prices): Egypt, Pakistan, Turkey, Jordan, Morocco, Kenya, India, Sri Lanka, Lebanon, most of Central and Eastern Europe, Ukraine. For these, oil feeds the current account, subsidies and inflation together.
- Gas: Egypt flips between exporter and importer depending on Zohr output and LNG terms; Mozambique and Senegal are future exporters; Qatar is the structural exporter.

## Metals
- Copper: Zambia, Chile, Peru, DRC. Zambia's debt capacity and its value recovery instrument hinge on it.
- Gold: Ghana, Uzbekistan, Mali, Burkina Faso, Russia (reserves). Gold prices support reserves for central banks that buy domestically (Ghana's programme, Uzbekistan).
- Iron ore and bauxite: Brazil, Guinea, Ukraine (steel, Metinvest, DTEK context).
- Platinum group metals and coal: South Africa (terms of trade and the rand).

## Agriculture
- Soy, corn, beef: Brazil, Argentina, Paraguay. Wheat and sunflower: Ukraine (Aragvi/Trans-Oil's Black Sea terminals), Russia, Kazakhstan. Cocoa: Côte d'Ivoire, Ghana (farm-gate price policy is fiscal policy). Coffee: Ethiopia, Colombia. Food importers in North Africa and the Gulf carry subsidy exposure to wheat.

## Fertiliser and phosphates
- Morocco (OCP), Egypt, Nigeria (Dangote fertiliser). Gas prices drive urea; phosphate rock drives OCP.

## How to use this page
For every sovereign and corporate page, record the two or three commodity sensitivities with the figure that matters (export share, revenue share, breakeven) and its source. In a macro note, state the commodity regime and then list the credits on each side of it.
