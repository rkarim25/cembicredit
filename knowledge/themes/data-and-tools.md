# Data and tools: what the house has and where it lives

## In the repo
- `database/issuers/*.json`: issuer records (financials, debt, covenants, views) and the site's screener data via `js/issuers_data.js`.
- `database/cognitive_credit_models/`: parsed Cognitive Credit workbooks with formulas, raw exports under `raw/`.
- `models/*.xlsx`: live-formula credit models; `CEMBI_Master_Comp_Sheet.xlsx`.
- `gbi_em_data.json`, `credit_data.json`, `ust_curve_data.json`, `macro_trade_tracker.json`: the desks' data, regenerated by their scripts.
- `reports/`: one truth document per topic. `runs/`: audit trail per run.
- `knowledge/`: this base. `notion_export/`: nightly Markdown backup of the Notion Work workspace.

## External data (free, official)
- IMF: WEO database (DataMapper API, annual), the data portal https://data.imf.org (SDMX 3.0 API: CPI, interest rates, reserves, effective exchange rates, commodity terms of trade, financial soundness indicators, BOP, quarterly GFS; replaced IFS and the old SDMX 2.1 service in 2025), Article IV staff reports and DSAs, programme documents, COFER. The pipeline pulls the monthly and quarterly series into the sovereign dashboard every Monday.
- World Bank: WDI, International Debt Statistics (creditor composition of external debt), Debt Reporting Heat Map. https://data.worldbank.org
- BIS: locational banking statistics, debt securities, policy rates, exchange rates, effective exchange rates (REER). https://data.bis.org. The pipeline pulls WS_CBPOL, WS_XRU and WS_EER monthly into the sovereign dashboard; REER covers 64 economies only. Gap: no REER for most frontier sovereigns (candidate source: Bruegel's REER database, 178 countries, monthly, downloadable CSV).
- Central banks and finance ministries: per-country list in `sources/sovereign-sources.md`.
- Exchanges and filings: LSE RNS, Oslo Børs NewsWeb, SEC EDGAR, Euronext Dublin, local exchanges; company IR sites.
- Trade and commodities: EIA, OPEC, FAO, Comtrade.
- Ratings: agency sites (press releases are free; full reports need a login).

## Paid or desk-only (via Reza)
- Bloomberg: prices, yields, spreads, curves, WIRP, DDIS, CDS, FX forwards, surveys. Requested through [BBG] items in reports.
- Cognitive Credit: corporate models; downloaded from a logged-in browser.
- Askb and broker research: pasted into Keep with the `#askb` or `#email` tag.

## Refresh cadence
- Daily: UST, CDX, news (existing nightly jobs). Weekly: reserves (most central banks publish weekly or monthly). Monthly: CPI, trade, fiscal. Quarterly: BoP, debt statistics, GDP. Annual: Article IV, budgets, audited accounts.
