# Catalyst calendar (rolling)

Maintained by hand and by runs. Add an entry with its source; remove it after the event, moving the outcome to the country page's dated facts log. Dates to be verified by the run that cites them.

## Standing cycles
- IMF Spring Meetings: April. IMF and World Bank Annual Meetings: October. Programme approvals and restructuring announcements cluster around both.
- JP Morgan index rebalances: last business day of each month; inclusion and exclusion announcements precede by weeks.
- Rating agency sovereign review calendars: published each December for EU-regulated agencies; use them for the European and some EM names.
- OPEC+ meetings: roughly every two months; matters for Gulf, Nigeria, Angola, Azerbaijan, Gabon.
- FOMC: eight meetings a year; the UST desk tracks the curve regime.
