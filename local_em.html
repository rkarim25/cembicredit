Local EM desk: GBI-EM constituents

Official data only, each value with its period: policy rate (BIS, or the IMF monetary policy-related rate where BIS has none), CPI y/y (IMF data portal), ex-ante real policy rate (policy rate less 12-month expected inflation where a run supplied it, else less latest CPI), BIS or IMF real effective exchange rate z-score against the trailing ten years, local currency per USD (BIS monthly average) with its 12-month change, gross reserves (IMF) with 12-month change, current account (IMF BOP, last four quarters). Local bond yields, swap levels and FX forwards are market data and come from Reza via [BBG]; the earlier version of this page carried hand-typed levels and was retired on 09-Oct-26. CEEMEA constituents are listed first. Method: local-currency rates and FX framework; full history and charts per country: sovereign dashboard.
